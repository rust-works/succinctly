- **jq: a write on a large document builds its value in one pass** (#6125). Materializing a container of
  plain RFC 8259 JSON (the first step of an assignment, `del`, `|=`, `tojson` and every other stage that
  needs an owned value) now reads the container's own text directly, not through a rank and a select per node;
  a leniency, a repeated key, a lone surrogate, invalid UTF-8 or nesting past 64 levels still takes the walk, and
  yq mode and a node a binding shares are untouched. On a 10 MB `users` document, `.users[0].name = "x" | .users | length`
  goes from 0.76x to 1.05x of jq 1.7.1 on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X and from 0.82x to 1.34x on an Apple M4 Pro,
  and `del(.users[0]) | .users | length` from 0.81x to 1.11x and from 0.88x to 1.44x. Peak memory is unchanged
  (788 MB at 100 MB).
