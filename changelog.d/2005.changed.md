- **yq: `any(cond)`, `all(cond)`, `any(gen; cond)` and `all(gen; cond)` are gated behind `--jq-extensions`** (#2005).
  Real yq's `any` and `all` take no argument: every predicate spelling is `bad expression, please check expression syntax` there (v4.53.3), while `succinctly yq`
  accepted all four with no flag, so `5 | any(. > 3)` leaked jq's `Cannot iterate over number (5)` and `{"a":1} | all(. > 0)` printed `true` where the bare `all` is
  refused. They now fail at parse time with `"any(f)" is not part of yq's syntax; pass --jq-extensions ...`, like `min_by`, and under the flag are jq's own surface.
  Bare `any`/`all` and a user-defined `any`/`all` are unchanged. `succinctly jq` is unaffected.
  Pinned by `test_yq_any_all_predicate_forms_gated_2005`.
