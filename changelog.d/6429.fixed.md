- **jq: the size cap a counted repeat over a looping body meets is measured and pinned** (#6429). `"ba" | [match("((?:(b|)*|a)+){4,8}";"g") | .string]` is `["b","",""]` in jq and `["ba",""]` here, because the copies the rewrite writes double with each count of the upper bound (`{3,7}` 11 KB, `{4,8}` 22 KB, `{5,9}` 45 KB alone, and more once the capture rewrite has added its groups) and pass the 32 KiB cap at an upper bound of 7. No smaller spelling exists in the `regex` crate's syntax, and a cap three times larger admits `{4,8}` but not `{5,9}`, so the cap stays; `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md` carries the sizes and a unit test and a CLI row pin the fallback.
