- **jq: `-R` over a pipe holds one 256 KiB window instead of a 1 MiB probe** (#7412).
  A pipe's length is not known, so `-R` read its first four windows (1 MiB) to decide whether to read
  it a window at a time, and held them as its first window: `cat log | jq -R ...` held 1.68 MiB of
  anonymous memory on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X at every size from 1 MB to 100 MB, where the same lines read
  from a file held 0.91 and jq 1.7.1 holds 0.84. The probe is now one window, so a pipe holds what a file
  does. Peak RSS of `-R length`, `-R 'fromjson|.id'` and `-nR 'reduce inputs ...'` over a pipe falls by
  0.7-0.8 MiB at 1, 10 and 100 MB on the 7950X (7.2-7.5 to 6.4-6.7 MiB) and on an Apple M4 Pro (4.2-4.7
  to 3.5-4.0 MiB). Times are unchanged within an A/A control on both machines, and 2-8% faster at 1 MB.
