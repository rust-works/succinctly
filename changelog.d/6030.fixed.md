- **`succinctly jq` `strftime` prints `%y` and `%C` of a negative year as jq's libc does** (#6030). `%y` is the absolute value of the year's remainder by 100 in two digits, and `%C` of a negative year of fewer than three digits is `-0`: year -5 formats `%y` as `05` (it was `-5`), `%D` and `%x` as `01/01/05` (`01/01/-5`), and `%C` as `-0` (`00`). Checked against `/usr/bin/jq` over 25,920 year, format and zone rows.
