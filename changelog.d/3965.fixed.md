- **jq: `$x` after a `catch` handler no longer refuses when the `try` body always raises after navigating** (#3965, a #3891 residual). `path(. as $x | try error(.a) catch . | $x)` raised `Invalid path expression` where jq answers `[]`; so did `error(.c)`, `.a | error` and the same bodies with another handler. A body whose last stage is `error` emits nothing and its raise backtracks to the `try`'s fork point, so what the message or earlier stages navigated is undone. A body that emits before it raises still moves the register and is refused, as in jq. A handler that navigates under `//` (`catch (.a)? // 5`) is still refused where jq answers.
