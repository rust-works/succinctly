- **yq: a tab right after a quoted scalar or an alias in block context is read as separation** (#6937). `a: "b"⇥`, `"a"⇥`, `- 'x'⇥`, `a: &x "b"⇥` and `a: *x⇥` were a parse error ("tab character used for indentation"), where yq reads them: the scanners for a quoted scalar and an alias stop just past their closing delimiter, so the main loop was entered mid-line and took the tab at the cursor for indentation, or for a line of its own. A tab that starts a line, and content after the tab (`a: "b"⇥c`), are still refused, as in yq. Found by a mutation fuzz of the yaml-test-suite inputs against yq v4.53.3.
