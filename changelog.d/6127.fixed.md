- **jq: a repeated group that Oniguruma reads as an assertion is refused** (#6127). `"ab" | test("(?:a|\\b|b)*")`, `(?:\\b)+`, `(?:a|$)?` and `(?=a)*` answered `true`; jq refuses them with `target of repeat operator is invalid` (here with the `invalid regex:` prefix), as it already did for `\\b*` (#5535). The rule jq applies (an assertion is an invalid target, a sequence of several nodes is not, an alternation is when any alternative is, a plain `(?:...)` group is its body) was captured over 75,780 generated groups and the scan agrees on all of them.
