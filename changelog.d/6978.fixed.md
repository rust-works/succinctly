- **yq: an empty block scalar after a key, `- ` or flow key on a `---` line no longer swallows the next entries** (#6978). In `--- x: >` followed by `b: 1` (also `--- x: |`, `--- &a x: >`, `--- - >` and `--- [a]: >`) the scalar read `"b: 1\n"` and `b` was also read as an entry of its own, where yq reads the scalar as empty (`{"x":"","b":1}`). The reader took every header on a marker line for `--- >`, where the scalar is the document's own node and may sit at column 0; that holds only when nothing but node properties (`!!str`, `&a`) sit between the marker and the header. Found while probing the shapes around #6976.
