- **`succinctly jq` runs a tree recursion that pipes one call of itself into the next** (#7460). `def f($n): if $n == 0 then . + 1 else f($n - 1) | f($n - 1) end; 0 | f(13)` is `8192` in jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2, where `succinctly jq` raised `f/1 exceeded maximum recursion depth` after about 8,000 calls: the rest of the pipe ran inside the first call's output sink, so every call ever made held its native frames until the outermost returned. A pipe that starts with a call to a `def` whose arguments and body answer at most once now runs the call to its value first, so the stack holds the recursion's depth, not its calls (`f(16)` and a 10,000-node depth-first walk that counts through `.c += 1` now answer); a recursion that is genuinely deep is still refused with the same resource limit.
