- **yq: `recurse(.[]?; cond)` stops at an alias and gives `key`, `path`, `parent`, `line` and `column` a position** (#4095). The owned walk it replaced expanded every alias (a 29-node document counted 51) and read no position, so `[recurse(.[]?; true) | path]` was a list of `[]`. It now answers what `..` does for `cond` = `true`, node for node, and what `path(recurse(.[]?; cond))` does for a pruning `cond`. A write through it, and a `cond` the walk cannot ask at a position (one that reads `key`, `path` or `tag`), are unchanged ([#4223](https://github.com/rust-works/succinctly/issues/4223)).
