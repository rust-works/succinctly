- **yq: a verbatim tag followed directly by a non-space is refused, as yq does** (#6298). `a: !<tag:x>x`, `[!<tag:x>x]`, `!<tag:x>"q"`, `a: !<tag:x>#c` and `!<tag:x>[a]` read the text after the closing `>` as the tagged value (`{"a":"x"}`, `["x"]`, `"q"`, ...) where yq's scanner needs whitespace, a line break or the end of the input there and stops with `did not find expected whitespace or line break`. A verbatim tag followed by a space, a tab, a line break or the end of the input is unchanged. Nine refused and nine unchanged documents are pinned against yq v4.53.3's answers.
