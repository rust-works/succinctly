- **yq: `ireduce` reads its whole source before INIT** (#8022). yq's `reduceOperator` evaluates `SRC` in full before INIT, so an error anywhere in the source wins: `error("s") as $x ireduce (error("i"); .)` is `Error: s` in yq and was `Error: i` here, and `error("s") as $x ireduce (select(false); .)` and `(1, error("s")) as $x ireduce (0; select(false))` were silent successes here, since an empty INIT never read the source and an UPDATE that dropped every accumulator stopped reading it. `succinctly yq` now answers as yq does, with and without `--eval-all` and `-n` and on the right side of a write; the source is still streamed, with INIT's and UPDATE's failures held until the source has been read to its end. jq's `reduce` still evaluates INIT first.
