- **jq: a repeat operator after a comment with nothing before it is refused under the `i` and `x` flags too** (#6261). `"aab" | match("(?#c)*"; "gi")` answered four empty matches where jq 1.7.1 refuses it (`target of repeat operator is not specified`), as it does without a flag: the `(?i)` put before the pattern made the `regex` crate read the operator as repeating the comment. The refusal is now made on the pattern as written, for a comment at the start of a group or after a `|`.
