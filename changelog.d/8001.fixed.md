- **yq: a second `;` in `ireduce` makes its UPDATE a block that yields nothing** (#8001). yq's `;` is a block operator of `,`'s precedence that associates to the right, so `.[] as $x ireduce (0; . + $x; .)` is `0; (. + $x; .)` there, and a block evaluated as a value yields nothing without evaluating either side: yq prints nothing and exits 0, where `succinctly yq` refused the filter with `expected ')', found ';'`. It now folds as yq does: the fold prints nothing (and prints INIT over an empty source), an `error` after the second `;` never fires, and a union in front of the block keeps the operands before the one the `;` follows (`ireduce (0; . + $x, . + 1; 9)` over `[1,2]` is `3`). jq's `reduce` still takes exactly two parts.
