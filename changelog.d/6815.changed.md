- **library: the payload types of `jq::eval_generic::CommentTree` changed** (#6815).
  `Leaf` and `Array` hold a `MetaBox` in place of the `NodeMeta`, and `Object` holds a `MetaBox`, a
  boxed field map and a `KeyMap` in place of the `NodeMeta` and two `IndexMap`s, so a node is 32 bytes
  instead of 224. Build a node with `CommentTree::leaf`, `array` or `object` (they take the old
  arguments), replace a node's metadata with `set_meta`, and read or write it through `meta()` and
  `meta_mut()` as before; a `MetaBox` and a field map read through `Deref`, and `KeyMap` has `get`,
  `get_mut`, `entry_or_default`, `iter`, `values`, `len` and `is_empty`. `meta_mut()` returns a guard
  that derefs to `NodeMeta`. The `succinctly` command-line output is unchanged.
