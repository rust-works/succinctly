- **yq, jq: a computed result is printed as JSON into one buffer instead of keeping a `String` per node alive** (#6814).
  `format_json` (the printer behind `succinctly yq -o json` for any result the cursor streamers do not
  print, and behind `succinctly jq`'s `-S`, `-a`, `-C` and `-s`) built a `String` for every value, a
  `Vec<String>` of them for every array and object, and a `join` per level, which was 42-51% of the live
  heap at the peak of a whole-document write. Arrays and objects now append straight into one buffer (a scalar or key still
  builds its own short-lived text); the output is byte-identical. A 100 MB `.zzz = 1` with `-o json` peaks at 1059 MB instead of 1260 MB
  (`-I0`) and 1454 MB (indented) on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X, and at 1174 MB instead of 1505 MB and 1730 MB
  on an Apple M4 Pro, and finishes 10-45% sooner; across the `users`, `navigation`, `comprehensive` and
  `nested` patterns peak RSS falls 9-47% and time 10-45% (up to 3-12% and 6-24% for a result that is
  only a part of the document). Indented output now peaks where compact output does.
