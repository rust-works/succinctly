- **yq: `-`, `*`, `/` and `%` with an empty left operand no longer evaluate the right one** (#6982). yq answers nothing there without evaluating the right side, so `.[] * error("x")` on `[]` prints nothing in yq and stopped with `Error: x` here; the same for every `*` merge flag and `.[]? * (.[-1])`. `+`, the comparisons, `and`, `or` and `//` still evaluate the right side, as yq does.
