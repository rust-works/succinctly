- **jq: a NaN with a payload is an invalid numeric literal, as in jq 1.8** (#7522). `NaN1`, `nan123`, `sNaN12` and `-NaN10` were read as NaN (printed `null`) from input, `--stream`, `--seq`, `fromjson`, `tonumber`, `--argjson`, `--jsonargs` and `--slurpfile`, as jq 1.7.1 reads them. jq 1.8.0 dropped them as a security fix (CVE-2024-53427): `Invalid numeric literal`, exit 5 for a document and exit 2 for a named argument. `succinctly jq` now follows jq 1.8.2 on every one of those, with its line and column. `NaN`, `nan`, `-NaN` and a zero payload (`NaN0`) are still numbers, and `succinctly yq` is unchanged. Recorded as a difference from the pinned 1.7.1 in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
