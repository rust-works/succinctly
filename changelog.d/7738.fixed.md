- **jq: `-n input`, `first(inputs)` and `limit` over a stream that is not UTF-8, or holds a parse fault, answer at once** (#7738).
  The lazy route handed such a stream (a pipe, a FIFO, a device, a terminal) to the route that reads it whole, which never ends on a stream that does not (`cat /dev/urandom | jq -n input`), where jq 1.7.1 reports the parse error, or replaces the byte in a string with U+FFFD, as its reader gets there.
  A stream is now substituted a window at a time and a fault reported from what has been read; a regular file keeps the read whole its length bounds.
