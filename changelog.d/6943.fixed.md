- **yq: a literal or constructor after a stage that matched nothing is emitted once** (#6943). yq's `valueOperator` re-emits a copy of its literal when its context holds no node, so `.a[] | 1` over `a: []` is `1`, and so is `select(false) | 1`, `.zz[] | "x"`, `.c.b | {"k": 1}`, `.a[] | 1 + 1` and `.a[] | -1`; `.a[] | [1]` is `[]`, `.a[] | (1, 2)` is `1`, `2`, and `[.a[] | 1]` is `[1]`. `succinctly yq` printed nothing for all of them, having modelled the rule for an operand of `and`/`or`, the right side of `=` and a few single stages only. It is the pipe's whole context list that is empty, not a node: `.d[] | select(false) | 1` is one `1` however many members `d` has, `.d[] | 1 | select(false)` stays empty, and `map(select(false) | 1)` is `[1]` as `[.[] | f]` is. The same holds over `--eval-all`'s list of documents (`select(fi == 9) | 0` is `0`). The pipe's outputs are counted and the stages before a re-emitting one are run a second time only when the whole pipe was empty, so a pipe with outputs keeps the lazy and cursor routes it had; jq mode is unchanged. 48 rows over a four-member document are pinned in `tests/yq_cli/i6943.rs`, all captured from yq v4.53.3; the old build agreed on 13 of them. What still differs (a write's right side, `map_values` and `|=` bodies, a computed source) is in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
