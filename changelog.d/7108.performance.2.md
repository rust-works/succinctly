- **`succinctly yq`** (#7108). A `reduce`/`ireduce` (and the fold `with(PATH; SRC as $v | WRITES)` is) whose UPDATE is `|= . + N` (or `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`) of a number field by a number is answered on the owned tree, as `+=` is, and no longer re-indexes the whole state every step: `.k |= (.x[] as $v ireduce (.; .z |= . + $v))` over 5,000 elements took 3 s and takes 0.01 s. A missing key, a non-number and every other shape stay on the evaluator, with unchanged output.
