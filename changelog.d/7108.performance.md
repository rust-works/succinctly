- **`succinctly yq`** (#7108). A `reduce`/`ireduce` (and the fold `with(PATH; SRC as $v | WRITES)` is) whose UPDATE is a plain `=` of a scalar onto a field of the object it updates, alone, in a pipe or behind `SOURCE as $v`, is answered on the owned tree and no longer re-indexes the whole state at every step: `.k |= (.x[] as $v ireduce (.; .y = $v))` over 10,000 elements took 14 s and takes 0.04 s (3 s to 0.01 s at 5,000), and `with(.k; .x[] as $v | .y += $v)` the same. A glob or `<<` key, a container value and every other shape stay on the evaluator, with unchanged output.
