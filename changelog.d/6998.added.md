- **yq: `from_props`, `from_csv`, `from_tsv`, `from_xml`, `to_xml`, `load_props` and `load_xml`** (#6998). The codec operators of yq v4.53.3, now that the `-p props|csv|tsv|xml` decoders and `-o xml` exist: each is the decoder or encoder applied to a string (or to a file's text, behind `failed to load F: bad file 'F': ...`), under the flags of the run (`--csv-separator`, `--xml-attribute-prefix`, ...). A number, a boolean or `null` is decoded as its text and a collection as the empty string (`EOF` for props and the tables, `null` for XML); `fromxml`-style spellings work; `to_xml` indents by two whatever `-I` says. Every row of `tests/yq_cli/i6998.rs` is yq's. A round trip through a decoder and its encoder keeps the trailing line break yq removes (`limitations.md`).
