- **jq: `$var.field` in a `reduce`/`foreach` update no longer rebuilds the whole object** (#5499). Each loop element was converted to a literal tree, converted back to a value, and only then asked for one field. The field is now spliced in directly when the object holds it. Interleaved A/B on `users` documents of 2, 10 and 70 MB, identical output: `reduce .users[] as $u (0; .+$u.score)` -34% on an M4 Pro and on a 7950X (70 MB: 1.22 s to 0.80 s and 1.39 s to 0.90 s; jq 1.7.1 was 0.74 s on an M5 Max, where this was 0.58x and is now 0.97x), `reduce .users[] as $u ({}; .[$u.name] = $u.score)` -43% to -47%, and a `foreach` update -32% to -35%. A lookup of a field the object lacks is about 1% slower. jq mode only; a longer pipe, an absent field, a non-object, EXTRACT and path-position reads keep their old route and error text.
