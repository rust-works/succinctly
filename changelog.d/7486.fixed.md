- **jq: `TZ` is read as libc reads it** (#7486). `localtime`, `strflocaltime` and `localtime | mktime` looked `TZ` up in jiff's zone database, so `TZ=posix/Asia/Tokyo`, `TZ=right/Europe/Paris` and `TZ=Asia//Tokyo` were UTC (on macOS too for the last), `TZDIR` was not read as glibc reads it, and `TZ=asia/tokyo` loaded Tokyo on Linux. `TZ` is now a tz file under the zoneinfo directory (`$TZDIR` on Linux, `/usr/share/zoneinfo` on macOS, whose libc ignores `TZDIR`) or at a path, before it is a POSIX string, as jq 1.7.1 reads it on glibc and on macOS. Over 49 spellings of `TZ` under six `TZDIR` settings on x86_64 Linux, `localtime`, `localtime | mktime` and `strflocaltime` now give jq's local times for 271 of the 294 pairs (214 before); the rest are `right/` zones, whose offsets now load but whose leap seconds are not counted (recorded in the limitations page), and POSIX rules applied before 1967, unchanged.
