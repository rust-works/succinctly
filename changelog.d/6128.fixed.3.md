- **jq: a repeat operator after a comment repeats the item before the comment, so a loop over one ends at its empty iteration** (#6128). `"bab" | [match("(?:b(?#)*|a)+"; "g") | .string]` printed `["bab",""]`; jq 1.7.1 reads `b(?#)*` as `b*` and answers `["b","","b",""]`. The comment is left out of what the regex engines read when a repeat operator follows it and an item precedes it. Over 68,970 generated cases, 588 are fixed and none broken; a comment right after a quantifier (`b*(?#)*`) keeps its earlier answer.
