- **jq: arithmetic, a comparison and `//` over reads of a bound value are answered from the value** (#7132, part five). `$a | .[0] + .[1]`, `first + 1`, `first > 1`, `first // 0` and `.[$i] + 1` over an array the filter built (`[range(n)] as $a | [range(m) | $a | .[0] + .[1]]`) wrote `$a` out as text and indexed it again on every call: 2.9 to 5.8 ms per call over 100,000 elements on a Ryzen 9 7950X and 2.8 to 5.1 on an M4 Pro, where jq takes 0.04. The operand grammar read arithmetic and `//` only as operands, and had no arm for `first`/`last`/`nth`; such a stage is now answered from the value whenever its answer is one the bridge's copy would print, compare and compute as, 0.018 to 0.021 ms per call on the 7950X and 0.012 to 0.013 on the M4 Pro, in a loop and in a fold alike. Stages that answer nothing from the value are unchanged (eight control programs, four of them per-element arithmetic, `//` and `select`, within 2% on both machines). Output, errors and their text are jq 1.7.1's (390 programs pinned in `tests/jq_cli/i7132_operands.rs`, captured from `/usr/bin/jq`).
