- **jq: a regex with two greedy `X+` repeats and one optional repeat between them, on the second engine, is refused instead of answered wrongly** (#6096). `"a" | test("(?=a)a+a*a+")` was `true` (upstream `fancy-regex` 0.20.0) where jq and the `regex` crate say `false`, and `a+b*a+`, `.+.*.+`, `a+a?a+` and `(?:ab)+c*(?:ab)+` behind a lookahead, a backreference or `\K` did the same. Such a pattern now gives `invalid regex` for any text; the same shape with nothing that needs the second engine, and with `l` or `n`, keeps its normal route and answers as jq does. Two repeats between the plusses, a mandatory item, another atom and bounded repeats are not affected.
