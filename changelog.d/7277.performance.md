- **jq: a `--seq` pipe on standard input holds about 1.6 MiB less** (#7277).
  Its reader read a block onto the 4 MiB its probe read before ending its first window, which grew the
  buffer to 8 MiB and made a window of 5 MiB; the probe is now handed out a block of records at a time, as
  the plain pipe's has been since #7266. `cat jsonseq-100mb.json | succinctly jq --seq -c .` peaks at 4.97
  MiB of private memory instead of 6.61 MiB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (peak RSS 16.3 MiB instead of
  18.2 MiB) and at a 6.28 MiB footprint instead of 7.94 MiB on an Apple M4 Pro (9.30 MiB peak RSS instead
  of 10.97 MiB), the same at 10 MB and 1 GB, with run times and output unchanged.
