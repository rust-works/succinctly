- **jq: a call to a `def` with no parameters whose body is a string's `length` is answered from the string** (#6833). `def f: length; "abcd" * 1750000 | . as $t | [range(100) | ($t | f)] | .[0]` wrote the bound 7 MB string out as text and indexed it again on every call, 1172 ms on a Ryzen 9 7950X (537 ms on an M4 Pro) against 89 ms (42 ms) for the same program with `length` written inline; the call is now read off the string as the inline stage is, 13x faster at 7 MB, 16x at 700 KB and 5x at 70 KB on the 7950X (13x, 9x, 2.8x on the M4 Pro). The count doors (`length`, `utf8bytelength` and the prefix, suffix and search tests of a string) see through such a call in both modes, so the same program as a `reduce` step in `succinctly yq` is 12x faster at 7 MB on the 7950X (25x on the M4 Pro). A `def` with a parameter, a body that is itself a call, and every shape the door does not answer keep the evaluator, and the answers are the same as before and as jq 1.7.1's. The `. as $w | ($t | f)` shape in `succinctly yq` is #6534's and is not changed here.
