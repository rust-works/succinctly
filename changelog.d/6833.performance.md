- **yq: a call to a `def` whose body is a string's `length` is read off the string in a loop body instead of being written out and indexed again every round** (#6833).
  With `--jq-extensions`, `def f: length; "abcd" * 1750000 | . as $t | [range(100) | . as $w | ($t | f)]` took the reindex bridge once per call,
  as the inline `length` did before #6534; the owned stage now answers a parameterless `def` call whose body is exactly `length` from the string, as `succinctly jq` and both modes' `reduce` step already did (#6841).
  A 7 MB string over 100 rounds takes 32 ms instead of 541 ms on an Apple M4 Pro (16.9x; 1 MB 12.0x, 10 MB 17.9x) and 566 ms instead of 1120 ms on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (2.0x, where the pipe's per-round copy of the bound string remains, #6832).
  The output is unchanged.
