- **jq: a call to a `def` with no parameters whose body is a string's `length` is answered from the string** (#6833). `def f: length; "abcd" * 1750000 | . as $t | [range(100) | ($t | f)] | .[0]` wrote the bound 7 MB string out as text and indexed it again on every call, 0.55 s of user time on an Apple laptop against 0.04 s for the same program with `length` written inline; the call is now read off the string as the inline stage is (0.04 s). The count doors (`length`, `utf8bytelength` and the prefix, suffix and search tests of a string) see through such a call in both modes, so `reduce range(40) as $i (0; . + ($t | f))` in `succinctly yq` takes 0.01 s where it took 0.20 s. A `def` with a parameter, a body that is itself a call, and every shape the door does not answer keep the evaluator, and the answers are the same as before and as jq 1.7.1's. The `. as $w | ($t | f)` shape in `succinctly yq` is #6534's and is not changed here.
