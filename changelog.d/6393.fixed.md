- **yq: a line of spaces wider than a block scalar's indent is content, as it is in yq** (#6393). go-yaml eats a block scalar line's spaces only up to the indent column, so a line of spaces alone with more of them than the indent has the extra spaces as its text. `a: |` over `  line1` and a four-space line (the kind an editor leaves behind) is `"line1\n  \n"` in yq; `succinctly yq` called that line blank and answered `"line1\n"`, and `|-` lost the line, `>` and `>-` likewise; the same line first under an explicit indent (`a: |3` over four spaces) was dropped too. A line no wider than the indent is still blank, and `|+` already kept both. The range finder and the folded decoder in `light.rs` now read it so, and its indent detection takes the widest leading blank line as the parser does (#6381), which also fixes a scalar whose widest blank line precedes a `# c` line. Checked against yq v4.53.3 on 3,344 documents (three hosts, eleven headers, eleven trailing-line shapes, explicit indents, end of input and the document root) with no difference, and on 5,500 random block-scalar documents, where the documents that differed in value fell from 116 per 1,500 to 0.
