- **jq: a stage over a bound array that is a pipe of door-answered stages, an `as` bind of a count or an `if` over a count is answered from the value** (#7132, part two). `$a | length | tostring`, `$a | has(0) | not`, `$a | first | tostring`, `$a | length as $n | $n + 1` and `$a | if length > 5 then "big" else . end` in a loop over a bound array each wrote `$a` out as text and indexed it again (4 ms per call over 100,000 elements, jq's 0.08); they are now 0.03 ms.
