- **yq: a `{...}` with a `select` or a `.[]` in a pair no longer pays for the fold on every record** (#4240). #4193 sent every construction with an operand that might be empty through yq's fold up front, +9% (two entries) to +16% (five) on a 7 MB document. An empty operand always empties the cross product, so the cross product is now built first and the fold runs only for a record whose construction came out empty: -5% to -8% against #4193's route on a 7-14 MB `users` document (M4 Pro, interleaved, control range -2% to +2%), and within noise of the fan-out it replaced. The trade-off: a construction that is empty for every record is built twice, which reads +54% on a document where `select` drops every record (`.users[] | {"n": .name, "s": (.score | select(. > 1e8))}`) -- slower than #4193's route there, though no slower in output. Side effects in such an operand are not run twice.
