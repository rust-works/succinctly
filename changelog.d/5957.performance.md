- **jq: a parenthesised read of a bound variable (`($x).k`, `$x | (.k)`, `$x | (.k)?`) defers the bind under a repeated ambient as `$x.k` does** (#5957). The deferred bind's walk tested the head of a field chain with a bare `$x` and accepted `.k` and `.k?` as field stages, so `. as $x | .users[] | ($x).limit` kept the bind eager and decoded the whole bound node although only one member is read; a parenthesis only groups. One definition of "the variable" and of "a field read", both seeing through parentheses, now serves the deferred bind's walk, the fold's projection and `is_field_read`. On the Apple M5 Max (interleaved min of 11, output identical, control within +-0.8%), `. as $x | [.users[] | select(.n > ($x).limit)] | length` over a 1 MB, 10 MB and 100 MB document takes 17.7 ms, 164 ms and 2.66 s instead of 28.2 ms, 271 ms and 3.76 s (-37%, -40%, -29%), `$x | (.limit)` -46%, -46% and -29%, and the bare `$x.limit` spelling, which already deferred, is unchanged (-0.9% to -0.3%). x86_64 and the M4 Pro were not measured.
