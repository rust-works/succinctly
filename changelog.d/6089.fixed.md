- **yq: `(PATH | key) = value` and `(PATH | key) |= filter` rename the key** (#6089). `printf 'a:\n  x: 3\n  y: 4\n' | yq '(.a.x | key) = "meow"'` prints `meow: 3` in place of `x: 3`; `succinctly yq` accepted it and printed the document unchanged with exit 0. The new name keeps the key's quoting, anchor and comments, `|=` binds `.` to the key's text, and a later pipe stage sees the new name (`(.a.x | key) = "z" | .a | keys` is `z`, `y`). A name that is already a member (yq keeps both entries), a mapping or sequence, or a text with a line break is refused with an error, and `+=`, `-=`, `*=` and `//=` on a key target are a parse error where they used to do nothing; the remaining gaps are in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
