- **`succinctly jq` reads one input at a time under `fromstream(inputs)`, `inputs[k]` and `in(inputs)`** (#7572). Each collected every output of its generator before looking at the first, so it read the whole input first: an error on an early input named the end of the input (`printf '1\n2\n' | jq -n 'fromstream(inputs)'` said `(at <stdin>:2)` where jq 1.7.1 says `:1`; `[inputs[.]]` over `[1,2,3]` said `<unknown>` where jq says `:0`), and the inputs jq leaves unread for the main loop and for a `catch` were gone (`printf '1 2 3' | jq '[.] | in(inputs)'` exited 5 where jq exits 0). They now take one output at a time and stop at an error, as jq's `foreach`, `E | .[$k]` and `xs | has($x)` do, so a `stderr` or `debug` of a later output no longer runs either.
