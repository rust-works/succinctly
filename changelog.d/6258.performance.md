- **jq: a literal-bound slice of a variable or other owned array is cut in place** (#6258).
  `. as $a | [range(100) | $a[:2] | add] | length` wrote the whole array out as JSON, indexed it and decoded the kept part on every call.
  Over 200,000 integers it took 1.9 s (min of 7) and 1.2 GB; it is now 15 ms and 15 MB, 129x faster, and 133x faster over 1,000,000 integers (10.6 s to 80 ms), since the cost no longer grows with the array.
  Apple M4 Pro under a busy machine, CPU time, interleaved.
  Answers are unchanged: the bridge's number spelling (`1` read back as a literal) prints the same.
  `path()` of a literal slice and a literal slice of a string cursor are not moved (#6265).
