- **jq: a computed integer compares as a double against a decimal literal, as in jq** (#6275).
  `jq_numeric_cmp` read a bare `Int` as a literal, so the result of `1+1`, `length` or `floor` was compared by its exact decimal digits: `(1+1) == 2.00000000000000000001` and `(0+0) == 1e-400` were `false` where jq answers `true`, and `(9007199254740992+0) == 9007199254740993` likewise.
  A bare `Int` is now a computed double; two literals still compare exactly. A computed integer that crosses the reindex bridge (`[2.00000000000000000001,(1+1)] | unique`) and the first value of a `range` still read as a literal; both are listed in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
