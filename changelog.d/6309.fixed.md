- **jq: a `{n,}` repeat (n of 2 or more) of a capture group ends at the body's empty way and reports its empty iteration, as Oniguruma's does** (#6309). `"abb" | [match("(b|){2,}$";"g") | .captures[0].string]` printed `["b",""]`; jq 1.7.1 answers `["",""]`, and `"ba" | [match("(b*|a){2,}";"g") | .string]` printed `["ba",""]` where jq answers `["b","",""]`. The capture rewrite (#6069) now takes such a repeat as its `*` loop, and the loop rewrite (#6106, #6305) reads that. Over 2,498 generated `{2,}`, `{3,}` and `{4,}` patterns the 519 that differed from jq now agree, and none changed the other way; a counted repeat over a body with a group inside it, and a bounded one, keep their earlier answer (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`).
