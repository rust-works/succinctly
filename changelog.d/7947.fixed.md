- **jq: a string read past a caught input fault decodes invalid UTF-8 as jq does** (#7947). After a fault, jq drops the rest of its read, so its lexer starts the next read outside any string; `succinctly jq` substituted the whole text once, deciding in or out of a string by its quotes, and a quote in the dropped part of the read put the strings after it out of step. A real string body then got the rule for bytes outside strings: with `1 a"`, spaces to byte 4,095, and a string holding `E0 80 80`, `[limit(3; repeat(try input catch .))]` under `-n` gave three U+FFFD where jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 give one, and a surrogate (`ED A0 80`) or a sequence above U+10FFFF (`F4 90 80 80`) gave one per byte. A value read past a fault that holds a substituted byte is now substituted again from the bytes jq read, from its own first byte; a run with no invalid UTF-8, or no fault, is unchanged.
