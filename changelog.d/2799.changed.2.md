- **yq: `sort` and `sort_by` follow real yq's comparator and sort algorithm** (#2799, part 3). yq orders `null` first, then bools, two integers numerically, two numbers as floats and anything else by the node's *text*, with containers tying (empty text) and sorting before any non-empty text, so `[2, "10", 3] | sort` is `["10",2,3]` (was `[2,3,"10"]`), `[.b, .a] | sort` with `"1"` and `1` is `["1",1]`, and `[[2],[1]] | sort` is unchanged. That relation is not a strict weak order, so a mixed array's order depends on the algorithm; yq runs Go's `sort.Stable` and succinctly ports it (`src/jq/go_sort.rs`) for mixed arrays, keeping Rust's sort for single-kind ones where the answers are identical. `sort_by(.a, .b)` compares the key lists position by position. yq panics on NaN or infinity here; this build orders them rather than crash. jq mode is unchanged.
