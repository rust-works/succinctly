- **jq: navigating into a large owned value no longer re-indexes all of it** (#4052). `(.|tojson|fromjson) as $x | .users[] | {r: $x} | .r.users[0].id` serialized and indexed the whole 63 KB `{r: $x}` at every element to read one field, so it was quadratic in the document: 66 M, 2,448 M and 21,595 M instructions over 6 KB, 64 KB and 196 KB `users` documents on an Apple M5 Max. The owned navigation peel (`projection_peel`) checked that a pipe had a stage behind its first before it looked inside that stage, and `.r.users[0].id` reaches it as the one stage of a pipe, with nothing behind it; the peel declined and the bridge took the whole object. A lone nested pipe is now flattened first: 43 M, 110 M and 259 M instructions on the same three documents, and 3.5 ms -> 2.4 ms, 113 ms -> 6.4 ms and 993 ms -> 14.8 ms wall-clock on a 7950X (min of 5, interleaved), with identical output in both modes (630 stage/wrapper pairs against the previous build). The cost no longer depends on whether an `as` binding happens to be in scope.
