- **jq: streaming input holds less: a `--seq` file with no RS is scanned, not held, and a pipe's windows are shared, not copied** (#7260).
  A lone `--seq` file that does not open with an RS was read whole just to print jq's one "Unfinished abandoned
  text at EOF" warning; it is now scanned 64 KiB at a time with jq's own read rules. A pipe's certifying thread
  reads each window's buffer through a shared handle instead of a copy, the probe is read and freed in 1 MiB
  blocks, the lazy `inputs` route reads every window onto its text, and a resumed `--seq` tail carries its line
  count instead of a line break per line. `--seq -c '.users[]|.id'` over 100 MB with no RS peaks at 5.3 MB
  instead of 76 MB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X and 3.5 MB instead of 74 MB on an Apple M4 Pro (jq 1.7.1: 3.6 and
  2.8 MB), 79% and 40% faster; `-r .name` on a 100 MB NDJSON pipe peaks at 7.5 MB instead of 10.7 MB and 6.3 MB
  instead of 10.7 MB, and `reduce inputs` 1-1.2 MB less.
