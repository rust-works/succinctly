- **`succinctly yq` deletes the node a `del()` target derives from through a `select(...)` stage, and keeps the document for a target that selects nothing** (#4476). `del(.arr[] | select(. > 1) | tostring)` over `{"arr": [1, 2, 3]}` is `{"arr": [1]}` as in yq v4.53.3, and `del(.a | select(false), 1)` leaves the document, where both raised `Invalid path expression`. A probe of 240 `del()` targets (6 documents, 40 targets built from `select` with `tostring`, `length`, `not`, `@json`, `type`, comma and optional forms) fixed 80 and changed none that already matched. A derived document root (`del(.. | select(...) | tostring)`), arithmetic and `map` after a `select`, and a bound variable are not part of this fix.
