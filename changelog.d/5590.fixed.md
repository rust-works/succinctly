- **jq: three programs jq 1.7.1 compiles no longer fail with exit 3** (#5590). A surrogate pair in a string literal (`"\ud83d\ude00"`) is one character, `"😀"`; a lone low surrogate is U+FFFD, and a high one without its low half stays a compile error, as in jq. A keyword spelled right after the dot (`.then`, `.elif`, `.else`, `.end`, `.as`, `.catch`, `.and`, `.or`) is a field name, where `succinctly jq` read the dot as the identity and failed on the word; `. and true` and `if . then 1 end` read as before. A namespaced function name (`def a::b: 1; a::b`, `def a::b::c(x): x; a::b::c(1)`) defines and calls. `succinctly yq` is unchanged. Pinned against the jq 1.7.1 source build in `tests/jq_cli/i5590.rs`; the syntax-form probes of `dev compare jq` (#5552) stop listing the ten differences they found.
