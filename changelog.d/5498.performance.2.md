- **jq: `--stream` and `--stream-errors` under `-n` hand `input`/`inputs` their events as the program asks, not from a list built first** (#5498).
  `--stream -n 'reduce (inputs|select(length==2)) as $e (0;.+1)'` over a generated `users` document held every event before the program ran: 207 MB for 6.9 MB of input, 1.96 GB for 69 MB (jq 2.4 MB).
  The `jv_parse.c` port (`jq_stream_errors.rs`) is now a pull-based reader that feeds jq's streaming parser one `fgets` refill at a time, and the `input` queue pulls from it through the producer seam (#5842), so the program holds the input's bytes and the parser's state: 17 MB and 78 MB at those sizes on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X, 13 MB and 74 MB on an Apple M4 Pro.
  `--stream` also stops being slower than jq there: interleaved min of 11 against the merge-base, 0 output differences, on the 7950X the `reduce` is 47% faster at 6.9 MB and 41% faster at 69 MB, and `first(inputs)` takes 4.4 ms and 29.5 ms (was 233 ms and 2.39 s); on the M4 Pro the `reduce` is 13% and 14% faster and `first(inputs)` takes 3.2 ms and 8.7 ms (was 114 ms and 1.11 s).
  `--stream-errors` reads the same way: 40% faster `reduce` and 98% faster `first(inputs)` on the 7950X, 8-9% and 97-99% on the M4 Pro.
  The other `--stream` routes (a program that reads `.`, `-s`, no `-n`) are unchanged here; slices 4 and 5 moved them.
