- **yq: arithmetic on a hex or octal integer keeps its base** (#3027).
  `0x10 + 1` is `0x11` and `0o17 + 1` is `0o20`, as in yq, which writes `+`, `-`, `*` and `%` results with the left operand's format (upper-case hex digits, the sign after the prefix: `0x-10`; `1 + 0x10` is `17`).
  `/`, and a float operand, now fail with yq's `strconv.ParseFloat: parsing "0x10": invalid syntax` instead of computing a result.
  `-o=json` still prints the number.
