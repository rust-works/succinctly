- **jq: the earlier regex engine and `fancy-regex` are removed** (#7965). ADR-0031's last step deletes the imitation of Oniguruma over the `regex` and `fancy-regex` crates, about 18,700 lines, now that jq mode runs on the vendored Oniguruma; `SUCCINCTLY_REGEX_ONIG=0` no longer selects it, and `fancy-regex` is no longer a dependency. No answer changes: on the 459,678-row regex corpus every answer is identical to the build before. The stripped release binary is 4.6% smaller (16,965,216 to 16,187,872 bytes, macOS arm64), more than the 2.8% the Oniguruma build added. yq mode keeps the `regex` crate.
