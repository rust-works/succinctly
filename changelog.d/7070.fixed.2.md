- **`succinctly yq` keeps a sequence's flow style and quotes when `+` appends a scalar to it** (#7070). `{"k": (.a + "z")}`, `[.a + "z"]` and `.a + "z"` over `a: ['x']` printed a block sequence (`- x` / `- z`); they print `['x', 'z']`, with the appended scalar in the quote style of the first element, as yq does (`[x, 'y']` gives `[x, 'y', z]`). 165 rows from yq v4.53.3 are pinned. A write (`.a += "z"`), a number and a variable operand are recorded in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
