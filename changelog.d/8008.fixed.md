- **yq: `del()` treats `env(NAME)`, `strenv(NAME)` and `now` as parentless targets, as yq does** (#8008).
  `del(env(X))`, `del(.[] | env(X))` and `del(.a, now)` raised `Invalid path expression with result "hi"` where yq leaves the document unchanged; like a literal, such a target now ends yq's back-to-front delete loop, so `del(env(X), .a)` deletes `.a` and `del(.a, env(X))` deletes nothing. `del(.[] | select(. == env(X)))` still deletes the matching entries, and `succinctly jq` keeps jq's refusal of `del(now)`. 62 rows captured from yq v4.53.3, across the document, `-n`, `ea`, `-o=json` and `-i` routes.
