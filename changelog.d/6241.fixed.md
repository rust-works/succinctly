- **jq: a lazy counted repeat over a body that holds an option group, a bare `\p{L}` among them, is written out as jq writes it** (#6241). `"ca" | [match("(?:a?|\\p{L}){2,}?"; "gn") | .string]` was `["ca"]`; jq 1.7.1 answers `["c","a"]`, as it does for `(?:a?|\\w){2,}?`. The length of a body that held an option group was not worked out, so it was taken for a long one and given the counted-loop rewrite, where Oniguruma writes `n` plain copies; `succinctly jq` puts such a group around every bare `\p{..}`, which is how `\p{L}` differed from `[\p{L}]`. An option group (`(?s:\w)`, `(?-i:\p{L})`, `(?i:b)?`) now counts as its body. Over 11,760 body shapes, counts and texts the answers match jq except one pattern, `\w` under an inline `i` (#6263).
