- **jq: a look-behind with alternatives of different widths is refused as jq refuses it** (#6201). Oniguruma, in the Perl syntax jq uses, rejects `(?<=a|bc)d`, `(?<!a(?:b|cd))e` and `(?<=(?:a|bc){2})d` (`invalid pattern in look-behind`), and the `regex` crate answered them (`(?<!a|bc)d` returned `[]` where jq errors). A look-behind whose body holds an alternation of branches of fixed, differing widths, none written as empty, is now an error when the body has no repeat of a varying count, and when the alternation is the body or an item of it under a repeat that must run at least once (`(?<=(?:a|bc)+)`). Alternatives of one width (`ab|cd`), a branch written empty (`a|`) and a body that repeats a varying number of times that may be zero (`(?<=a?)`, `(?<=(?:a|bc)*)`) stay accepted, as in jq. 8,000 generated look-behinds without a varying repeat matched jq 1.7.1 in what they accept, and none that jq accepts was refused over 32,000 more with one.
