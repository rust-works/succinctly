- **jq: a look-behind with alternatives of different widths is refused as jq refuses it** (#6201). Oniguruma, in the Perl syntax jq uses, rejects `(?<=a|bc)d`, `(?<!a(?:b|cd))e` and `(?<=(?:a|bc){2})d` (`invalid pattern in look-behind`), and the `regex` crate answered them (`(?<!a|bc)d` returned `[]` where jq errors). A look-behind whose body holds an alternation of branches of fixed, differing widths, none written as empty, and no repeat of a varying count, is now an error. Alternatives of one width (`ab|cd`), a branch written empty (`a|`) and a body that repeats a varying number of times (`(?<=a?)`) stay accepted, as in jq. 8,000 generated look-behinds without a varying repeat matched jq 1.7.1 in what they accept; none that jq accepts is refused.
