- **yq: `filter(f)` over an empty context answers nothing** (#7171). yq runs `filter` once per node of its context, so `.b[]? | filter(. == 1)` prints nothing there; it was parsed as the array construction `[.[] | select(f)]` and printed `[]` once (and `. *=? (.b[]? | filter(. == 1))` failed with `cannot multiply !!map with !!seq`). `filter` is now a builtin of its own that evaluates as that array does for each node it runs on (comments, quotes and anchors of the children it keeps print as before) and that a pipe whose left side matched nothing does not run. Pinned against rows captured from yq v4.53.3.
