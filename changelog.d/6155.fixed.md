- **yq: an integer index that misses a mapping finds no node when it is read as an operand, an assignment's right side or a `select` condition** (#6155). `.a + .[0]` over `a: 1` / `b: 2` raised `number (1) and null (null) cannot be added`, and `select(.[0] | not)` kept the mapping; yq answers `1` and keeps nothing, as it does for `.a + .zz`. An index past the end of a sequence is still a `null` read.
