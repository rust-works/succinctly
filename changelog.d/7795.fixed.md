- **`succinctly jq` retries a `?//` inside `first(...)` in a def again** (#7795). Since #7744 a call whose definition's body is `first(f)` was run to its value before its consumer without looking inside `f`, so `def f: first([1] as [$a] ?// $a | $a); f | if . == 1 then error("e") else . end` raised `e` where jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 retry the bind with its next pattern and answer `[1]`. A `first(f)` whose `f` holds a `?//` keeps the streaming route; nothing else changes.
