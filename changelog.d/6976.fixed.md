- **yq: an empty block scalar after a key that starts with `---` no longer swallows the next entries** (#6976). In `----: >` followed by `b: 1` (also `---a: |` and `---: >`) the scalar read `"\nb: 1\n"` and `b` was also read as an entry of its own, where yq reads the scalar as empty (`{"----":"","b":1}`). The block-scalar reader took any line starting with `---` for a `--- >` marker line, where the scalar is the document's own node and may sit at column 0; a marker is `---` followed by white space, a line break or the end of the text, as the parser already has it. Found by a mutation fuzz of the yaml-test-suite inputs against yq v4.53.3.
