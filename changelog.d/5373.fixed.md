- **jq: `\w` and `\W` are Oniguruma's word class** (#5373). Outside a character class jq's `\w` also matches `²`, `³`, `¹`, `¼`, `½` and `¾` (its Latin-1 table counts them) and does not match U+200C/U+200D, which the `regex` crate's `\w` does: `"x²y" | [splits("\W")]` is `["x²y"]` in jq and was `["x","y"]`, `"a²" | gsub("\w";"x")` is `"xx"` and was `"x²"`. Inside a class (`[\w-]`, `[^\W]`) jq's `\w` is the plain Unicode word, so the six are not in it there. `\b` is still the `regex` crate's, so a boundary next to one of the six differs. yq mode is unchanged.
