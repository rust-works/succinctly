- **`strptime`'s `%Z` reads only `GMT` and the local zone's names, and `GMT` and `%s` convert to local time last** (#5754). `%Z` took any run of letters; it now takes a run of capital letters naming `GMT` or one of the local zone's `tzname` names (its latest standard and daylight-time names, `AEST` and `AEDT` in Sydney), and anything else is no match, as in jq. `GMT`, like `%s`, makes the fields a UTC instant that becomes local time once the whole format is read, so `"2015-03-05 10:00 GMT" | strptime("%F %R %Z")` is 21:00 in Sydney and a field read after `%s` is a UTC one. An instant before 1900 is 1969-12-31 23:59:59 UTC, as jq's `timegm` gives it.
