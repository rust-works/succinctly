- **yq: `..` stops at an alias** (#4214). yq's recursive descent yields an alias node and does not descend into it; `succinctly yq` followed the alias and delivered the target's children a second time, so on `anchors: {base: &b {p: 1, q: 2}, copy: *b}` `[.. | path | join(".")]` listed `anchors.copy.p` and `anchors.copy.q`, `[..] | length` was 8 where yq says 6, and `.. | select(. == 1)` matched twice. `.. | path`, `.. | key`, `.. | parent`, `recurse` and `.anchors.copy | ..` now stop at the alias; `.anchors.copy[]` still iterates the aliased mapping, as in yq. jq mode and documents without aliases are unchanged.
