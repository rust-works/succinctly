- **`succinctly jq` copies a recursive `def`'s body once fewer per call when there is nothing to substitute into it** (#7699). A call to a `def` with `$` parameters copied the body to substitute the parameters' bare names into it even when the body never reads them, before binding the `$` names with their `as` wrappers; a recursion copies the body on every call, since each level's call nodes are new. The copy is now skipped when no parameter is read by its bare name and no argument is a literal: 19-25% less time on the recursions #7460 named (`def f($n): ... f($n - 1) | f($n - 1) ...; 0 | f(16)` 281.6 -> 227.8 ms on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X, 251.5 -> 189.0 ms on an Apple M4 Pro; jq 1.7.1 44-46 ms), `f($n - 1) + f($n - 1)`, `fib($n)`, `[f, f] | add` and three piped calls alike (-23.5 to -25.7% instructions), output identical; a body that reads a bare parameter, a literal argument and a call whose bound body is cached are unchanged. The rest of the gap to jq (3-6x on these shapes) is the copies that remain, recorded in #7699.
