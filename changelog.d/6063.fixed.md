- **yq: `--argjson` reads a number written with a bare trailing dot (`5.`, `-0.`, `007.`), as the `-p json` reader does** (#6063). `succinctly yq --argjson n 5.` was refused (`invalid JSON for --argjson n`), where yq's JSON reader answers `{"n":5}` for `{"n":5.}`. A whole-valued span is now the integer (`5.` prints `5`, not `5.0`), also for `--input-format json` under `--slurp` and `--eval-all`; `.5` stays `0.5`, and `.`, `-.`, `1.5.` and `1..` are still refused.
