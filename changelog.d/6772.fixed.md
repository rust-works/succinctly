- **yq: `head_comment` keeps the blank lines of the comment block and joins a comment above a leading `---` to the one under it, as in yq** (#6772).
  yq's head comment is the text above the node with its blank lines kept: `# c` / blank / `a: 1` is `c` followed by a line feed (so yq's own
  comment-operators example prints `welcome!` and then a blank line), a blank line inside the block stays (`# c` / blank / `# d` is `c`, a blank line, `d`),
  and a first document's `# c` / `---` / `# d` is one text, `c` and `d` on two lines, where the first key the comment under the marker
  was indexed on has none (`.a | key | head_comment` is empty). `succinctly yq` answered the comment lines alone, and
  left `d` on that key. An indented continuation (`# c` / `  # d`) is still answered as `c` and `d`, where yq keeps the indentation and the `#`.
