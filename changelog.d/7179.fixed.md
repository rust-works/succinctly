- **yq: under `--eval-all`, `!=` reads its operands read-only, as yq does, so an absent key is empty there and `null` for `==`** (#7179). Both cross the whole document list, so `yq ea '.zz != 1' a1 a2 a3` is four `true` (an empty left runs once per right result) and `.a != .c` is the one pairing of the document that has `a` with the one that has `c`; `succinctly yq` evaluated `!=` as it does `==`, sixteen results for the first. `==`, `<`, `>`, `<=` and `>=` and every run without `--eval-all` are unchanged. The rows are pinned against yq v4.53.3; the origin of a comparison's boolean (`.k != 20 | fi`) and `and`/`or` under `--eval-all` still differ (limitations.md).
