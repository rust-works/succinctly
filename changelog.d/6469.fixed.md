- **jq: a lazy repeat that keeps its text no longer costs the pattern's other lazy repeats their write-out** (#6469). A pattern is read again at the next reading only for the lazy repeat it gave up inside, so `"aac" | [match("(?:^x*|(?:(y*)|x)+(?:(y)|x?){0,2})*?(?:(?:(b*)|a){0,2}?)*?c";"g") | .captures | map(.string)]` is `[[null,null,null]]`, as in jq 1.7.1, where the last group was `""`.
