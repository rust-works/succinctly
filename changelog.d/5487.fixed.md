- **jq: a pipe headed by a variable keeps the variable's storage identity** (#5487). `[nan] as $a | ($a | . | .) == $a` was `false` where jq says `true` (jq's `jv_equal` short-circuits on `jv_identical`, and `[nan] == [nan]` is `false`): a pipe of two or more stages headed by `$var` handed back a rebuilt copy of the container. A container leaving the reindex bridge now comes back as the storage that went in, as every other read of the bridge already did, and a jq-mode slice naming every element of a non-empty array is that array, as the cursor-backed slice already answered. The `==` and `as` forms, an object, a generator around the pipe and a full-range slice all match jq 1.7.1 now.
