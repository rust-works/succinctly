- **jq: a value built past 384 levels is printed as jq prints it, not refused at the output** (#5554). A program that built a value more than 384 levels deep (`reduce range(1000) as $i ([]; .[0] = .)`, `1, setpath([range(400)|0]; 1), 7`, a recursive `def` that wraps its result) exited 5 with `nesting depth exceeds limit of 384` and printed nothing, where jq prints 256 levels, `<skipped: too deep>` and the closers. The refusal was a check made before the writer ran, and jq's conventions have a writer that stops at jq's print depth, so the check is gone. Output is byte-identical to jq 1.7.1 (stdout and exit status) for 10 programs across 12 flag combinations (compact, `--tab`, `--indent`, `-S`, `-r`, `-j`, `-a`, `-C`, `--seq`, `-e`) over `-n` and a document input. A deep value consumed by another construct (`[., 1]`, a comma after it, `error`, `halt_error`, `debug`, `stderr`, `first`) and a fold step with no owned door still refuse, inside the evaluator.
