- **yq: a binding or a fold after a stage that matched nothing runs its body, as in yq** (#8044). yq evaluates a binding's body over its context once per output of its source, or once with nothing bound when the source yields nothing, and a fold over a source that yields nothing is its INIT, so over an empty context both answer like the literal or collect they hold (#6943): `select(false) | (. as $y | 3)` is `3`, `select(false) | (. as $y | [.])` is `[]`, `select(false) | (1 as $y | $y)` is `1` and `select(false) | (.[] as $x ireduce (0; . + 1))` is `0` in yq, where `succinctly yq` printed nothing. It now answers as yq does in a pipe, under `--eval-all`, on the right side of `=` and `|=`, in `map` and `with_entries` and in a fold's UPDATE over an empty accumulator list (`.[] as $x ireduce (select(false); . as $y | [.])` over `[1, 2, 3]` is `[[[]]]`). `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
