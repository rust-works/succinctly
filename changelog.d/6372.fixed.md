- **yq: a tab after an explicit indent's spaces, and after the spaces of the line that ends a block scalar, is refused as yq refuses it** (#6372). go-yaml eats a block scalar's leading spaces while the column is below the indent and raises `found a tab character where an indentation space is expected` on a tab met there; #6295 made that so for an auto-detected indent. What was left: a tab after fewer spaces than an explicit indent indicator gives (`a: |2` over ` <tab>x`, which read as an empty scalar and a stray key), and a tab after the spaces of the dedented line that ends the scalar (`a: |` over `  x` and ` <tab>y`, which read as `{"a":"x\n","y":...}`). A tab where the indent is known (`a: |2` over `  <tab>x`, or after the first content line) is still content. Checked against yq v4.53.3 on a grid of 11,340 documents (four hosts, nine headers, five leading-line shapes, 0-6 spaces), with no difference in which are refused, and on 6,000 random block-scalar documents, none newly refused here that yq reads.
