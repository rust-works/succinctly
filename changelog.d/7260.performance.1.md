- **jq: `-sR` moves a single input into its string instead of copying it** (#7260).
  The run held the text twice, as read and as the string; now it holds it once. `-sR length` on 100 MB of
  NDJSON peaks at 112 MB instead of 214 MB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X, against jq 1.7.1's 142 MB, and at 106 MB
  instead of 209 MB on an Apple M4 Pro, against 244 MB. It is also 45% and 22% faster (96.6 -> 53.0 ms and
  22.9 -> 17.7 ms). Several inputs are joined into the first one's buffer, reserved once.
