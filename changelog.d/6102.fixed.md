- **yq: `pivot` pads a short row with an empty node, as yq does** (#6102). `printf -- '- [foo, bar]\n- [sis]\n' | yq pivot` prints `-` with no value for the cell the short row lacks; `succinctly yq` printed `null`. A `pivot` that is the whole filter now also moves every cell with its own comment, quoting, anchor, alias and tag (`"c"`, `&x d` and `# cc` used to come out plain) and quotes a mapping key that reads as another type (`"1":`). `-o=json` still prints `null`. A `pivot` inside a larger filter (`[pivot]`, `.x |= pivot`, `pivot | .[1][1]`) still pads with `null`; the gaps are listed in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
