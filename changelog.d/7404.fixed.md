- **jq: `--seq` keeps jq's "Potentially truncated top-level numeric value at EOF" warning after a stretch that ends at an RS with a value pending** (#7404).
  On the windowed route (a file of 4 MiB or more, or a pipe), the stream's tail was handed on at that RS as if
  nothing were pending, which in jq's last read ends the stream, so the bare value after it and jq's warning about
  it were lost. The tail now carries the pending value's fault, as jq's reader does.
