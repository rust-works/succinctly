- **jq: `toboolean`, `trim`, `ltrim` and `rtrim` word their errors as jq 1.8.2 does** (#7529). A string `toboolean` refuses is quoted with JSON escapes (`\u0001`, `\u007f`, `\u0000`), where it showed Rust's (`\u{1}`, `\u{7f}`, `\0`), and the refusal of a non-string names the value (`null (null) cannot be parsed as a boolean`, `array ([1]) ...`), which it left out. The three trim builtins say `trim input must be a string` on a non-string, where they said `trim requires string`, `ltrim requires string` and `rtrim requires string`. These are 1.8 builtins, so 1.8.2 is the only reference.
