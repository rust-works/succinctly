- **jq: a pipe on standard input holds about 6 MiB less** (#7266).
  The plain route's pipe reader handed out the 4 MiB its probe read as one window with a block read onto it,
  which grew the buffer to 8 MiB and gave the certifying thread a 5 MiB copy; the probe is now handed out a
  block at a time from the buffer it was read into. `cat ndjson-100mb.json | succinctly jq -r .name` peaks
  at 17.2 MiB instead of 23.3 MiB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (private memory 6.2 MiB instead of 12.5 MiB) and
  at 11.8 MiB instead of 18.2 MiB on an Apple M4 Pro, the same at 10 MB and 1 GB and for `-c .` and `@tsv`
  over a pipe, with run times and output unchanged.
