- **`succinctly yq` reads a hexadecimal integer literal in filter source** (#7615). A filter `0x10` was a parse error before; it is now the integer 16 and keeps its spelling like every literal that carries its source text: `0x10 | tostring` is `"0x10"`, `.a = 0x10` writes `a: 0x10`, `[0x1f, 31] | unique` is `[31,31]`, `0x10 | tag` is `!!int`, and `0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF` is a parse error, all as yq v4.53.3 behaves. jq mode still refuses the spelling, and `0x`, `0xg` and `-0x10` stay errors.
