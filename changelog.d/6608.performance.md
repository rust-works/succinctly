- **jq: a constructor of static reads, `{id, name}`, `[.id]` or `{a: .b.c, k: "lit"}`, is built from the tree instead of re-indexing the value it reads** (#6608).
  After `recurse(.children[]?)`, each visited node was written out as JSON text and indexed again to read two
  fields, so the walk grew with depth at a fixed node count. `[.[] | recurse(.children[]?) | {id, name}] | length`
  over 1 million nodes in chains 10, 30 and 60 deep went from 2.08, 2.98 and 4.90 s to 0.84, 0.80 and 1.31 s
  (jq 1.7.1: 0.98-1.05 s), and `| [.id]` from 1.71, 2.52 and 4.40 s to 0.69, 0.65 and 1.14 s (Apple M5). A
  computed key, a generator, a variable, or a step that would raise (`.id` on a number) still takes the old route,
  so every output and every error is unchanged.
