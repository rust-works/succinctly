- **jq: a constructor of static reads, `{id, name}`, `[.id]` or `{a: .b.c, k: "lit"}`, is built from the tree instead of re-indexing the value it reads** (#6608).
  After `recurse(.children[]?)`, each visited node was written out as JSON text and indexed again to read two
  fields, so the walk grew with depth at a fixed node count. `[.[] | recurse(.children[]?) | {id, name}] | length`
  over 1 million nodes in chains 10, 30 and 60 deep went from 3.00, 4.07 and 6.17 s to 1.51, 1.39 and 1.91 s
  (jq 1.7.1: 1.71-1.76 s, so 1.2-1.3x faster than jq on the first two), and `| [.id]` from 2.25, 3.36 and 5.59 s
  to 1.09, 1.01 and 1.52 s (AMD Ryzen 9 7950X, interleaved, median of 5). A computed key, a generator, a variable,
  or a step that would raise (`.id` on a number) still takes the old route, so every output and every error is
  unchanged. A walk more than about 40 levels deep is still slower than jq.
