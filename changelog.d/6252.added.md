- **jq: Oniguruma's absence operator `(?~X)` is read as jq reads it** (#6252). `"abcab" | match("(?~ab)"; "g")` was refused as `invalid regex`; jq 1.7.1 answers `""`, `"bc"`, `""`, `"b"`, `""`. The operator repeats any character, newline included, up to where `X` would match, and is written that way (`(?:(?:(?!X)(?s:.))*)`) before the engines see the pattern, so a quantifier after it, a capture group, a lookaround or a backreference around it work as in jq. Over 5,075 generated cases (29 absent bodies in 35 positions, flags `g`, `gn`, `gi`, `gx` and `gl`) the answers match jq's; the exceptions are a lazy repeat of the operator under `l` (the `l` flag's own limit) and two copies of one group name. A range cutter `(?~|…)`, an operator inside another one and one beside a quoted run stay refused.
