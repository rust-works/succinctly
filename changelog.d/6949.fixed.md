- **yq: a multi-line plain key-only entry of a flow mapping is read when a line of it ends in a blank** (#6949). `{a ` / ` b}`, `{ multi⇥` / `  line, a: b}` and `- {a ` / ` b}` were a parse error, where yq reads the key `"a b"` with a null: the implicit-key scanner ended the key at the blanks before the line break, and the loop that followed refused the continuation line. The explicit-key scanner had handed that decision to its line-break arm since #402; the implicit one does now. A key that reaches a `:` on a later line (`{a ` / ` b: 1}`), a comment between the lines and a quoted key with a continuation are still refused, as in yq. Found by a mutation fuzz of the yaml-test-suite inputs against yq v4.53.3. The blank arm of both key scanners (implicit and explicit) also re-scanned the rest of a blank run from each blank of it, so a key holding 320,000 consecutive blanks took 18 to 23 seconds; each now steps to the end of the run once and takes a few milliseconds.
