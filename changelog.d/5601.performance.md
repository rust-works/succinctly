- **jq: the stage after a computed number no longer writes the number out and indexes it again** (#5601, #5593 follow-up).
  `[.numbers[]|.+1|.*2]` wrote each `.+1` result out as JSON and indexed it to run `.*2`. A computed `Int` or `Float` (not a NaN, not a
  document's literal) now meets an arithmetic stage, `type`, a comparison, `and`/`or`/`not` and `tostring` directly, and `floor`, `ceil`,
  `round`, `trunc`, `sqrt` and `fabs` of any owned number, including the `$x|floor` a `reduce` or `foreach` binds, are one libm call.
  Over a generated `numbers` document (Apple M5 Max, interleaved min, 1 and 10 MB, jq 1.7.1): `[.numbers[]|.+1|.*2]` 0.55x -> 1.08x of jq,
  `.*2|floor` 0.55x -> 1.13x, `.+1|type` 0.65x -> 1.07x, `.+1|floor|.+1|floor` 0.41x -> 1.11x, `floor|.+1` 0.90x -> 1.80x, and
  `reduce .numbers[] as $x (0; .+($x|floor))` 0.63x -> 0.87x. A `select`, `if`, `[...]` or `as` after a computed number still bridges.
