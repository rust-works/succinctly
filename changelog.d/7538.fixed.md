- **CLI: a standard output that cannot be written no longer panics** (#7538). With standard output on `/dev/full`, a full disk or a file past `ulimit -f`, `json generate`, `dsv generate`, `yaml generate`, `jq-locate`, `yq-locate`, the `--version` and `--build-configuration` reports of `succinctly jq` and `succinctly yq`, and the `bench` tables exited 101 after a Rust panic message (`failed printing to stdout`). They now print `succinctly: error: writing output failed: <strerror>` once on standard error and exit 2, as `succinctly jq` ends a failed write to standard output (jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2: `jq: error: writing output failed: ...`, exit 2). A closed pipe (`| head`) ends them with SIGPIPE, as it ends `succinctly jq`, instead of a panic on `EPIPE`.
