- **jq: a fold on an untracked stage steps from the register it carries** (#4049, a #3999 residual). `del(try (.a and (reduce . as {a:$a} ?// $a (0; .))))` on `{"a":true}` is `{}` in jq: the first alternative fails `path_intact`, `?//` retries to `$a`, and the `and` result `true` is `jv_identical` to the register's `true`, so `.a` is deleted. #3999 retried correctly but the fold could not *step* from a register whose position it did not know, and a destructuring `reduce` handed the stage no register, so the result refused ("with result true"). A fold entered on an untracked stage whose register the stage still carries now seeds its pattern walk from that register (it sits at the fold's root), so jq's `path_intact` decides each step -- a `null` or boolean element is the register's node by kind (`path(.a | 5 | reduce null as {a:$q} (null; .))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]`, a `foreach` over it extends the register's own path, `["a","a"]`) -- and a `reduce` over it leaves the register where it entered, as jq's backtracked loop does. A `foreach` still states no register at its emissions, so `del(.a and (foreach . as {a:$a} ?// $a (0; .; .)))` still refuses where jq answers (recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`). Over the 9 fold-pattern operands of the path-register sweep (119,907 rows, `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1), ACCEPT_WRONG stayed 0, DIFF stayed 8, REFUSE_WRONG fell from 7,745 to 7,285 and MATCH rose from 112,154 to 112,614, with no regression.
