- **jq: `mktime` raises `invalid gmtime representation` when the normalised year less 1900 leaves C's `int`, as jq 1.8.2 does** (#7723). `[2147485547,12,1,0,0,0,0,0] | mktime` answered `67768036191676800`; `timegm` cannot store that year back, and `strftime("%s")` of it now prints `-1`. The overflow of a minute, hour or day carry, which macOS's `timegm` also refuses, is the C library's and stays converted here; the difference is recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md` (glibc not captured).
