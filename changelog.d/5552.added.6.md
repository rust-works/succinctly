- **compare(jq): the oracle sweep scripts run as a `succinctly dev compare jq` dimension** (#5552).
  `--dimension sweeps` runs the `scripts/jq-*-sweep.*` scripts listed in `comparison/jq/manifest.toml`'s `[[sweep]]` against the
  harness's subject and pinned jq 1.7.1, reads each one's exit status (0 agrees, 1 diverges, anything else or a timeout is an error),
  checks a diverging sweep against the ledger as `sweep:<id>`, and reports each sweep's result, time and, when it diverges, the end of
  its output; `--ledger-only` re-runs the listed ones. Twenty-six of the 27 sweeps are folded (about 25 minutes on an M4 Pro), and
  all twenty-six agree with jq; `fold-body-message`, whose verdict is an error message that changed against a base build, stays a
  manual tool. To be foldable, `jq-seq-oracle-sweep.py` gained `--oracle` and `jq-path-register-sweep.py` `--jq`, the shell sweeps
  take `JQ_ORACLE` ahead of `/usr/bin/jq`, and they and several Python sweeps exit 2 instead of 1 on a setup error. The nine sweeps
  that judged a change against `--base` and gated nothing without one gained `--jq-gate`, which fails the run on any wrong answer
  against jq alone (`jq-assign-path-oracle-sweep.sh`, a recording, gained `--gate`, which runs a diverging case once more before
  counting it). `assign-path` and `path-context` fold their
  jq-mode half (`--jq-only`; `path-context` no longer calls its yq-only known divergence stale under it). `path-register`,
  `fold-update-alt-bind`, `fold-body-register-grid` and `fold-source-register` take a `--sample` that keeps only the sample:
  `path-register --sample` used to list the whole 63.6M-row grid, about 15 GB, before picking from it, and now holds 80 MB for
  20,000 rows. `docs/guides/developer.md` lists every sweep, what it gates and how it is folded (#5952).
