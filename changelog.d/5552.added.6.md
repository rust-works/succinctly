- **compare(jq): the oracle sweep scripts run as a `succinctly dev compare jq` dimension** (#5552).
  `--dimension sweeps` runs the `scripts/jq-*-sweep.*` scripts listed in `comparison/jq/manifest.toml`'s `[[sweep]]` against the
  harness's subject and pinned jq 1.7.1, reads each one's exit status (0 agrees, 1 diverges, anything else or a timeout is an error),
  checks a diverging sweep against the ledger as `sweep:<id>`, and reports each sweep's result, time and, when it diverges, the end of
  its output; `--ledger-only` re-runs the listed ones. Twelve of the 27 sweeps are folded (about 7 minutes on an M4 Pro), and all
  twelve agree with jq; `comparison/jq/README.md` says why each of the other fifteen is not. To be foldable, `jq-seq-oracle-sweep.py`
  gained `--oracle`, the libm, fanout and `?//`-retry shell sweeps take `JQ_ORACLE` ahead of `/usr/bin/jq`, and those and four Python
  sweeps exit 2 instead of 1 on a setup error.
