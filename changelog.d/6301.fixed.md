- **jq: a loop whose body holds an inline flag switch ends at its empty iteration as Oniguruma's does** (#6301).
  `"ba" | [match("(?:(?i)b*|a)+"; "g") | .string]` is `["b","",""]` in jq and was `["ba",""]` here: the loop keeps the `regex` crate's longer match when its body holds `(?i)`, `(?s)`, `(?m)` or the like, and the rewrite for #6106 had left such a body alone.
  The switch reaches the rest of its group, later ways included, so it is now written as a flag group over what it reaches (`x(?i)y|z` is `x(?i:y)|(?i:z)`) before the loop is rewritten.
  Over 13,056 generated cases (16 body shapes, 7 switches, 6 loop forms, 17 strings) 885 differed from jq before and none after.
  A switch that names `x` or `U`, and a lazy repeat over such a body, keep their loop as written.
