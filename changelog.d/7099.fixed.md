- **yq: a write and `-P` keep the first document's `%YAML` directive and print its leading comments as yq does** (#7099). The text yq sets aside in front of the first document and prints back before it lost parts on every route that builds the output from the document's comment tree (a write such as `.a = 3` or `del(.x)`, `sort_keys`, `to_entries`, `-P`): a `%YAML 1.2` line was dropped, with its `---` too when nothing else was in front of it, and `# a` / `%YAML 1.2` / `---` printed two blank lines in its place; a comment or `%YAML` line indented by blanks lost its indent (yq prints the comment as written and the `%YAML` line as `#   %YAML 1.2`); a comment after `---` over an empty `[]` or `{}` root was dropped, even by a write that fills the root; and a line of only blanks after `---` printed twice when a comment followed it. A head written to a scalar root (`. head_comment = "x"`, `|=` and `comments =` too) now removes the header as yq does, where it left the header in place. Checked against yq v4.53.3 by 5,376 generated documents (headers of comments, indented comments, blank lines, directives and markers over nine kinds of root, under 19 filters): 1,979 now match where they did not, and none that matched stops. A comment after `---` that `to_entries` and `with_entries` move into the first entry, an operator that rebuilds the root (`map(.)`, `{"x": .} | .x`), a header with several `---` lines and something between them, and the `head_comment` read operator over a header still differ.
