- **yq: `length` of a string is its byte count** (#4270). yq measures a string with Go's `len`, so `"héllo" | length` is `6`, `"日本" | length` is `6` and `"😀" | length` is `4`; `succinctly yq` counted characters (`5`, `2`, `1`) and still does in jq mode, where `utf8bytelength` is the byte count. ASCII text, arrays, objects, `null` and numbers (the width of the source spelling) are unchanged.
