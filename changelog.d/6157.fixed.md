- **jq: a repeated group whose body is `\K` is refused** (#6157). `"a" | test("(?:a|\\K)*")`, `(?:\\K|a)+`, `(?:\\K)?` and `(?:a|(?:\\K))*` answered `true`; jq refuses them with `target of repeat operator is invalid` (here with the `invalid regex:` prefix), as #6127 made it for `\b`, `\G` and a look-around. Oniguruma reads `\K` as an assertion like `\G`, so a `\K` among several nodes (the regexes `(?:a\K|b)*`, `(\K)*` and `(?:\K\K)*`) still repeats. A sweep of 7,392 generated `\K`, `\G` and `\b` groups agrees with jq 1.7.1 on every verdict except the two shapes recorded as #6164 and #6165.
