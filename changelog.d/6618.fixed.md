- **jq: a `--seq` record, an `input` or a `--slurpfile` document nested 256 containers deep is read, as jq reads it** (#6618).
  The checked materializer held every node to the 256-level ceiling, the scalar inside the 256th container included, so a document jq accepts (`[[…[1]…]]` with 256 arrays, or 255 arrays around `{"a":1}`) was refused as `nesting depth exceeds limit of 256` by `-n input` and `--slurpfile`, and dropped without a word by `--seq`: `jq --seq -nc '[inputs|type]'` printed `[]` where jq prints `["array"]`, and `--seq -s length` printed `0` where jq prints `1`.
  Only a container is held to the ceiling now (a scalar recurses no deeper); 257 containers is still refused, as jq refuses it.
  `succinctly yq --eval-all` over JSON reads the same 256-container document, as yq does.
  The filters that walk the document through the cursor (`tojson`, `tostring`, `del`, `walk`, `flatten`, `-C` and the rest listed in the limitations page) keep their own guards and still refuse it (#6636); `--argjson` keeps a ceiling of 128 levels (#6637).
