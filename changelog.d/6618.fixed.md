- **jq: a document 256 containers deep is read, as jq 1.7.1 reads it** (#6618). jq's parser counts containers against its depth ceiling and not the scalar the deepest one holds: 256 nested arrays around `1` are accepted and 257 are refused. The materializer counted the scalar as a level as well, so `--seq -n '[inputs|type]'` dropped a record of that depth without a word (`[]` where jq prints `["array"]`), `--seq -s` and plain `-n` with `inputs` refused it (`nesting depth exceeds limit of 256`), and `--seq -s length` counted one record short. All three now agree with jq at 255, 256 and 257 nested arrays. An object chain reaches jq's ceiling at about 128 deep (each key takes a second slot of its parser's stack), where it agreed already. Filters that walk a value 256 deep (`tojson`, `walk`, `==`, `sort`) still refuse it where jq does not (#6631).
