- **yq: a tab after a U+2028, U+2029 or U+0085 break in a block scalar is refused below the scalar's indent** (#6287). go-yaml ends a block scalar's line at those three breaks as at `\n`, so `a: |` over `  x<U+2028><tab>y` is `found a tab character where an indentation space is expected`, as is a tab after fewer spaces than the indent (`<U+2029>` + one space + tab under an indent of 2); `succinctly yq` read all of them as scalar content. A tab after at least the indent's worth of spaces, or one that does not follow such a break, is still content. Checked against yq v4.53.3 on a grid of 4,608 documents (four hosts, `|`/`>`/`|-`/`>+`, three breaks, 0-5 spaces), 1,344 of them refused, with no difference in which. The same breaks still do not end the line for dedent, indent detection or the strip of the spaces after them (#4913). The check rides on the walk over a block scalar's lines, which now goes eight bytes at a time and notes a tab on the way, so a scalar without a tab costs nothing extra and the walk itself is faster: a 24 MB document made entirely of block scalars indexes 1.02-1.03x faster on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X and 1.10-1.12x faster on an Apple M4, and 0.93x / 0.99x when every scalar also holds tabs and U+2014 (min of 9, interleaved).
