- **jq: more constructor fold steps that extend part of their state are linear** (#6549).
  After #6538, a step that rebuilt its state with a constructor was linear only for a field or index one
  step down. It now also extends a slot further down, as in `{a: {b: (.a.b + [$i])}}` (nested or not),
  through a `+` chain such as `.a + [$i] + [0]`, and beside a member that counts it, such as
  `{a: (.a + [$i]), n: (.a | length)}`. `length` of a container in a fold step's operand no longer
  re-indexes the state either. On an Apple M5 these folds took 0.9-1.6 s at 8,000 steps and now take
  8-13 ms (jq 1.7.1: 51-110 ms); at 32,000 steps they take 23-40 ms, where jq takes 0.73-1.7 s.
