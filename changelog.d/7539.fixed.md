- **The NEON and SVE2 JSON index builders agree with the portable one when a `"` follows a bare value** (#7539). On aarch64 they took the quote in `a"` or `[1"a"]` as the start of a new string, so the semi-index of such malformed text (and every position after it) differed from the one x86_64 and the portable PFSM builder produce; the quote now ends the value and opens nothing, as the state machine says. `tests/json_builder_differential_properties.rs` checks every builder the target compiles (scalar, PFSM, dispatcher, SSE2/AVX2 or NEON/SVE2, Standard and Simple Cursor) against the portable one over every short byte combination at each chunk boundary, byte soups, and generated and mutated documents. No `jq` output difference was found before the fix.
