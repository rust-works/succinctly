- **yq: `(.a | key) head_comment = "x"` and the other comment slots address a mapping key's own comments** (#2796, part 4). `(.b | key) head_comment = "x"` on `a: 1\nb: 2` raised `head_comment = ... is not yet supported` (and `(.a | key) line_comment = "x"` was skipped without a trace); they now print `a: 1\n# x\nb: 2` and `a: 1 # x`, as yq v4.53.3 does. This is the way to put a comment above a key: the head goes above the key and replaces the lines already there (`# mid` above `b:` is `b`'s head), the foot goes below the entry, the line comment on the key's line, and `comments` sets all three. A foot or line comment already on the entry, a line comment on a mapping or sequence value, anything under a flow collection, `style`/`anchor` on a key and a write to several targets still raise `not yet supported`. Checked by 11 pinned-yq goldens and a differential fuzz of about 7,200 accepted writes over random commented documents, none differing.
