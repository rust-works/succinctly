- **jq: a fold that writes an array slot by a negative index, or by one that pads, is linear** (#7563, #7452).
  `reduce range(1;n) as $i ([range(n)]; .[-$i] = $i)` (and `|=`, `+=`, `//=`, a constant `.[-1]`, inside
  `foreach`), and a fold that grows null-padded rows such as `reduce range(n) as $t ([]; .[$t][799] = 1)` or
  `.[$t][$t] = 1`, re-indexed and rebuilt the whole accumulator on every step, so the time grew with the square
  of the step count where jq 1.7.1 is linear. Both indexes now take the in-place fold write, through the same
  `set_index` the evaluator writes with, and a `-$i` key no longer evaluates on a rebuilt copy of the state; an
  index still negative after counting back from the end keeps jq's `Out of bounds negative array index`, and the
  answers are unchanged.
