- **jq: `capture` and `scan` keep a capture group that did not take part in the match, as `null`** (#5363). `"b" | capture("(?<x>a)?(?<y>b)")` was `{"y":"b"}` where jq gives `{"x":null,"y":"b"}`, and `"a" | [scan("(a)(b)?")]` was `[["a"]]` where jq gives `[["a",null]]`: both silently dropped the group, so a position in `scan`'s array and a key in `capture`'s object went missing, in the first-match and the `"g"` forms alike. The object `sub`/`gsub` evaluate their replacement against now has the key too (`has("y")` is `true`). `match` and `test` already agreed.
