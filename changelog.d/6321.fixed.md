- **yq: a bare `:` after an explicit key is a null value, not a missing one** (#6321). `printf '? a\n:\n'` printed `{}` where yq has `{"a":null}`, and with another entry after it the key was paired with that entry's key: `? a⏎:⏎? b⏎: 1` was `{"a":"b"}` (yq `{"a":null,"b":1}`), `x: 1⏎? a⏎:⏎y: 2` was `{"x":1,"a":"y"}`, `- ? a⏎  :⏎- 2` lost its key, and `m:⏎  ? a⏎  :⏎  b: 1` was `{"m":{"a":"b"}}`. The colon with a property on it (`: &y`, `: !!str`) already wrote its null node; the bare one now does too. A value on the next lines, a sequence at the colon's indent, a comment line between, and a `:` followed by text are unchanged. 40 documents are pinned against yq v4.53.3.
