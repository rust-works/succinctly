- **yq: `key`, `path` and `parent` read once per member of a wide YAML mapping or sequence are linear, not quadratic** (#2784). `[.[] | key | path] | length` over a flat `kN: N` mapping took 2.84 s at 5k keys and 200 s at 40k (4x per doubling); it takes 0.02 s and 0.16 s (2x per doubling). The scan that finds a member in its parent resumes from the member the last scan found, as JSON's has since #3702/#3839; YAML opted out because block-sequence items are unwrapped from their `-` nodes, which `YamlCursor::next_element` now steps over the way `uncons_cursor` does. Interleaved A/B on an M4 Pro and a Ryzen 9 7950X, output identical on every row: mapping and sequence (items on the `-` line, on the next line, and mixed) of 2,000/4,000/8,000 members, `-96.0%` to `-99.8%`; the walk-route holdouts (`key | line`, `.`, `[.[]] | length` at 100,000 members) read -0.33% and +0.73%, with retired instructions unchanged to 0.02-0.11%. `parent | length` stays quadratic, which is #2574.
