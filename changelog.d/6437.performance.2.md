- **`-R 'fromjson | ...'` parses each line in place** (#6437). Each raw line was written out as JSON text, indexed, and decoded back out of the index before `fromjson` parsed it, about a fifth of the instructions of the run. A line that parses now goes straight to the parser; one that does not still takes the old route, which words the error. On a 7950X (jq 1.7.1, interleaved, identical output) `ndjson_raw_fromjson` at 10 MB goes from 149.7 ms (0.89x of jq) to 95.8 ms (1.40x), at 1 MB from 16.6 ms (0.91x) to 11.0 ms (1.37x).
