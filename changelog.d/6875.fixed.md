- **DSV: a delimiter that ends the text leaves an empty field after it** (#6875). `a,b,` read as `["a","b"]` and `1,` (the last row of a file with no final newline) as `["1"]` where Python's `csv` reads `['a','b','']` and `['1','']`, and the same text with a line break after it was right (`["a","b",""]`), so a file read differently with and without its final newline, with no error. The field iterator (`DsvRow::fields`) and `DsvRow::get` stopped at the end of the text after moving past the delimiter, so nothing was read there; they now read the empty field when the delimiter is the last byte, a marker (one inside a quote is not) and not a newline. Checked against Python's `csv` on 13,000 random tables (comma, tab, semicolon and pipe, LF and CRLF terminators, both quoting modes, with and without a final line break): the old build differed on 17 of 4,000 and the new one on none.
