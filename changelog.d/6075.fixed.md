- **yq: a single-quoted scalar with an empty line is written across lines when it sits in a nested flow collection or is a mapping key** (#6075). `printf "a: [['x\n\n  y']]\n" | yq .` keeps the quotes and indents the continuation by the scalar's depth (`a: [['x`, an empty line, `      y']]`); `succinctly yq` wrote `y']]` at the start of its line, which a reader folds to a space. A mapping key holding the break is `? 'k`, an empty line, `  z'` with the `:` on a line of its own, where `succinctly yq` wrote `'k` and `z': 1` on two lines. Both the streaming and the write route now match yq v4.53.3 on the documents pinned in `tests/yq_cli/i6075.rs`.
