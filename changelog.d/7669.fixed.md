- **`succinctly jq` reads a lazy fixed interval `x{n}?` as exactly `n` repeats on every regex route** (#7669). `"a" | test("(?>a){2}?a")` is `false` and `"aa" | [match("(?>a){2}??") | .length]` is `[2]`, as in jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2, where they were `true` and `[0]`: on a pattern with an atomic group, a look-around, a backreference or the `l` flag, the second engine read the `?` as another quantifier, Ruby's `(?:x{n})?`, where jq's Perl_NT syntax makes it the reluctant (and so still fixed) `x{n}`. A quantifier after it now repeats `x{n}` as in jq (`x{n}?+` is `(?:x{n})+`).
