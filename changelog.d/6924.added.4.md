- **yq: `-o kyaml`** (#6924, slice 2 of #6850).
  The results are written as KYAML, YAML's flow subset, by a port of yq's KYAML encoder: one entry per line with a trailing comma, bare or double-quoted keys, `-I` spaces of indent and `---` between documents (`-N` removes them).
  Checked against yq v4.53.3 over 3,500 random JSON documents (no difference); comments and an opening `---` are not read yet and are listed in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
