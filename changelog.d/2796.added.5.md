- **yq: `.a head_comment = "x"` on a mapping or sequence value prints the head inside it, as yq does** (#2796, part 5). On `a:\n  b: 1\ns: 2`, `.a head_comment = "x"` raised `head_comment = ... is not yet supported`; it now prints `a:\n  # x\n  b: 1\ns: 2` (above the first entry, at its indent), and `# x\n- a: 1` for a sequence item. A head the first entry already owns wins in yq, so that case is refused rather than guessed. A container's foot and line comment, an empty collection and a flow one still raise `not yet supported`. Checked by 5 pinned-yq goldens and a differential fuzz of about 1,300 accepted writes over random commented documents, none differing.
