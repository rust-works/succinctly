- **yq: `@uri` writes a space as `+` and `@urid` reads `+` as a space** (#4204). yq's `@uri` is Go's `url.QueryEscape` and `@urid` is `url.QueryUnescape`; `succinctly yq` followed jq (`%20`, and a literal `+`), so `"hello world" | @uri` was `"hello%20world"` where yq v4.53.3 prints `"hello+world"`, and `"a+b" | @urid` was `"a+b"` where yq prints `"a b"`. A literal `+` still encodes as `%2B`, `%20` still decodes to a space, and jq mode is unchanged.
