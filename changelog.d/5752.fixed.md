- **jq: `strptime`'s `%b`, `%B` and `%h` take a month's full name or its three-letter abbreviation, and no more** (#5752). The parser read every letter that followed and accepted any word that began with a month's first three, so `"05 Mart 2015" | strptime("%d %B %Y")` was March where jq 1.7.1 rejects it (`date ... does not match format`); `Marx`, `Marc`, `Marchx`, `Septembe` and `Sept` were accepted the same way, while `Ma` was rejected. The input now matches a full month name if it starts with one and its abbreviation otherwise, in any letter case, and the letters after the match belong to the rest of the format, so they have to match it. `05Mar2015` with `%d%b%Y` and `05 March 2015` with `%d %b %Y` still parse. Across 495 name, format and input combinations (every month's full and abbreviated spelling in three cases, one- and two-letter prefixes, trailing letters, digits and punctuation, and eleven format shapes) the rows differing from jq 1.7.1 went from 199 to 23, none introduced; the 23 left are the weekday of a format that sets no date (#5753) and `Mar-`/`Mar 2015` before a `%Y` with no separator, where jq's libc hands back the unparsed rest of the text.
