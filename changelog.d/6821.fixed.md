- **yq: a `$name` stage after a fan-out answers its value once** (#6821). yq's pipe evaluates its right side once over the whole list the left side produced, and `GET_VARIABLE` returns the variable's stored list, so `.n as $v | .x[] | $v` is one `7`, `.x | map($v)` is `[7]`, `.x[] | select(. > 5) | $v` is one `7` from an empty context, and every stage after the variable sees that one node; `succinctly yq` read the variable once per element (`7 7 7`, `[7,7,7]`, nothing). A `$name` that is not a stage (`[$v]`, `{"k": $v}`, `. + $v`, the body of an `as`) is read per node in yq too and is unchanged. 52 rows captured from yq v4.53.3, including `map` over a sequence, a mapping, a scalar, `null` and an empty collection, and `path`, `key` and `parent` after the variable, which also match now. A `$name` as a branch of a `,` after a fan-out (`.x[] | (5, $v)`) is still read per node. jq mode is unchanged.
