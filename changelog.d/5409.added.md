- **`succinctly dev compare jq`** (#5409). Phase 1 of a reproducible jq vs `succinctly jq` comparison: a sha256-pinned reference manifest (`comparison/jq/manifest.toml`), a hermetic source build of jq 1.7.1 (`scripts/compare-jq-build.sh`), an interleaved timer gated on output identity and exit status, and generated `env.json`, `results.json` and `report.md`. Dirty-tree, battery and wrong-build runs are marked invalid. Requires the `bench-runner` feature; a 20-query smoke suite. Phase 2 adds dimension A (all 218 builtins jq 1.7.1 lists, plus the 9 new in 1.8.1 against 1.8.1: 98.1% of the 207 probed agree on output) and G (136 CLI probes, 127 agree), checked against a known-divergence ledger (`comparison/jq/divergences.toml`) that fails the run on an unlisted or stale entry; the coverage figure in `docs/reference/jq-language.md` is now generated. Phase 3 adds B (jq's own `jq.test`, `onig.test`, `man.test` and `optional.test`, vendored and sha256-pinned: 711 of 714 cases agree) and C (JSONTestSuite against jq, `succinctly jq` and `--validate`: `--validate` accepts all 95 `y_` and rejects all 188 `n_`; jq itself accepts 25 of the `n_` cases). Phase 4 adds D and E: 43 queries over six pinned, generated input shapes at 1, 10 and 100 MB, timed against jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.1 with the three tools interleaved and the order rotated each repetition, gated on output identity, with CV flags, peak RSS from separate untimed runs, `--control` (jq against itself) for the noise floor and `--query` to run one row. On an M4 Pro the geomean speedup over jq 1.7.1 is 1.9x at 1 MB, 2.9x at 10 MB and 4.6x at 100 MB, and jq is faster on 9 to 11 rows per tier. Phase 5 adds F: 85 robustness probes (nesting, recursion, runaway generators, pathological regex, 10 MB strings, million-key objects, SIGPIPE) classed as ok, error, crash or timeout; 63 agree with jq, and a crash or hang of succinctly is listed even when jq does the same. Phase 6 adds H (binary size, libraries, dependency count, parallelism, licences), `--verify results.json` (reruns the deterministic dimensions and spot-checks timing rows, failing on a changed row or a speedup outside a noise-scaled tolerance), `--write-docs` (generates `docs/compare/jq.md` and a per-platform report and results.json), and a `compare-jq.yml` workflow for x86_64 and ARM64 Linux.
