- **yq: `-0` writes the NUL in place of the line break a comment-only or empty document ends in** (#5721). `printf '# c\n' | yq -0 .` printed `# c`, a line break and no NUL, and `select`, `(.)` and `..` printed the line break and then the NUL; an empty input printed a line break. yq writes `# c<NUL>` (a lone NUL for an empty input, `---<NUL>` for a bare `---`); `succinctly yq` now does, on the streamed and the `-P` routes, and a result that follows it in the same document (`select(. == null), 1`) still gets its own NUL. With `--colors` a comment's span keeps its break (`ESC[90m# c<LF>ESC[0m<NUL>`), as yq's does.
