- **yq: a negative index of `null` is out of range** (#4275). yq treats `null` as an empty array for a negative index, so `null | .[-1]`, `{"a": null} | .a[-1]` and `null | .[0 - 1]` raise `index [-1] out of range, array size is 0` (not suppressed by `?`, `//` or `[...]`), exactly as `[] | .[-1]` already did here; `succinctly yq` answered `null`. A non-negative index of `null`, a negative index of `{}` and jq mode are unchanged.
