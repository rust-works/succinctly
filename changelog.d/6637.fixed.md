- **jq: `--argjson` and `--jsonargs` read a value as deep as jq reads it** (#6637). A text of 129 to 256 nested arrays was refused (`invalid JSON text passed to --argjson`), because the validator they went through stopped at 128 containers; jq's ceiling is its parser's stack of 256 entries. They now read up to that, and an object, which takes two entries (itself and its key), is still refused at 129 as jq refuses it, with a mix of the two between. Over 110 runs (arrays, objects, a mix, empty containers and arrays holding objects, at 11 depths from 100 to 258, for both flags) 18 of 66 sampled on `main` differed from jq 1.7.1, and none do now.
