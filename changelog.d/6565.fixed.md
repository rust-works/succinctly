- **yq: a dash with properties after an explicit key's `:` or `?` owns the block scalar below it, as a dash after another dash does** (#6565). `? a` / `: - &x` / `  |` / `    t` is `{"a":["t\n"]}` in yq v4.53.3 and was `{"a":[null,"t\n"]}` here: the header at the dash's own column read as a second item, because the marker walk that finds the line above's owner (`deferred_value_owner`) stopped at the `:`. With a line between the dash and the content (`: - &x` / `  |` / `    t` / `` / `   u`) the document is refused by yq and was accepted here, the line dropped (#6550's shape, after a `:` or `?`). The walk now takes `-`, `?` and `:` markers, and counts a dash as the owner only when it is the last marker, so `- ?` is still owned by its `?`. A differential of 4,080 documents (an explicit key's `:` or `?` before a dash, a chain, a tag or a bare dash, under a mapping and a sequence; `|` and `>-` headers at columns 0 to 7; a line below at columns 0 to 7) against yq v4.53.3 found 880 differences before and 196 after, none where yq accepts a document and `succinctly yq` refuses it, all of the 196 in `? - &x` (the dash on the `?` line, parsed inside the key, #6570). The grids of #6540, #6543, #6550 and #6553 are unchanged.
