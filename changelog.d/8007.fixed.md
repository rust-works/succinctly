- **yq: `now` reads the clock once for its whole context, with no document, as yq does** (#8007). yq's `nowOp` returns one new node for the nodes it is given, however many there are and even when there are none, as `env(NAME)` does since #7996. `[.[] | now] | length` over a three-member sequence was `3` where yq v4.53.3 answers `1`, `select(false) | now` printed nothing where yq prints one timestamp, two documents printed `---` between their two values, and `--eval-all` printed one value per document where yq prints one. A write's right side reads it once too (`.x = ([.x[] | now] | length)` writes `1`). The `!!timestamp` tag yq gives the value is not reproduced: `now | type` is still `!!str`. `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
