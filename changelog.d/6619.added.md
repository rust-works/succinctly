- **`json generate` makes a `--seq` stream with one truncated record: `json-seq-bad-first` and `json-seq-bad-last`** (#6619).
  Both are the `json-seq` pattern's records plus one cut-off record before the first or after the last, so `jq --seq`
  runs behind a `ignoring parse error` diagnostic. `--verify` checks that exactly that one line is not JSON. The jq
  comparison (`comparison/jq`) gains pinned inputs for them and two rows, `seq_field_bad_first` and
  `seq_field_bad_last`, and the perf guard gains `seq_name_bad_last`: until now every `--seq` row read a clean stream.
