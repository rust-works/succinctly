- **jq: `strptime` starts from jq's zeroed `struct tm`** (#5340). A field the format leaves unset was the epoch's 1970-01-01 (`"10:15" | strptime("%H:%M")` was `[1970,0,1,10,15,0,4,0]`); jq reads it from a cleared `struct tm`, which is 1900-01-00 (`[1900,0,0,10,15,0,...]`), and `"2015-03" | strptime("%Y-%m")` is `[2015,2,0,0,0,0,6,58]`, as in jq 1.7.1. The weekday and yearday are computed from that date; for a format that sets no date they are the platform libc's in jq (glibc `8`/`367`, the macOS pin `6`/`-1`), which this does not call, recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
