- **yq: a plain scalar's continuation line drops every blank in front of its text** (#6209).
  `a: [x⏎ ⇥ y]` (a space, a tab, a space) folded to `x  y` with two spaces; go-yaml drops all the blanks of a continuation line, spaces after a tab included, and reads `x y`.
  The same held for `[x⏎⇥ y]`, a tab and spaces in block context, and a blank line followed by such a line (`x⏎⏎ ⇥ y` kept a space).
  A scalar that starts on a `---` line folds differently in yq (the first break is dropped); that is #6276 and not changed here.
