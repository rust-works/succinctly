- **yq: `recurse(.[]?; cond)` walks cursors instead of materializing the document** (#4095). Under `--jq-extensions`, for a `cond` that yields at most one value, `succinctly yq` now takes the cursor walk jq mode took since #3867, where it built the whole alias-expanded document as an owned tree first. `first(recurse(.[]?; true))` over a 100 MB `users` document is 75.6% faster on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X and 73.6% on an Apple M4 Pro; collecting every node is 42.5% and 31.0% faster; a `length > 0` condition is 34.8% and 28.5% faster. A `cond` made only of `type` tests is 3.8% faster on the 7950X and 10.6% slower on the M4 Pro at the same size (+11.2% to +11.9% at 1 and 10 MB). Over a 26-level alias ladder (2^26 leaves once expanded), `.root | first(recurse(.[]?; true))` takes 3.7 ms on the M4 Pro and 1.3 ms on the 7950X where it took 16.2 s and 38.4 s (and over 8 GB). Interleaved A/B against `c31f900b8`, output identical on every row.
