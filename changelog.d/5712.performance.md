- **jq: a string's `length` and `startswith`/`endswith` are answered from the string when they are composed too** (#5712, #5691 follow-up).
  #5710 answered them only as a lone last stage. A leading `length` that a later stage runs on (`$s|length|tostring`), a `select` condition (`select(length>3)`) and an `until`/`while` condition now take the same route instead of writing the string out and indexing it again once per crossing. On a 6.6 MB `--rawfile` on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X: `$s|length|tostring` 33 ms to 13 ms, `$s|select(length>3)|length` 44 ms to 13 ms, `$s|startswith("a")|not` 32 ms to 12 ms. `"" | until(length >= N; . + "x") | length` was quadratic (N+2 reindexes: 271 ms at N=20,000, 1,054 ms at N=40,000) and is now linear (8.4 ms and 25 ms). Output is identical in both modes. Measured on one machine (x86_64); ARM64 was not. Still through the bridge: a comparison of the length (`$s|length>5`), `try length`, and a `length` inside a per-element `range` body.
