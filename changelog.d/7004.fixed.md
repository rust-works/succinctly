- **jq: `strftime`, `strflocaltime`, `todate` and `todateiso8601` of a timestamp just below zero print the second jq does** (#7004).
  jq formats a number through the array `gmtime` (or `localtime`) returns, whose seconds are `tm_sec + (t - floor(t))`, and for `-1e-17` that sum rounds to `1.0`: `-1e-17 | todate` is `"1970-01-01T00:00:01Z"` in jq, and `succinctly jq` printed `"1970-01-01T00:00:00Z"` although its own `-1e-17 | gmtime | todate` already printed `01`.
  Only a timestamp within about `1.1e-16` below zero is affected.
