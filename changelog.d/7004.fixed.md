- **jq: `strftime`, `strflocaltime`, `todate` and `todateiso8601` of a negative timestamp just below a whole second print the second jq does** (#7004).
  jq formats a number through the array `gmtime` (or `localtime`) returns, whose seconds are `tm_sec + (t - floor(t))`, and for a negative timestamp a hair below a whole second that sum rounds up to the next integer: `-1e-17 | todate` is `"1970-01-01T00:00:01Z"` and `-1.0000000000000002 | todate` is `"1969-12-31T23:59:60Z"` in jq, where `succinctly jq` printed `"1970-01-01T00:00:00Z"` and `"1969-12-31T23:59:59Z"` although its own `gmtime` already held the seconds jq does.
