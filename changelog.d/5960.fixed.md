- **`strptime` reads `%z`, refuses `%p` after an hour past 12, and takes only C white space** (#5960). `%z` was skipped; it now reads a sign and four digits and, like `GMT`, makes the fields a UTC instant that becomes local time once its offset is taken off, so `"2015-03-05 10:00 +0500" | strptime("%F %R %z")` is 05:00 under `TZ=UTC`, and a later `%s` or `%Z` naming `GMT` drops the offset, as in jq. `%p` refuses an hour past 12 read before it (`"2015 13 PM" | strptime("%Y %H %p")`). White space in the format, before `%s` and at the end of the input is C's `isspace`, so other Unicode white space is text, as in jq.
