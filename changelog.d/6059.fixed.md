- **yq: a plain scalar in a flow collection that continues on a line starting with a tab is refused as in yq** (#6059). `a: [x⏎⇥y]` printed `a: [x y]`, where yq stops with `found a tab character that violates indentation`: go-yaml refuses a tab at a column below the enclosing block's indent plus one in the leading blanks of a continuation line (also a tab-only line, and one before a closing `]` or `,`). A collection at the document root (`[x⏎⇥y]`), a tab after enough spaces, and a tab that starts a new item after a `,` are still read, as in yq.
