- **jq: `-R` over several regular files reads them a window at a time** (#7024). `jq -R . a.log b.log` (up to 32 regular files, 4 MiB or more between them) read every file whole, where one file has been read a window at a time since #7023. Each file is now read a window at a time and in turn, as the one stream of lines jq reads: a file's last line without a line break runs on into the next file's first and is read in the later file, `input_line_number` counts each file's own lines, and each file's bytes are decoded on their own, as jq 1.7.1 does. At 100 MiB of NDJSON in 4 or 16 files peak RSS falls from 108.5-111.7 MiB to 11.7-14.7 on x86_64 and from 105.4-107.0 to 8.2-12.5 on aarch64, flat from 10 MiB; `-R .`, `-R length` and a `select` read 4.3% to 6.7% faster on x86_64 and within the control's range on aarch64, and `-nR 'reduce inputs as $l (0; .+1)'` 65% to 73% faster on both.
