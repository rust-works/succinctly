- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that is a pipe into a comma with a navigating sibling no longer drops a path, and no longer writes where jq raises** (#4059, a #4041 residual). `(foreach .a as $v (0; 1 | (not, ($v|.b?)); try ($v|.b?))) = 9` on `{"a":null,"z":{"b":1}}` raises `Cannot index number with string "b"` in jq (it names `["a","b"]` and `["a","b","b"]`, and the second write meets the number the first one wrote) and was `{"a":{"b":{"b":9}},"z":{"b":1}}` here, with `path(...)` answering only `[["a","b","b"]]`: the comma states jq's register per sibling, but only a comma at the head of UPDATE had that statement read, so behind a stage that cannot move the register (`1 | ...`, `length | ...`) the first sibling's register was lost. A pipe is now read through its last stage when every stage ahead of it cannot move the register. The same change stops a sibling that navigated and then computed (`$v|.b?|tostring`) from being taken for the fold's own register when their values are equal (`null`, `true`, `false`): `path(...)` over it moves from the wrong `[["a","b"],["a","b"]]` to `[["a","b"]]` (jq: `[["a","b"],["a","b","b"]]`), still short a path. A 20,000-program seeded grid of UPDATE shapes against jq 1.7.1 fixes 18 rows (the pipe-into-comma shapes) with no regression, and a 30,000-row sample of the register sweep over all 565 operands is identical to `main`.
