- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that is a pipe into a comma with a navigating sibling, or a sibling that navigates and then computes, no longer drops a path or writes where jq raises** (#4059, #4063; a #4041 residual). `(foreach .a as $v (0; 1 | (not, ($v|.b?)); try ($v|.b?))) = 9` on `{"a":null,"z":{"b":1}}` raises `Cannot index number with string "b"` in jq (it names `["a","b"]` and `["a","b","b"]`, and the second write meets the number the first one wrote) and was `{"a":{"b":{"b":9}},"z":{"b":1}}` here, with `path(...)` answering only `[["a","b","b"]]`. Two causes. The comma states jq's register per sibling, but only a comma at the head of UPDATE had that statement read, so behind a stage that cannot move the register (`1 | ...`, `length | ...`) the first sibling's register was lost; a pipe is now read through its last stage when every stage ahead of it cannot move the register. And a sibling that navigated and then computed (`$v|.b?|tostring`, `$v|.b?|not`) leaves jq's register on what it navigated to, which the fold took for its own register whenever the two values were equal (`null`, `true`, `false`) and so ran EXTRACT from the wrong node (`path(...)` answered `[["a","b"],["a","b"]]` where jq has `[["a","b"],["a","b","b"]]`); EXTRACT now runs from the node the register moved to. A seeded grid of UPDATE shapes against jq 1.7.1 fixes rows with no regression, and a sample of the register sweep over all 565 operands is identical to `main`. Not changed: a navigating pipe on the accumulator (`. | .b?`) and a trailing `| .` after the comma still skip a path over a `null` source, and the write that follows still lands where jq raises (#4063); a sibling jq's register paths of which this fold cannot read all of can now yield some of them instead of none.
