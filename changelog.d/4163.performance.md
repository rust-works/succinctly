- **jq: `nth($i)` before `key`/`path` no longer materializes the array on every read** (#4163). `nth(n)` is jq's `.[n]`, but the generic evaluator did not know that: a plain read went through the whole-input bridge, and a position read (`nth($i) | tostring | key`) left the cursor walk for the owned identity route, so 16,000 reads of a 16,000-element array took 113 s where `.[$i]` takes 0.83 s (`nth($i) | tostring | key` on 3,000 elements: 4.5 s to 0.03 s). `nth` now takes the computed-index arm of the evaluator and of the path-context walk. It also fixes an inconsistency: `[10,20,30] | nth(-1) | key` answered `-1` where `.[-1] | key` answers `2`, the resolved index. The slice shape `.[$i:][0]` is separate (a slice builds its tail, see #4195).
