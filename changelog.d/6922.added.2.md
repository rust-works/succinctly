- **yq: `-o=props` and `@props` write the comments of the document** (#6922).
  Above each property they write its key's and its value's head and line comments, as yq's properties encoder joins them (`# h` over `a: 1 # c` is `# h c`, a blank line before every commented property but the first); the first document's leading comment block comes first when the result is that document, and a mapping's or sequence's own comment is dropped, as in yq.
  A value the filter builds or writes (`{"z": .a}`, `.a = 5`) still prints without them.
  Checked against yq v4.53.3 on 2,700 random commented documents: every difference left is a comment that `head_comment` already attributes to another node than yq does, or a `---` between the results of two documents.
