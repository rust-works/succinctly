- **yq: an explicit indentation indicator on a block scalar header below a compact key with a property is measured from the key's mapping** (#6490). `printf -- '- k: &y\n  >2\n    t\n' | succinctly yq -o=json -I=0 .` printed `[{"k":"  t\n"}]` and yq v4.53.3 answers `[{"k":"t\n"}]`: the value side read the dash's column for `- k: &y` (its line does not end in the colon, so the key was not recognised as deferring its value), and only the first dash of `- - k:`. Over 400 documents (compact key with no property, `&y`, `!!str`, both, or a comment; five headers; four indents; the key under a mapping, behind two dashes or behind an anchored dash) every one that yq reads now gives its value here; a header at the column of a nested dash (`a:` / `  - - k:` / `    |`), which yq refuses and succinctly reads, is the one difference left (#6540).
