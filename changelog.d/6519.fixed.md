- **jq: a look-behind is sized as Oniguruma sizes it, so one that can match nothing sets no group and `(?<=(a)?)` is refused** (#6519). `"ab" | match("(?<=(a)|)b")` captured `{offset: 0, string: "a"}` where jq 1.7.1 captures `{offset: -1, string: null}`: a body that can match the empty string, and is sure of it, is no look-behind to Oniguruma. A group or an assertion makes the length unsure, so `(?<=(a)?)b`, `(?<=(a)*)b`, `(?<=b|()|c)\z` and `(?<=bb?)\z` are refused with `invalid pattern in look-behind`, `(?<=b|c{0})\z` is answered, and `(?<=a*b*)b` is `undefined error code`, all as in jq. The quantifier reduction that comes first (`x+` is `x`, `x?` and `x*` are removed, `a*b` is made possessive, an exact count of a string is the longer string) is read too, which settles `(?<=(?:a|b)+)`, `(?<=a*bc)`, `(?<=a*a)` and the look-behind bodies a generator draws that this build answered differently before (1,717 of 10,000 generated patterns differ from jq before, 86 now, none the other way). A negative look-behind whose body can match nothing never matches, as in jq (`"a" | test("(?<!(?:a|\\b[ab])?)")` was `true`). Pinned over 55 rows against jq 1.7.1 in `tests/jq_cli/i6519.rs`.
