- **`succinctly yq` reads `$ENV` and `$ARGS` as unbound variables without `--jq-extensions`** (#7616). yq has neither, so a filter that reads one yields nothing, as `$foo` does: `$ENV.FOO` prints nothing and `[$ENV] | length` is `0`, where they were jq's environment and arguments objects, and `--security-disable-env-ops` no longer refuses `$ENV.FOO`. A program that binds the name (`1 as $ENV | $ENV`) reads its binding, and after a fan-out answers it once, as yq does. Under `--jq-extensions` both keep jq's meaning, and `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
