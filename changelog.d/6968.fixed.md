- **yq: a `#` directly after a closing quote starts a comment in block context** (#6968). `a: "x"#c`, `- 'x'#c`, `a: &x "v"#c` and `? "x"#c` were a parse error (`mapping values are not allowed in this context`), where yq reads the comment, and prints it back (`a: "x" #c`). The check that a value is the last thing on its line took a `#` for a comment only after a blank, and the quote that precedes it is not one. After a plain scalar the `#` is still content (`a: x#c` is `x#c`), and a comment between a quoted key and its colon is still refused. Found by a mutation fuzz of the yaml-test-suite inputs against yq v4.53.3.
