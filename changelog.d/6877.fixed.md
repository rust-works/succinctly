- **yq: `sort`, `sort_by`, `reverse`, `unique` and `unique_by` of a flow-style sequence keep its flow style and comments** (#6877). `.l | sort` over `l: [3, 1, 2, 3] # c` printed the items as a block sequence without the comment, where yq reorders the node in place and prints `[1, 2, 3, 3] # c`; the same held at the document root, behind any static path, and for the quoting of the items (`['a', "b", c]`) and an `&anchor`. The write form (`.l |= sort`) was already right. A read of this shape over a flow-style sequence is now answered through it, `(.l |= sort) | .l`, for YAML output (36 rows pinned against yq v4.53.3 in `tests/yq_cli/i6877.rs`, among them other documents, `. | sort`, `-N`, `-I=4`, `-r` and `-P`); a block-style sequence, a reorder that is only part of the read (`[.l | sort]`, `.a | sort, .b | sort`) and `-i` are unchanged. Any document of the input holding such a sequence sends the whole input to the DOM route.
