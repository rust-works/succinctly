- **jq: a scalar crossing the owned-evaluator bridge reuses its index buffers** (#4217).
  After #4154 a scalar `ReindexedDoc` still allocated its text and three index buffers (the
  interest bits, their rank and the leaf's parentheses word) and freed them a moment later,
  once per scalar a query bridges. A dropped scalar document now hands those buffers to the
  next one on the thread, so a crossing in steady state allocates only its text: 3 fewer
  allocator calls per member on every row (`[.[] | del(.)]` over 2,000 integers: 16,022 ->
  10,022; strings 20,022 -> 14,022; booleans and nulls 12,022 -> 6,022). Each live document
  keeps its own index, so `document_token` still tells two of them apart.
  Interleaved A/B, min of 15, output identical: `[.[] | del(.)]`, `paths(.)` and
  `del(.[]?)` over 300k-1M integers -6.0% to -9.1% on a Ryzen 9 7950X and -5.0% to -6.8% on
  an M4 Pro, 300k strings -7.6% to -8.4% / -4.4% to -7.5%; a container-rooted control
  (`[.[] | del(.a)]` over 100k objects) -0.2% / -0.2%, within noise.
