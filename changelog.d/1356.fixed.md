- **yq: `tonumber` of an integer string keeps its spelling** (#1356).
  `"0x10" | tonumber`, `"007"`, `"+5"`, `"0X10"`, `"0o17"` and `"1_000"` print as written through `tostring`, interpolation and YAML output and keep their base under arithmetic (`"0x10" | tonumber | . + 1` is `0x11`), as yq does; `-o=json` still prints the number, and the strings yq refuses (`"-0x10"`, `" 0x10"`, `"0x"`, `"0o9"`) are still refused.
  A nested `_1` carries the `!!int` tag yq gives it.
  The float spellings (`"+2.0"`, `"2."`, `".5"`) are not part of this change.
