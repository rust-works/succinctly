- **jq: `--stream -s` gives jq's events and jq's parse error text** (#5498, slice 5).
  `-s` read its events from a parsed document, the last route that did. It now reads jq's streaming parser, as the plain walk and `-n` do, so a repeated key is emitted every time (`printf '{"a":1,"a":2}' | jq --stream -sc .` prints `[[["a"],1],[["a"],2]]` and `[["a"]]`, where `succinctly jq` printed `[[["a"],2]]`), and a fault is jq's own text and position: `1 2 }` prints `jq: parse error: Unmatched '}' at the top-level at line 1, column 5`, not `Invalid JSON text`.
  A matrix of 6,820 runs (22 streams plus 10 shapes, 11 programs, 10 flag sets, one and two files) against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 changes 597 rows from different to identical and none the other way; 1,311 rows over stdin are pinned in `tests/jq_cli/i5498_slurp.rs`.
  With every route on the reader, the eager expansion (`expand_into_stream_events`, the up-front `tostream` walk, the re-read of a malformed document for its events and `DocumentForm::StreamEvents`) has no caller and is gone.
