- **`succinctly yq` folds U+0085, U+2028 and U+2029 inside a quoted or flow scalar as yq does** (#4913). yq's scanner reads all three as line breaks: U+0085 folds like a `\n` (`a: 'x<U+0085>y'` is `x y`, it stayed `x<U+0085>y`), U+2028 and U+2029 stay in the value, and the blanks beside any of them are trimmed (`'x <U+2028> y'` is `x<U+2028>y`, it kept both blanks and was double-quoted on YAML output). The single-quoted, double-quoted and plain decoders share one rule, and a body without any of the three bytes pays one `memchr` pair more than before. 3,000 random scalars in all three styles agree with yq v4.53.3 in JSON mode.
