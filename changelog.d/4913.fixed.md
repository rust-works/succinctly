- **`succinctly yq` folds U+0085, U+2028 and U+2029 inside a quoted or flow scalar as yq does** (#4913). yq's scanner reads all three as line breaks: U+0085 folds like a `\n` (`a: 'x<U+0085>y'` is `x y`, it stayed `x<U+0085>y`), U+2028 and U+2029 stay in the value, and the blanks beside any of them are trimmed (`'x <U+2028> y'` is `x<U+2028>y`, it kept both blanks and was double-quoted on YAML output). The single-quoted, double-quoted and plain decoders share one rule, and 3,000 random scalars in all three styles agree with yq v4.53.3 in JSON mode. Every reader's "does this scalar need decoding" test, three `contains` passes, is now one eight-bytes-at-a-time pass that also looks for the three characters, so the change does not cost a document without them anything: interleaved against the base on strings, unicode, comprehensive and flow YAML at 1, 10 and 50 MB, `-o json` read -2.0% (7950X) and -2.5% (M4 Pro) and `-o yaml` -1.3% and -3.9%, as medians of medians. Still open: a nested flow collection's, an explicit key's and a block plain scalar's break (see `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`).
