- **`succinctly jq` refuses a recursion whose outputs climb back through a heavy combiner instead of overflowing the native stack and aborting** (#7856). A combiner runs its other parts for each output of the call it holds, from inside that call, so the outputs of a recursion climb back up through every level on top of the frames of its descent, and only the descent checked the native-stack floor (ADR-0025): `def f($n): if $n <= 0 then (foreach (1,2) as $x (0; . + $x)) else {k: f($n - 1), v: (foreach (1,2) as $x (0; . + $x))} end; first(f(9000)) | length` aborted with `fatal runtime error: stack overflow` (exit 134) in a release build, where jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 print `2`. An output of a call to a def that may recurse now checks the stack on its way to its consumer, and a climb past three quarters of the evaluation stack is refused with `f/1 exceeded maximum recursion depth` (exit 5, uncatchable), as a descent is at half. A recursion whose climb takes it past three quarters of the stack without overflowing it is now refused where it answered: the issue's program answers to 6,680 levels on an Apple M4 Pro, where it answered to about 8,900 and aborted from 9,000.
