- **jq: refusing a deeply nested document is linear on the routes that read past a parse fault** (#7470).
  On `-n input`, `inputs` and `input_line_number`, a document past jq's parse-depth limit (`{"a":` 400,000
  levels deep, 2.4 MB) did work in the square of the depth where jq 1.7.1's is linear: after each fault jq drops the
  rest of its read and parses on from the next, and every such read that opened another refused container was
  split, indexed and validated to the end of the text, which made the refusal quadratic in the depth. A read
  past a fault is now parsed only through the bracket jq refuses: doubling the depth now doubles the work where
  it quadrupled it, and the output is unchanged.
