- **jq: a loop beside a lookaround, backreference or `\K` ends at its empty iteration, as Oniguruma's does** (#6128). A pattern with syntax only `fancy-regex` reads used to keep its loops as written: `"ba" | match("(?:b*|a)+(?=)")` was `"ba"` and is now jq's `"b"`. The same holds for a loop inside a lookaround or atomic group, and next to `\G`, `\K` or a `(?#...)` comment. A differential of 50,000 generated patterns with such syntax beside, around or inside a loop found 257 cases fixed and none broken (the cases left are listed in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`).
