- **yq: a key with no value, followed by a quoted or flow-collection key, keeps its null** (#6386). `a:` / `"b": 1` read `{"a": "b", "b": 1}` (and `a:` / `[x]: 1` read `{"a": [], "": 1}`): the empty value's node was written at the start of the *next* line, and a node is decoded from its first byte, so a quote or a bracket there made the key's text its value. It happened when the next line was at the key's indent or a dedent (`x:` / `  a:` / `"b": 1`), and the sequence-item form (`x:` / `  - a:` / `"b": 1`). The node now sits at its key's colon, where yq puts an empty value, so `.a | line` of `a:` is the key's line (it was the next line's) and its `column` is 3. A block scalar header at the key's own indent (`a:` / `|` / `  x`) is the key's value, as in yq, and is read as one now rather than by the accident of that position (`a:` / `|-` / `  x` / `b: 1` read `{"a": "x", "x": "b"}`).
