- **jq: a long chain of `E as $x |` bindings is linear** (#7471).
  Each bind substituted its value into a copy of the whole rest of the chain, read or not, and held the copy while
  the rest ran, so `1 as $x0 | 1 as $x1 | ... | $x0` cost memory and time in the square of the chain's length
  (4,000 bindings: 1,750 MiB peak RSS on an Apple M4 Pro), where jq 1.7.1 runs it in a few MB. A body that does
  not read the bound variable is now evaluated as it is, and a chain answers that question for all of its links in
  one walk: 4,000 bindings take 30 MiB (Apple M4 Pro), and doubling the chain doubles the work and the memory. A chain whose
  every variable is read at its end still copies the rest of the chain per bind, as before.
