- **jq: a fold step that is an `if` choosing `.` or a closed value is 1.9-3.0x faster** (#6569).
  A `reduce`, `foreach`, `until` or `while` step such as `if $x > . then $x else . end` (a running max)
  or `if C then 1 else 2 end` now answers from the chosen branch. Before, the whole `if` ran on the
  evaluator's general route every step. On an Apple M5 over 4,000,000 `reduce` steps, the running max takes
  743 ms instead of 1,550 ms (jq 1.7.1: 659 ms), and `if 3 > 5 then . else . end` takes 373 ms instead
  of 1,126 ms (jq: 448 ms).
