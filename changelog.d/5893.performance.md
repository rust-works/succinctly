- **jq: a constructor fold step that reads a scalar off the accumulator is linear too** (#5893).
  `[., length]`, `[., (.|type)]`, `{a: ., n: length}` and `{(.s): .}` as a fold's step used to take the
  re-index bridge every step, like `[.]` did before #5875: `reduce range(40000) as $i (0; [., (.|type)])`
  took 31.7 s. The members that read the accumulator are now settled before it moves into the new
  container, and that fold runs in 0.1 s (an Apple M-series chip, release build; 400,000 steps take
  0.14 s). A member that reads a container out of the accumulator (`[., .a]`) is still answered from a
  rebuilt copy and is still quadratic. Output is unchanged, byte for byte against jq 1.7.1 on the
  programs in `tests/jq_cli/i5893.rs`.
