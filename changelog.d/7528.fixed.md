- **jq: a regex may nest groups up to 511 deep** (#7528). `"a" | test("(" * 251 + "a" + ")" * 251)` was refused with the `regex` crate's own `regex parse error ... exceed the maximum number of nested parentheses/brackets (250)`, where both jq 1.7.1 and jq 1.8.2 answer it. `succinctly jq` now answers every pattern with up to 511 nested groups (capturing, `(?:`, with a repeat or an alternation between them, under any flags) and refuses 512 or more with jq 1.8.2's `Regex failure: parse depth limit over` (jq 1.8.1's oniguruma stack-overflow fix, GHSA-f946-j5j2-4w5m; jq 1.7.1 accepted up to 2,047). Look-arounds and atomic groups nested more than 63 deep are still refused, by the second regex engine's own limit.
