- **jq: a destructure of `.` behind `first(., X)`, `limit(1; ., X)` or `nth(0; ., X)`, as a `reduce` source in a moved `reduce` body, now raises as jq does** (#4667).
  `del(reduce .[]? as $i (.; reduce (first(., {a:1}) | . as {a:$a} | .) as $j (.; .)))` on `{"a":5}` is `Invalid path expression near attempt to access element "a"` in jq and printed `null` here, so `del` deleted the document and `=`/`|=` replaced it; `path(...)` of it answered `[[]]`.
  These wrappers emit their comma's first leaf only, which is `.`, so the destructure after them meets the accumulator, which is not the register once the outer source has moved it. The register walk now reads them as that `.`. A source with a `try`/`?` in it, one where the accumulator is the register (`reduce 1 as $i (...)`), and a fresh first leaf (`first({a:1}, .)`) are unchanged, as in jq.
