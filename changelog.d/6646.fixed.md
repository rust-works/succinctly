- **jq: `--seq --stream` and `--stream-errors` stop reporting open failures after a held-back closing event** (#6646). When the last file before a missing one ends in a container whose closer follows a scalar and has no byte after it (`\x1e[1]`, `\x1e{"a":1}`), jq's parser holds the closer's event until the next read, which is the open of the missing file, and its main loop ends there: a later missing file is not reported. succinctly reported all of them. Text where the closer follows a container, nothing, or has a byte after it, or that is not one well-formed value, keeps every failure. Where the closing event is placed (`input_filename`), and the programs that read `input` (`first(inputs)`), are not changed.
