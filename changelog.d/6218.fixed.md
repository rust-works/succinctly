- **jq: under the `n` flag a trailing possessive quantifier over a lazy atom answers as jq does** (#6218). `"cabb" | [match("(?:a??)*+"; "gn")]` printed the `a`; jq 1.7.1 finds nothing, because the possessive quantifier is the atomic group, which takes its empty way and fails the `n` flag's empty-match rejection (`a??+` is the reverse: jq finds `a`). A trailing possessive quantifier over an atom that can match nothing first (a lazy quantifier or an alternation inside it) keeps its atomic group under `n`, and an ungrouped `a??+` is written as `(?:a??)+`. Over a 14,280-case matrix the `n` answers match jq except `X?++`.
