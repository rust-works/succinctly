- **jq: a `--seq` file with one bad record is read a window at a time up to that record** (#7262).
  One regular file of 4 MiB or more (named, or redirected onto standard input) that holds a record
  other than `RS value LF` was read whole wherever that record stood. It is now read as a `--seq`
  pipe is (#7164): windowed up to its first record that is not plain, and only the rest is read
  whole. `--seq -r .name` over 100 MiB whose last record is cut short (`seq_field_bad_last`) went
  from 1073.7 to 750.3 ms (-30.1%) and 145.7 to 10.2 MiB peak RSS on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X, and
  from 997.5 to 693.8 ms (-30.4%) and 152.2 to 6.6 MiB on an Apple M4 Pro, now 1.39x and 1.72x
  of jq 1.7.1 (10 MiB: -28.8% and -29.3%). Output is identical. A bad first record still
  costs the whole file (`seq_field_bad_first`, unchanged), and so does a bad record in any of
  several files.
