- **jq: a `$` that something follows, and an escaped letter jq does not define, match as Oniguruma matches** (#6080). `"ab\n" | match("b$\\n")` is `[1,2]` in jq 1.7.1, because `$` matches before a final newline and leaves it for the next token; it found nothing here, and so did `$\n`, `b$\s` and `(?:b$|x)\n`. A pattern with a `$` that is not the last token of its branch or pattern is now read by the exact engine, as one with `\z` already was. `\g`, `\h`, `\i`, `\j`, `\k`, `\l`, `\m`, `\o`, `\q`, `\v` and the capitals `\C`, `\E`, `\F`, `\H`, `\I`, `\J`, `\L`, `\M`, `\T`, `\V` are the letter itself in jq (`"v" | test("\\v")` is `true`): they were a parse error here, and `\v` a vertical tab. `\g<n>` and `\k<n>` keep their meaning.
