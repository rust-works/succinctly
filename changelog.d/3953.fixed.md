- **jq: a `reduce` UPDATE that hands the accumulator back after a register destructure now answers in path position as jq does** (#3953, a #3940 residual). `path(reduce 1 as $k (.; (. as {a:$a} | .)))` refused (exit 5) where jq answers `[]`, as did `del()` (`null`) and `= 5` (a root write), and so did a `foreach` in UPDATE whose source destructures or navigates the register (`path(reduce 1 as $k (.; (foreach .[]? as $k (.; .; .))))` on `[true]`). jq backtracks a `reduce`'s source and UPDATE, so the register its final check reads is INIT's however far UPDATE navigated, and an UPDATE that emits the accumulator's own node leaves the accumulator at the register. The resolver now says so for a syntactically node-preserving UPDATE (`.`, a pipe of such, a single-pattern bind of `.` whose body is one, a `foreach` whose INIT, UPDATE and EXTRACT are no-ops over a source the resolver path-checks), inside an outermost `reduce` whose source, INIT and loop pattern cannot move the register; the #3853 refusal of a no-op bind body in a fold UPDATE is lifted only there. Unchanged, and still raising or refusing: a `reduce` whose own source navigated (`del(reduce .[]? as $k (.; foreach .[]? as $k (.; .; .)))` on `[true]` raises as in jq), a bind or navigating INIT ahead of a nested `foreach`, a source hidden under `first`/`limit`/`select`/`try`, a nested `foreach` that can yield nothing, a destructured `$var` handed back, a literal or navigated INIT, an array pattern on an object, and a `foreach` outer fold. The refused-where-jq-answers residuals are recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
