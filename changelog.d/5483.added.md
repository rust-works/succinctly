- **yq: `explode(.)`** (#5483). yq's `explode(.)` expands every alias into a copy of its anchor's node, drops the anchors and expands `<<` merges (`a: &x {p: 1}` / `b: *x` becomes `a: {p: 1}` / `b: {p: 1}`); `succinctly yq` answered `parse error: unexpected character '('`. It parses as `(.. | select(kind != "alias")) anchor = ""`, whose cleared anchors make the aliases that named them print as the nodes they stood for; 15 rows (plain, merge, merge list, nested, sequence, multi-document, override, `| .key`, `| keys`) captured from yq v4.53.3 match. Only `explode(.)` is accepted: a path other than `.` is a parse error rather than a wrong answer, `.a | explode(.)` is refused, and the flow style of an expanded alias copy is lost (#2651). jq mode's `explode` (string to codepoints) is unchanged.
