- **jq, yq: splitting an empty string has no pieces, and `/ ""` gives the characters alone** (#4260). `"" | split(",")` and `"" / ","` were `[""]` where jq and yq answer `[]`; `"abc" / ""` was `["","a","b","c",""]` where both answer `["a","b","c"]` (`"" / ""` was `["",""]`, `"s" / ""` was `["","s",""]`). `"abc" | split("")` and every non-empty separator over a non-empty string already agreed. One helper now serves `split(sep)` and string `/`, in both modes.
