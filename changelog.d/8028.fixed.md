- **yq: `with_dtf(LAYOUT; E)` over a once-per-context stage is answered once, as yq does** (#8028). yq's `with_dtf` evaluates `E` over the whole context it is given, so when `E` holds a stage yq answers once for its whole context (`now`, `env(NAME)`, `strenv(NAME)`, `{}`, a `$name`) the call is answered once too. `[.x[] | with_dtf("2006"; now)] | length` over `x: [1, 2, 3]` was `3` where yq v4.53.3 answers `1`, and so were `with_dtf("2006"; strenv(X))`, `with_dtf("2006"; now | tostring)` and `with_dtf("2006"; select(true) | now)`; `select(false) | with_dtf("2006"; now)` printed nothing where yq prints one value, two documents printed `---` between their values, `map(with_dtf("2006"; now))` held three, and a write's right side read it per node (`.y = [.x[] | with_dtf("2006"; strenv(X))]` now writes `[hi]`). Stages of the body before its once-stage still run over every node, so `[.x[] | with_dtf("2006"; split("x") | now)]` over integers raises `cannot split` as in yq. A body with no such stage (`with_dtf("2006"; .)`, `with_dtf("2006"; [now])`) is still answered per node, as in yq. `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
