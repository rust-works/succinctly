- **jq: an array's elements live in one refcounted block** (#7260).
  An array was an `Rc<Vec<_>>`: a refcount box and a separate element buffer, two allocations per array, which
  `tostream` and `paths` pay for every path they emit. The count, length and elements now share one block, and a
  large `Vec` becomes an array without being copied. At 10 MB of the `comprehensive` input, `[tostream]` peaks at
  368 MB instead of 440 MB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (jq 1.7.1: 359 MB) and 395 MB instead of 433 MB on an Apple M4
  Pro (jq: 362 MB), and at 100 MB below jq on the 7950X (3580 MB, jq: 3696 MB); `[paths]` peaks at 223 MB instead
  of 260 MB (jq: 271 MB) and 230 MB instead of 248 MB (jq: 292 MB), 16% and 12% faster. Library users: the guard `ArrayVecMut`
  that `ArrayOf::make_vec` returns no longer derefs to `Vec`; it has the `Vec` methods callers use and derefs to a slice.
