- **yq: `explode(f)` for any path** (#5483). yq's `explode(f)` expands every alias in the nodes `f` selects, and in their descendants, into a copy of its anchor's node, drops the anchors there and expands `<<` merges there, leaving aliases and anchors elsewhere; `succinctly yq` accepted only `explode(.)` and refused every other argument (`explode(.a)`, `explode(.[])`, `explode(..)`) with `explode(f) is only supported as explode(.)`. It now parses as `(f | ..) anchor = ""`, so a selected alias prints as its anchor's value, an alias nested under the selection whose anchor lies outside it is expanded, and the anchors under the selection are cleared; 6 rows over a selected alias, a nested one, several selected nodes and a merge, captured from yq v4.53.3, match. Where it differs: an alias outside the selection that named a cleared anchor is written as its value, where yq writes a `*name` with no anchor that it cannot read back, and every merge in the document is expanded, not only those under the selection.
