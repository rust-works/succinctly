- **jq: `-C`/`--color-output` streams its output instead of building it twice** (#5415).
  `succinctly jq -C` used to materialize each result as an owned tree, render it into one
  whole `String` and color that into a second, and it left the M2 streaming route. It now
  renders through the printers uncolored output uses and colors the text a block at a time on
  its way out. `-C .` on an 84 MB document peaks at 296 MB instead of 2635 MB on an Apple M5 Max,
  the same as uncolored `.`, and its first output arrives after 0.67 s instead of 3.46 s
  (wall-clock 1.09 s instead of 3.53 s); `-C -c .` peaks at 200 MB instead of 1755 MB. `-C -S`
  and `-C -a` still materialize the value, but color it in blocks: 595 MB instead of 2426 MB.
  The colored bytes are unchanged, as are the refusals: a document with an undecodable string
  or nesting past the limit in it, a NaN or `Infinity` word, or `--preserve-input` keeps the
  old render. One thing moves, on stderr only: a malformed `true`/`false`/`null` or number under
  `-C` now reports the message `-c` gives (`Invalid JSON text: invalid keyword 'tru' ...`) where
  it reported the materializer's (`invalid boolean`), still with exit 5 and empty stdout.
