- **`succinctly jq --seq` reads a NUL byte as jq 1.7.1 does** (#7340). jq measures an `fgets` chunk that holds no newline with `strlen`, so its parser never sees the chunk from the first NUL on, and reads a number token as a C string, so a NUL ends the number: `\x1e1\x00\n` is the value `1` (was a warning and no value), and in a 4095-byte chunk without a newline the bytes after a NUL are skipped (`7 [8]` after one were read as values), a value open across them included. The warning for a stream with no RS byte now stops its column at the NUL as well, and a NUL jq never sees no longer makes a byte-order-mark prefix malformed (`\xef\xbb\x00x` warned nothing; jq reports `Unfinished abandoned text at EOF`).
