- **jq: a character device as the lone input (`/dev/urandom`, a terminal that streams lines), named or on standard input, is read as a pipe is** (#7737).
  It was read to its end before the program ran, so `-R -n 'input|length' /dev/urandom`, `-c . < /dev/urandom` and `-c halt /dev/pts/N` over a terminal that kept streaming grew until killed, where jq 1.7.1 answers at once.
  Every route #7478 opened to pipes now takes any source that is not a regular file or a directory: the plain filter and `-R` read it a window at a time, and the lazy `input`/`inputs` start on its front; a terminal typed into still ends at its `^D` and is read whole as before.
