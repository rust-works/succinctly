- **yq: a write to the root's head comment replaces the first document's `---` marker and the comments around it** (#6903). yq's head comment of the first document's root is its whole preamble, so `... comments=""` (the usual way to strip comments), `. head_comment=""` and `. head_comment="hi"` over `---` / `# c` / `a: 1` print `a: 1` and `# hi` / `a: 1`, where `succinctly yq` kept the `---` and the comments before and after it (and added `# hi` after the marker). Later documents keep their `---` as separators. Found by a differential sweep of real YAML files (22 of 400, macOS `.dSYM` relocation files start with `---`).
