The refusal now stops the program (exit 5) as it already did without the `try`, for `reduce` and `foreach`, a literal or computed key, and `?`. Only a refusal of a step that could have succeeded is loud; one jq raises whatever its register is stays catchable. A try-wrapped fold whose catch happened to agree with jq because a later alternative also fails (`del(try (.a and (reduce . as {a:$a,b:$b} ?// [$a] (0; .))))` on `{"a":1,"b":2}`) now refuses too: over the nine fold-pattern operands of the path-register sweep (119,907 rows) wrong answers fell from 126 to 14 (silent) and 32 to 10 (different output), at 118 more refusals.
