- **jq: a `try` or `?` around a fold no longer swallows the pattern walk's guessed refusal** (#3840, a #3743 residual). `del(try (.a and (reduce . as {a:$a} ?// $a (0; .))))` on `{"a":true}` is `{}` in jq, which finds the first alternative intact and deletes `.a`; the walk's guess at jq's `path_intact` refused it, the `try` caught the refusal, and the write was lost silently at exit 0 (`{"a":true}`). The refusal now stops the program (exit 5) as it already did without the `try`, for `reduce` and `foreach`, a literal or computed key, and `?`. Only a refusal of a step that could have succeeded is loud; one jq raises whatever its register is stays catchable. A try-wrapped fold jq answers by finding the alternative intact (`reduce . as {a:$a} ?// [$a] (0; .)` under `del(try ...)`) now refuses where it was caught.
