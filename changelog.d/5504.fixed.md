- **yq: `path(recurse(.[]?; cond))` stops at an alias, and a union after a `recurse(f)` position stage is branch-major** (#5504). With `--jq-extensions`, `[path(recurse(.[]?; true))] | length` over `a: &x {p: 1}` / `b: *x` expanded the alias and was 5, where `[recurse(.[]?; true) | path] | length` and `[path(..)] | length` are 4; it now walks the live document and prunes with its `cond` as the value walk does, so a `cond` reading a node's tag also sees what the value walk sees. `[recurse(.[]?; true) | (path, key)]` listed every path, then every key, per node (`[[],["a"],"a",["a","b"],"b"]`), and a navigation stage before a position reader ran over alias-dereferenced values, so `... | select(type == "!!map") | .x | select(. != null) | path` reached an anchor and its alias; both now answer as `..` does (192 of 192 swept reader, document and walk rows). `recurse(.[]?)` and `recurse` had the same two differences and are fixed with it.
