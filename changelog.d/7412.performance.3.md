- **jq: `map_values(f)` over an object in the document reads its fields where they stand** (#7412).
  It wrote the object out as JSON, indexed that copy and ran over it, with the copy, its text and the
  result all live at once; it now runs `f` on the document's own fields, and the check that every value
  decodes (so that one `f` never reads still raises jq's parse error first) is a walk that builds nothing.
  `map_values(.+1)|length` on a 10 MB object peaks at 92 MiB instead of 202 MiB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X
  (jq 1.7.1: 169 MiB) and is 18% faster; on an Apple M4 Pro it peaks at 87 MiB instead of 197 MiB (jq:
  206 MiB) and is 1-2% faster. At 1 MB it peaks at 16 MiB instead of 27 MiB (jq: 22 MiB) and 12 MiB
  instead of 22 MiB (jq: 27 MiB). `[.users[]|map_values(tostring)]` over 10 MB of `users` is 14-16% faster
  on both.
