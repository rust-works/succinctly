- **jq: an `until`/`while` loop that keys its state by a value read off it is linear** (#7473).
  `{i:0,p:{}} | until(.i>=4000; .p["k\(.i)"]=1 | .i += 1)`, the same in `while`, and
  `(.i|tostring) as $cs | .p[$cs] = .i` as the round, re-indexed and rebuilt the whole state every round, so
  the time grew with the square of the round count where jq 1.7.1 is linear (28x slower at 4,000 rounds on an Apple M4 Pro, 58x on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X). The
  loop's in-place step now reads such a key off the state: a string interpolation whose slots are scalars, and
  `.i | tostring`; a slot that is a container, raises or yields other than once keeps the evaluator's route, and
  the answers are unchanged.
  `last(while ...)` over the same round is not changed and stays quadratic.
