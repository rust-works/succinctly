- **jq: a field read through an `as` rebind of a fold's loop variable keeps its storage when stored** (#5938).
  `[{"items":[nan]}] as $in | reduce $in[] as $r ({}; $r as $s | .a = $s.items) | .a == $in[0].items` is `true` in jq, where the stored member is the element's own array, and was `false` here: the in-place step declined an `as` rebind of the fold's marker, so the evaluator rebuilt the member as a literal.
  The step now binds the new name to the same marker, and the gate counts a read of a name rebound that way as a read of the loop variable (`.a = $s.items`, `.a += $s.items`, `.[$s.n] = $s.items`, a rebind of a rebind).
  A rebind in front of arithmetic, a pipe of writes or a destructuring pattern is unchanged and recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
  `succinctly yq` is unchanged.
