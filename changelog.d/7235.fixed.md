- **yq: `line_comment =` on a block mapping or sequence is written on the next line, as yq does** (#7235). `.a line_comment = "x"` over `a:` / `  c: 1` / `b: 6` printed the document unchanged; yq's encoder cannot put the comment on the key's line, holds it and writes `b: 6 # x`, and this now does the same, including under a nested key, a sequence item, `|=`, and a multi-line text. When nothing follows the collection the comment is lost, as in yq. The text of a written or copied value read through `to_yaml` still drops every comment (#6933).
