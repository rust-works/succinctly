- **jq, yq: a reader that stops early ends the process with SIGPIPE, as it does jq and yq** (#5547). `succinctly jq -n 'range(10000000)' | head -n1` printed `failed to flush output: Broken pipe (os error 32)` and exited 1 (Rust starts a process with SIGPIPE ignored); it now prints `0` and dies of SIGPIPE, status 141 under a shell with nothing on stderr, which is what jq 1.7.1 and yq v4.53.3 do for `range(...)`, `.[]` over a large array and `repeat(1)`. The default disposition is restored by the two query subcommands only, through a `libc` dependency that is already in the tree (`ctrlc`, `jiff`), and only on Unix.
