- **jq: a loop body with a second exit after its first answers as Oniguruma's does when no way follows it** (#6424). `"bab" | [match("(\\A|a|\\B)+";"g") | .captures[0].string]` printed `["","a",""]` and jq 1.7.1 answers `["","",""]`; `"ba" | [match("(\\B|(?:a|b*)){2,}";"g") | .string]` printed `["ba",""]` and jq answers `["b","",""]`. Oniguruma tries what follows the loop once at a head, at the first exit that holds there, so a second anchor-only way (or a way that matches nothing) with no consuming way after it is the exit every loop ends with, except that it sets the group around the body: the loop now writes it back last among each head's ways, and the first exit only where its anchor holds. The same anchor again (or `\A` and `^`) is never the first to hold and is dropped. Over three draws of 1,500 loops with anchors (`--assert`, seeds 3, 4 and 5) the patterns that differ fall from 91 to 55 and none changed the other way; 433 of 3,744 generated two-anchor loops that differed agree, and none changed. A second exit with a consuming way after it still differs.
