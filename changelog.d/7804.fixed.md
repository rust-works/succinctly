- **A `#` or a Unicode blank inside a class under the regex `x` flag keeps jq's empty matches on multibyte text** (#7804). Oniguruma reads a `#` in a class as the character and keeps every blank there, where the `regex` crate reads the `#` as a comment and drops U+3000, U+00A0 and the other Unicode blanks; such a class did not parse, and the pattern went to the second engine, which left out the extra empty match jq makes one byte after an empty match (#6326). `"é" | [match("[#]?"; "gx") | .offset]` is `[0,1,1]` as in jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2, where it was `[0,1]`, and the same holds for `[#a]*`, `(?x)[#]{0,1}`, `[　]?` and the rest. Over a 14,976-row matrix of class forms, quantifiers, texts and the flags `g`, `gx`, `gxn` and `gxl`, the rows differing from jq went from 4,272 to 314 (all of them are the `l` flag on a lazy quantifier, a recorded limit).
