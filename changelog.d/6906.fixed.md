- **yq: `omit([...])` keeps the presentation of what it keeps** (#6906). `omit(["a"])` printed the rest of the document as plain block YAML: every comment, flow style (`c: {k: v}`), quoting (`d: "q"`), block scalar and the document's head and foot comments were dropped, even when nothing was removed (`omit(["zz"])`), where yq keeps them. `pick` has kept them since #5003. `omit` now joins the same replay: the members (or sequence items) it keeps come through as the document's own nodes, key and all. A key of the wrong kind for the node (`omit(["a"])` over a sequence, `omit([0])` over a mapping) matches nothing and the node comes through whole, as in yq. 29 rows in `tests/yq_cli/i6906.rs` match yq v4.53.3 over mappings, sequences and nested nodes; the shapes that still differ are the existing families (a `*x` whose anchor was omitted, which yq leaves dangling and this tool expands; a collection-keyed mapping, #6547; the `~` spelling, #2802).
