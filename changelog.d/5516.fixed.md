- **jq: `last`, `reduce` and `foreach` hand a container loop variable on as the element itself** (#5516).
  `[nan] as $a | last($a) == $a` is `true` in jq and was `false` here, as were `reduce $a as $v (null; $v) == $a`, `foreach $a as $v (0; $v) == $a`, `reduce $a as $v (null; [$v]) | .[0] == $a` and `reduce $a as $v (null; $v[0])` on `[[nan]]` (jq's `jv_equal` short-circuits on `jv_identical`, and `[nan] == [nan]` is `false`).
  A step whose output can be its loop variable, or a node of it, rebuilt the container from a literal; #3896's storage-sharing splice now covers that step too, and `last(f)` is `reduce f as $x (null; $x)` (#5377), so it follows.
  An assignment, arithmetic (`. + [$v]`, `.x = $v`) and an `if` condition keep their in-place step and still rebuild.
  The issue's own rows (a computed-bound full slice, an element of a shared slice, `limit`/`first` around a variable-headed pipe) already matched jq 1.7.1 since #5487 and are now pinned. `succinctly yq` is unchanged.
