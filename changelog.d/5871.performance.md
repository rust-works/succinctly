- **jq: a body rebuilt for each value that reads its variable in every stage and ends in a `def` call is linear in its stage count** (#5871).
  A tail that holds a `def` call cannot be a view of its pipe's shared storage (#3148), so every tail of the
  copy was copied again: `n - 1`, `n - 2`, ... stage clones per value. The stages ahead of the call are now one
  link whose own tails are views, and only the call is cloned again.
  `def f: . + 1; [range(0;20000) | . as $x | (<n-1 stages of . + $x> | f)] | add` at n = 10, 20, 40, 80
  took 0.14 s, 0.38 s, 1.17 s, 4.03 s and now takes 0.12 s, 0.23 s, 0.47 s, 0.96 s (Apple M5 Max, release,
  interleaved minimum of 3, output identical to jq 1.7.1). The same pipe with the call inlined, and with
  `. + 1` stages, did not move (0.99x to 1.02x). jq mode only: yq keeps its copies, since its first-stage
  rule for a failing generator reads the first stage of a pipe.
