- **yq: `recurse(.[]?; cond)` takes the cursor walk for a `cond` that reads `tag`, `kind`, `style`, `anchor`, `tojson`, `test`, `any`, `all` or `isempty`** (#5506).
  Those builtins were not on the allow-list `yields_at_most_one_value`, so such a `cond` kept the owned walk, which expands every alias: over a 22-level alias ladder `first(recurse(.[]?; tag != "~"))` took about ten seconds and 800 MB in a debug build (the cost doubles per level) and now takes 0.2 s, and `[recurse(.[]?; tag != "!!null")] | length` over `a: &b {x: 1, y: [1, 2]}` with `c: *b` beside it is 7, as `[..] | length` is, where it was 11. A `cond` passed through a `def` parameter, `?`, `try` or an object construction still keeps the owned walk.
