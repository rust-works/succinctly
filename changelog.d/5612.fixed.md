- **jq: a `.field`, `[i]` or `[]` after `first`, `last`, `limit`, `range`, `repeat`, `until`, `while`, `error` or `JOIN` no longer fails with exit 3** (#5612). `first(.a[]).id`, `last[0]`, `[limit(2; .[]).x]`, `range(3)[0]` and `try error("x").y catch .` compile and run as in jq 1.7.1; these nine were the only builtins the parser refused a postfix after (a sweep of every builtin name against every postfix form, 1688 probes plus the `JOIN`/`IN`/`INDEX` family, now reports no difference). `succinctly yq` takes one after `first` and `error`, as yq 4.53.3 does, and still refuses it after the names yq's lexer rejects (they follow jq under `--jq-extensions`). Pinned against jq 1.7.1 in `tests/jq_cli/i5612.rs` and `tests/yq_cli/i5612.rs`.
