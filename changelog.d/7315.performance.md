- **jq: a math builtin of a document number no longer decodes the number's text three times** (#7315).
  `floor`, `ceil`, `round`, `sqrt`, `fabs`, `log`, `sin` and the other unary math builtins, and `pow`/`atan2`,
  read each number's source span as UTF-8 once to check it, once more to read it, and a third time inside
  the reader, 12.5% of `[.numbers[] | floor]`'s instructions. They now validate it as a number and as text
  in one pass and read the double from that text. `[.numbers[]|floor]|add` on the 100 MB `numbers` document
  takes 769 ms instead of 921 ms on an Apple M4 Pro (-16.5%, 13.3% fewer instructions at 10 MB) and
  1222 ms instead of 1356 ms on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (-9.9%, 16.4% fewer instructions); `sqrt` and
  `map(floor)` gain about as much, `pow(.;2)` 2-6%. Output is unchanged.
