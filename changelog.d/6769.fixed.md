- **yq: `sub(re, s)` and `with_dtf(layout, expr)` take a comma, as in yq** (#6769). yq reads the arguments of both as the left and
  right child of whatever node holds them, so a comma spells them as well as `;` does, and yq's own string-operators and datetime
  pages write `sub("dogs", "cats")` and `with_dtf("Monday, 02-Jan-06 at 3:04PM MST", .a += "3h1m")`. `succinctly yq` accepted only
  the `;` spelling and refused the comma one as a parse error. The first item is the pattern (the layout), the rest is the
  replacement (the body), and the first output of a replacement list is the one read (`sub("dogs", "X", "Y")` is `X are great`,
  as in yq); parentheses only group, so `sub(("a", "X"))` is the same call. `succinctly jq` keeps refusing the comma spelling, as jq does.
