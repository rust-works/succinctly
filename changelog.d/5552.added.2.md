- **compare(jq): `--cachegrind` counts instructions beside the wall-clock** (#5552).
  `succinctly dev compare jq --cachegrind` (Linux, needs `valgrind`) runs each tool once per performance row
  up to 1 MB under `valgrind --tool=cachegrind` and reports `I refs` for succinctly and jq 1.7.1, the
  ratio, a geomean, and a mark on rows where the instruction count and the wall-clock disagree in sign.
  CI's `compare-jq` workflow passes it on the x86_64 and ARM64 Linux runners.
