- **yq: a chain of comparisons is parsed right-associatively** (#5629). yq gives `==`, `!=`, `<`,
  `<=`, `>` and `>=` one precedence (40) and its operator stack pops only a strictly higher one, so
  `1 == 1 == true` is `1 == (1 == true)` and prints `false`; `succinctly yq` stopped at the second
  operator with a parse error. A chain longer than the expression-nesting limit is an error here
  where yq takes any length. `succinctly jq` still refuses a chain, as jq 1.7.1 does.
