- **jq: a regex `{n}+` is the fixed count `{n}`, not a repeat of it** (#6079). `"aaaa" | match("a{2}+")` is `aa` in jq (a fixed count has nothing to give back) and was `aaaa` here, and `.{1}+` matched two characters where jq matches one, with no error. The `+` is dropped, and a quantifier after it applies to the `{n}` (`{n}++` repeats it, `{n}+?` makes it optional), as in jq. Of 3,360 pattern, text and flag rows, those with a fixed `{n}+` now all agree with jq 1.7.1; possessive `{n,m}+` and `*+` are still refused where they are not last.
