- **yq: `del()` of several array indices pads to the highest one past the end** (#4749). `[10,20,30] | del(.[5], .[0])` is `[20,30,null,null]` in yq, and `succinctly yq` answered `[20,30]`: a single past-the-end index already padded (#2305) but a batch of two or more did not. A batch of plain non-negative indices now pads once to the largest and then deletes the others, in either order (`del(.[5], .[7])` on three elements is `[10,20,30,null,null,null]`). A batch with a negative index or a slice, and a repeated index, still differ (recorded in `limitations.md`). The issue's mapping row needs a key-spelling bit and stays open.
