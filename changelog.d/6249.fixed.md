- **jq: under the `x` flag a repeat operator with nothing to repeat is refused** (#6249). `"x0" | match("*0";"x")`, `match("a| *b";"x")` and `match("(?: *a)";"x")` answered as if the operator were not there, where jq 1.7.1 refuses them with `target of repeat operator is not specified` (without `x` both already refused). The flag's `(?x)` prefix is a group that the `fancy-regex` fallback repeats; the pattern scan now refuses `*`, `+` and `?` at the start of a branch as it did `{2}`, on the pattern without the blanks and comments `x` ignores, and no longer skips patterns made of nothing but such a branch start. A `(?x)` written in the pattern itself still stands down.
