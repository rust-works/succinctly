- **jq: reading past a parse fault into a deeply nested remainder is linear** (#7611).
  On `-n input`, `inputs` and `input_line_number`, a text whose first fault is not a depth refusal and which
  goes on into a deep remainder (`[x[x[x...` with its closers at the end, 1.6 million levels) took seconds where
  jq 1.7.1 reports the fault at once: after each fault jq drops the rest of its read and parses on from the next,
  and every such read was split, indexed and validated to the end of the text. #7470 cut that work at a depth
  refusal; a read past a fault is now parsed only through the next fault of any kind, so doubling the remainder
  doubles the work where it quadrupled it, and the output is unchanged.
