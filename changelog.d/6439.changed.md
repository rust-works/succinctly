- **jq: a counted repeat of a Unicode class up to a few thousand copies compiles, where it was refused at 10 MiB** (#6439). `"a" | test("\\w{1000}")` printed `Regex failure: Compiled regex exceeds size limit of 10485760 bytes.` and jq 1.7.1 answers `false`. The size limit of the compiled program and of the lazy-DFA cache is 128 MiB now, so `\w{2500}`, `\d{5000}` and `.{100000}` compile, while `\w{3000}` and `\w{100000}` are still refused. The cost is memory on a refusal: a refused pattern allocates up to about 760 MB first, where the 10 MiB limit stopped at 30 MB (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md` has the measurements).
