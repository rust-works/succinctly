- **jq: a loop whose body has a capture group after its empty way ends at that empty way, as Oniguruma's does** (#6310). `"ba" | [match("(?:b*|(a))+") | .string]` printed `["ba"]`; jq 1.7.1 answers `["b"]`, as it does for the same loop without the group, and `sub`, `gsub`, `scan` and `splits` followed, as did `(b*|(a))+`. The loop rewrite of #6106 now lists a capture group's ways and writes the group once, in the iteration, so the pattern's groups keep their number and order; a group it would copy or drop (in a way before the empty one, in the empty way itself, or in the body of a counted repeat) keeps the loop as written (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`). Over two draws of 5,000 generated loops (`scripts/jq-regex-capture-loop-sweep.py`), 51 and 68 patterns that differed from jq now agree and none changed the other way.
