- **jq: a `reduce` nested in a fold body retries its `?//` alternatives on jq's own path error, so `try ((reduce ...) and (.a | .b)?)` no longer lets `del` through** (#4806, #4608).
  `path(foreach .a? as $k (.; .; (reduce . as [$a] ?// $a ({"a":5}; .a))))` on `null` is `["a"]` in jq and was refused here; `del(foreach .a? as $k (.; .; try ((reduce . as [$a] ?// $a ({"a":5}; .a)) and (.a | .b)?)))` raises `Invalid path expression with result false` in jq and was accepted. The nested `reduce`'s UPDATE navigates its computed `{"a":5}` (no node of the register), jq's `?//` catches that error and retries onto `$a` with the state slot handed back as `null`, where the walk refused the first error as final. That retry, on the exact error kind (never a `GuessedPathRefusal`), now applies at any fold depth, as it did only outside every fold body. Over 14,000 differential rows against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 (`path`, `del`, `|=`, 9 documents, `reduce`/`foreach` operands under `and`/`or`/`try`/`isempty`): 862 rows that differed now match, none newly accepts; two rows that both refuse now name a different node in the message. The register sweeps (`jq-path-register-sweep.py` over the fold contexts, the nested-fold, fresh-source, alt-chain, alt-passthrough and alt-retry sweeps) show 0 regressions.
