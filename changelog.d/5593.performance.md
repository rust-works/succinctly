- **jq: `floor`, `ceil`, `round`, `trunc`, `sqrt` and `fabs` of a document number no longer write it out and index it again** (#5593).
  Each was a text round trip and an index build per number to make one libm call. They now read the number's double the way the
  evaluator does and answer from it; a string, a computed double, a non-finite literal and yq mode keep the old route, so errors
  and every answer are unchanged. `[.numbers[]|floor]|add` over a generated `numbers` document is 1.56x-1.83x faster than before
  (0.85x-0.90x of jq 1.7.1 before, 1.45x-1.61x after; 1/10/100 MB, interleaved min, Apple M5 Max), `[.numbers[]|floor]|length`
  2.0x-2.6x faster, and `.numbers|map(ceil)|length` 1.9x-2.3x faster. A step after the builtin (`floor|.+1`) is still 0.87x-0.89x of jq.
