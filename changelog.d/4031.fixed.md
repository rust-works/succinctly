- **jq: `path(foreach (foreach (. as [$a] | .) as $k (.; .; .)) as $q (.; .; .))` now refuses as jq does** (#4031). A `foreach` whose source is a nested `foreach` over a destructuring bind of the register answered `[]` (and a write through it landed nowhere) where jq raises `Invalid path expression with result [[1]]`: the nested `foreach` does not backtrack its source, so the bind's tracked index step moves jq's register for the outer fold, whatever the nested loop pattern is. Only a nested `foreach` whose own *loop pattern* destructured (#3853) was routed through the resolver before; the nested source is now read too. A nested `reduce` backtracks and is unchanged.
