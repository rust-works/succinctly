- **yq: the empty lines at the front of an input are printed back on the DOM routes too** (#5008). yq's header preprocessing slurps them and prints them before the first document whatever the filter does, so `printf '\n\na: 1\n' | yq -P .`, `yq '.a = 2'` and `yq 'del(.a)'` keep both empty lines; `succinctly yq` kept them only on the streaming `.` route and dropped them under `-P`, a write, or a computed result. A header that holds a comment as well (`\n\n# c\na: 1` under `-P` or a write, and a run of empty lines between two header comments) is still printed differently, as is an empty line with a carriage return in it; both stay recorded in the yq limitations.
