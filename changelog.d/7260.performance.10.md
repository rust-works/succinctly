- **jq: a document the program builds whole is read a chunk at a time, without holding its text or index** (#7260).
  When the program's first stage is an assignment or `del(...)` over a lone regular file, and for `--slurpfile`
  of a regular file, the document is now parsed from 64 KiB reads straight into its tree; nothing else is built.
  At 10 MB of the `users` input, `.users[0].name="x"` and `del(.users[0])` peak at 75 MB instead of 84 MB on an
  AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (jq 1.7.1: 65-68 MB) and at 62 MB instead of 72 MB on an Apple M4 Pro (jq: 67-68 MB), and
  run 15-16% and 21-22% faster; `--slurpfile` of 10 MB of NDJSON peaks at 110 MB instead of 126 MB (jq: 99 MB)
  and 90 MB instead of 107 MB (jq: 100 MB), 12-17% faster. Any input the chunked reader declines is read again
  by the old route, so every message, position and exit code is unchanged.
