- **yq: `head_comment`, `foot_comment` and `comments` can be set on a scalar mapping value or sequence item** (#2796, part 3). `succinctly yq '.a foot_comment = "bye"'` on `a: 1\nb: 2` raised `foot_comment = ... is not yet supported`; it now prints `a: 1\n# bye\n\nb: 2` as yq v4.53.3 does. yq does not print a comment set on such a node above or below it but where go-yaml's emitter flushes it: a mapping value's head lands just above the next key (or trails the last entry), its foot follows the entry with a blank line unless it is the last, a sequence item's head is above it, and `comments` sets the line comment, the foot and the head together. A node or its successor that already owns head or foot lines, anything under a flow collection, a container target, and a write to several targets still raise `not yet supported`: the order yq flushes those in is not modelled. Checked by 13 pinned-yq goldens and a differential fuzz of about 5,300 accepted writes over random commented documents, none differing.
