- **yq: an arithmetic operator over two kinds that do not combine now fails with yq's wording, not jq's** (#6904).
  `printf 'f: 1.5\nn: null\n' | yq '.f + .n'` is `Error: !!float cannot be added to !!null`, and `succinctly yq` raised jq's `number (1.5) and null (null) cannot be added`.
  All five operators follow yq over every pair of kinds (captured against v4.53.3: 180 error rows): `-` over two scalars says `cannot be added to` as yq does, `*` names both operands (`cannot multiply !!float with !!str`, which was reversed for a float on the left), `/` and `%` say `cannot be divided by` and `cannot modulo by`, and a container operand is named with its key.
  The key is `()` here, since a value carries none; the exit status is 1 in both.
  `succinctly jq` keeps jq's wording.
