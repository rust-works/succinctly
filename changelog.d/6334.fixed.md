- **yq: a comment written inside a flow collection is kept** (#6334). `printf 'v: ["a", # c1⏎  "b", # c2⏎  "c"]⏎' | succinctly yq .` printed `v: ["a", "b", "c"]`, dropping both comments from the file; yq prints the collection across lines with each comment after its element's comma (`v: ["a", # c1⏎  "b", # c2⏎  "c"]`), and the closing bracket on a line of its own when the last element is commented. The same holds for a comment on a line of its own (`[` / `# head` / `x,` / `y` / `# foot` / `]`), which yq reads as the head of the element after it and the foot of the last one (`.v[0] | head_comment` answers `head`), lays out on lines of their own at the collection's indent, and keeps under `-P`; in a mapping the comment belongs to the entry's key. The parser records a comment for the element it follows or precedes (a sequence element, a mapping entry, nested collections included), and the cursor and document routes lay the collection out with the layout they share (`yaml::flow_layout`). Over 600 generated documents (541 holding a comment: flow sequences and mappings, nested, in a mapping value, a nested mapping and a sequence item, checked under `.`, a write, a navigation, `-P` and `-I4`), the ones that differ from yq under `.` fell from 454 to 159, and those that differ under some filter from 454 to 244, none changed the other way. A comment before a comma that precedes the closing bracket, some blank lines yq writes beside a footed collection, and `-P` with a collection that ends a sequence still differ (`docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`).
