- **yq: a comment written after an element of a flow collection is kept** (#6334, part of it). `printf 'v: ["a", # c1⏎  "b", # c2⏎  "c"]⏎' | succinctly yq .` printed `v: ["a", "b", "c"]`, dropping both comments from the file; yq prints the collection across lines with each comment after its element's comma (`v: ["a", # c1⏎  "b", # c2⏎  "c"]`), and the closing bracket on a line of its own when the last element is commented. The parser now records such a comment for the element it follows (a sequence element, a mapping entry's value, nested collections included), and the cursor route lays the collection out with the layout the DOM route already had for a held comment (`yaml::flow_layout`, shared). Over 2,000 generated documents (flow sequences and mappings, nested, in a mapping value, a nested mapping and a sequence item, under `.`, a write, a navigation, `-P` and `-I4`), about 1,100 differed from yq before and 73 do now, none changed the other way. A comment on a line of its own inside the collection, the `-P` rewrite to a block and `-I4` nested under a `- [` item still differ (`docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`).
