- **jq: a `-0` integer spelling keeps its sign in `pow`, `atan2`, `fmod`, `ldexp` and the other multi-argument math builtins** (#5364). `-0 | pow(.;1)`, `atan2(-0;-1)`, `fmod(-0;3)` and `ldexp(-0;2)` read the `-0` as positive zero and answered `0`, `3.141592653589793`, `0` and `0`; jq answers `-0`, `-3.141592653589793`, `-0` and `-0`, as it already did for `-0.0`, for a `-0` computed as `0 * -1`, and for the one-argument builtins. The same held for `fmin`, `fmax`, `scalb`, `scalbln`, `drem`, `remainder` and `fma`. 95 rows were captured from jq 1.7.1; yq mode, where a `-0` literal is integer zero, is unchanged.
