- **jq: `tostream` and `paths` share each path's keys instead of copying them into every path** (#7260).
  A key above a container is now held once per visit and shared by every path beneath it, where each path
  carried its own copies; and `tostream` consumes the document it materializes instead of writing it out,
  indexing it again and copying every leaf. `[tostream]|length` on 10 MB of the `comprehensive` input peaks
  at 444 MB instead of 615 MB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (jq 1.7.1: 359 MB) and runs in 390 ms instead of 527 ms
  (jq: 1688 ms); `[paths]|length` peaks at 264 MB instead of 326 MB (jq: 271 MB) and is 17% faster. On an
  Apple M4 Pro the same rows peak at 435 MB instead of 511 MB and 249 MB instead of 279 MB (jq: 362 and
  292 MB), 27% and 18% faster.
