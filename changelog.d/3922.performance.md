- **jq: `[.[] | ., .]` and `[...]?` keep the nodes they hold instead of decoding them** (#3922). A `,` stage that does not head its pipe (`[.users[] | ., .]`) ran the owned route, and a `?`/`try` around an array forced it to learn whether it fails; both decoded every node the array holds, and answered a decode failure for a value nothing read. On a 14 MB `users` document, release, Apple M4 Pro, interleaved A/B with identical output: `[.users[] | ., .] | length` 286.7 ms and 290 MB -> 33.9 ms and 42 MB (`-88%`), `[.users, 1]? | length` 150.0 ms and 144 MB -> 25.2 ms and 26 MB (`-83%`); the comma-array holdouts (`[.users, .users]`, `[(.users, .users) | .[0]]`, `[.users[0] | .name, .age]`) read within the control's range. Over an unreadable value `[.[] | ., .] | length` and `[.a, .b]? | length` now answer (`4`, `2`) like `[.a, .b] | length`; printing the array still raises. jq mode only.
