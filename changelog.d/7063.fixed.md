- **yq: a run of backslashes before `\(` and an unclosed `\(` are read as yq reads them** (#7063). yq does not pair backslashes left to right: of `n` before `(`, an interpolation starts when `n / 2` is even (`"\(1)"` is `1`, `"\\(1)"` and `"\\\(1)"` are the text `\(1)`, `"\\\\(1)"` is `\1`), and `succinctly yq` agreed only for one and two. A string with a `\(` that no `)` closes is kept as written, closed interpolations before it included (`"\(1)\("` is `\(1)\(`), where it was a parse error; the runs before a `(` in such a string keep `n / 2 + 1` backslashes. The rule is the same after a prefix, before a suffix and for each slot of a second one. Pinned by 221 rows captured from yq v4.53.3. yq's `unclosed interpolation string` warning is not printed, and three or more backslashes before a character that is not `(` still pair left to right (recorded in the limitations page).
