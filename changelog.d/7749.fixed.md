- **A NUL glued to a number before a closer is reported at jq's column, and a top-level number it ends is not printed before jq's error** (#7749). jq ends the token at the next structural byte, not at the NUL, so `[[1]1`, a NUL, `]` is `Expected separator between values at line 1, column 7` and `1`, a NUL, `}` is `Unmatched '}' at line 1, column 3` with nothing printed, in jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2, where `succinctly jq` reported column 6 and printed `1` first, on every route. The rewrite #7437 makes of such a token now puts the number at the token's end, with the spaces before it, also when the token starts in an earlier file.
