- **jq: a fold step that binds a scalar read off its state with `as` is 1.4-3.0x faster** (#6582).
  A `reduce`, `foreach`, `until` or `while` step such as `. as $s | $s + $x`, `(. + $x) as $t | $t` or
  `.n as $n | .n = $n + 1` now binds the value and answers the body on the state. Before, the whole
  binding ran on a re-indexed copy of the state every step. On an Apple M5 over 4,000,000 `reduce`
  steps, `.n as $n | .n = $n + 1` takes 1,054 ms instead of 3,156 ms (jq 1.7.1: 2,564 ms), and
  `. as $s | $s + $x` takes 928 ms instead of 1,799 ms (jq: 646 ms). An `if` body
  (`(. + $x) as $t | if $t > 1000 then 0 else $t end`) gains least: 1,849 ms instead of 2,584 ms.
