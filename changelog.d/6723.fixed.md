- **yq: `path`, `key` and `parent` of a value a `<<` merge lists name its anchor's location** (#6723). With `a: &a {x: 1}` and `item: {<<: *a, k: 0}`, `.item.x | path` is `["a","x"]` in yq and was `["item","x"]` here; `[.item | .[] | path]` was `[["item","x"],["item","k"]]` where yq gives `[["a","x"],["item","k"]]`, and `key` and `parent` followed (`.item.x | parent` is the anchor's mapping). The path-context walk now starts its trail over at the member's real location, and a step taken from it carries on from there (`.item.x.p | path` is `["a","x","p"]`). A write through the same path still changes the mapping's own entry, where yq changes the shared anchor's. Measured over 256 rows (8 documents of merge shapes x 21 reads and 11 writes) against yq v4.53.3: 28 got right, 0 got worse, and every write row is unchanged.
