- **yq: `key` and `path` after a spelled integer bracket on a mapping name the key as written, and a key rename through an integer bracket renames** (#8070). Since #7999 `.m[0x1]` read the member keyed `"0x1"`, but `.m[0x1] | key` and `.m[0x1] | path` still stood on the member keyed `1`: they answered `"1"` and `["m","1"]` where yq v4.53.3 answers `"0x1"` and `["m","0x1"]` (the same for `01`), and `.m[0x1] | [key, .]` paired that key with the wrong value. A typed key keeps yq's spelling too (`.m[0x1] | key` over `0x1: a` prints `0x1`, its path is `["m",1]`). `(.m[1] | key) = "q"` over `'1': six` and `(.m[2] | key) = "q"` over `2: two` changed nothing where yq renames the key (`'q': six`, `q: two`), and `(.m[3] | key) = "q"` over a mapping without `3` left `3: null` where yq writes `q: null`.
