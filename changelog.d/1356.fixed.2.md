- **yq: a float written `+2.0`, `2.`, `.5`, `1.e5`, `+1e5` or `007.5` keeps its spelling** (#1356).
  A document float and `tonumber` of such a string print as written through `tostring`, string interpolation, `join` and YAML output (`a: +2.0 | .a | tostring` is `+2.0`, `.5` is `.5`, `1.e5` is `1.e5`), and `sort`, `max` and `unique` return the node with its spelling, as yq does.
  `-o=json` prints yq's `FormatFloat` of the value: `2.0`, `0.5`, and `100000.0` for `1.e5` and `+1e5`, which printed `1.0e5` and `1e5` before.
  An underscored decimal or a hex float string (`"1_0.5"`, `"0x1p3"`) still normalizes.
