- **jq: `foreach` with a generator INIT runs its source on the real input for every INIT output** (#7519). `[1, 2] | [foreach .[] as $x (0, 1; . + $x)]` raised `Cannot iterate over null`, as jq 1.7.1 does: its `DUPN` hands every INIT output after the first a `null` input. jq 1.8.0 fixed that as a bug (jqlang/jq#3266), and `succinctly jq` now follows jq 1.8.2 and prints `[1,3,2,4]`, in value mode, in `path()` and in assignment targets. `reduce` keeps the 1.7.1 behaviour, as jq 1.8.2 does. The difference from the pinned 1.7.1 is recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md` and the ledger's `syntax:foreach-gen-init`.
