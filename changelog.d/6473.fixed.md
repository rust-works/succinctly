- **jq: a loop whose body has two or more ways that match nothing only where an anchor holds leaves where Oniguruma does** (#6473).
  A second exit with a consuming way after it, three or more exits, and a way that holds an anchor
  beside an item that may match nothing (`\Aa*`, `\B(\w*)`) are now written way by way, each kind of
  loop head (`\b` or `\B`, and `\A` at the first head) behind its own anchors: `"a b" |
  [match("(\\Aa*|\\B){2,}?";"g") | .string]` is `[""]`, as in jq 1.7.1, where it was `["a"]`. Over three
  draws of 1,500 anchored loops the patterns that differ from jq fall from 55 to 24, almost all of the
  rest a loop with a capture group in a pattern holding `$` or `\z`, whose groups the `fancy-regex`
  route does not copy.
