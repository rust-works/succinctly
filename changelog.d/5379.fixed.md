- **jq: a tail-recursive `def` runs in place instead of being refused after 40,000 frames** (#5379).
  A call with no arguments, last in its body, is a jump in jq (`TAIL_CALL_JQ`): `def f: if . < 300000 then . + 1 | f else . end; 0 | f`
  answers `300000`, and 3,000,000 levels in 13 MB. `succinctly jq` nested the body's native frames per call and
  stopped at `f/0 exceeded maximum recursion depth` after 7,569 levels (`MAX_EVAL_FRAMES` counts about 5.3 frames per call
  of that program). The call now loops in the plain, the document and the path-mode evaluators, and in `succinctly yq`:
  3,000,000 levels run in 0.29 s and 13.7 MB (release, Apple M5 Max), the same peak as 300,000, where the refusal
  came with 154 MB of native stack committed. The positions are jq's: the last stage of a pipe, an `if`/`elif`/`else`
  branch, the last branch of a comma, the body of an `as`, for a call to any def with no parameters (itself, a wrapper,
  the other half of a mutual recursion). A call with arguments, under `try`/`?`/`label`, or behind a generator still nests and keeps
  the guard. A loop that never ends (`def f: f; f`, which jq spins on) stops after 2^24 rounds with the refusal the nested form
  raised. See [ADR-0025](docs/adrs/adr-0025.md) and `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
