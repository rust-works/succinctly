- **yq: a `map(f)` whose body reads `key`, `path` or `parent` runs a literal after a stage that matched nothing, as the same body without the read does** (#8034).
  yq answers a constant over an empty context (`select(false) | 3` is `3`, #6943), and `succinctly yq` did for `map(select(false) | 3)`, but `.b | map(select(key == 5) | 3)` over `b: {x: 1, y: 2}` was `[]` where yq answers `[3]`, and `map(select(key == 5) | error("x"))` never raised: the route that evaluates such a `map` at each member's position skipped the rule. A re-emitting stage that reads path context itself now re-emits too, there and on the #8002 sites, since its reads are of the empty context: `select(false) | [key]` is `[]` and `select(false) | (1, key)` is `1` in `map`, `map_values`, `with_entries` and on the right side of `=` and `|=`, as in yq. A read after it still emits nothing (`select(false) | 3 | key`). `succinctly jq` is unchanged. Rows captured from yq v4.53.3 across the document, `--eval-all` and `-n` routes.
