- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE or EXTRACT under `try` whose body has a comma above every pipe no longer silently skips a write** (#3862, after #3770).
  `(foreach .x as $w (0; try (($w | .a[]), $w); .)) = 9` on `{"a":[{"b":1}],"x":{"a":[{"b":1}]}}` is `{"a":[{"b":1}],"x":9}` in jq and
  left the document unchanged here (exit 0), `del(foreach .x as $w (0; try (($w | .a), ($w | .b)); .))` echoed it, and `path(...)` printed
  nothing where jq prints `["x","a",0]` and `["x"]`. `fans_out` withheld the fold's register from any body with a comma above its pipes, so the
  `try` caught the body's own refusal. A comma forks, so jq runs each sibling from the register the comma was entered with: `fans_out` now lets a
  comma through when each sibling is a pipe or cannot move the register, and the comma states the register on a bare sibling's branch itself
  (`carry_frame_register`, as a `//` alternate does since #3788). A sibling that navigates without a pipe still withholds the register.
  The same rule answers `path(. as $x | 1 | (1, ..) | $x)` and `isempty(first(2,3))` stages, which were loud refusals where jq answers.
