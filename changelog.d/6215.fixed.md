- **jq: the `x` flag no longer hides a quantifier on an assertion** (#6215). `"cab" | match("\\b*"; "gx")` is an error in jq (`target of repeat operator is invalid`) and was an answer here, because the blanks and comments the flag ignores kept the repeat-target checks from seeing what the quantifier follows; `^+`, `a$?`, `(?:\\B){1,2}`, `(?:a|\\b)*` and `(?i)*a` were accepted the same way. The checks now read the pattern without the blanks and comments, so `\\b *` and `(?: \\B ){1,2}` are refused as well. A pattern with an inline `(?x)` and a quantifier right after a `(?#...)` comment still stand down; `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md` lists them.
