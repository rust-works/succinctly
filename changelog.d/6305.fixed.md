- **jq: a loop over a capture group ends at the body's empty way, as Oniguruma's does** (#6305). `"ba" | [match("(b*|a)+") | .string]` printed `["ba"]`; jq 1.7.1 answers `["b"]`, as it does for the same loop without the group, and `sub`, `gsub`, `scan` and `splits` followed. The capture rewrite of #6069 leaves the repeated group's own loop as a plain `(?:X)*`, and the empty-iteration rewrite of #6106 now reads that loop. Over 3,346 generated patterns (a repeated capture group with `*`, `+`, `{0,}` or `{1,}`, on 19 strings under `g`), the 117 that differed from jq now agree and none changed the other way; a capture group nested inside the repeated group, or a repeat with a count of two or more, keeps its earlier answer (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`).
