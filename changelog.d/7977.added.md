- **jq: the call machine, behind `SUCCINCTLY_CALL_MACHINE=1`, off by default** (#7977, step 1 of its migration plan).
  A jq-mode program that holds a recursive `def` can run on a compiled stack machine in jq 1.7.1's image: instructions over contiguous data, frame and fork stacks with gojq's protection watermark, tail calls, and values moved out at their last read.
  With the switch on it answers all 109 programs of its corpus (`scripts/call_machine/`) exactly as jq 1.7.1 does, including linear recursion 100,000 deep that the shipped evaluators refuse at their native-stack floor, and #7803's memo copies its array once at N = 3,200, where jq copies it at every level.
  With the switch unset or `0` nothing changes: the machine is never asked, so no program is compiled for it and no route moves.
  The default flips in a later step, once the machine's budget is complete and the flip is measured.
