- **yq: the second comment block of a run, after a blank line, before the next sequence item is that item's first key's foot, as in yq** (#5053). GitHub's default CodeQL workflow has `languages: ...` / a block of comments / a blank line / a second block (`# For more details on CodeQL's query packs...` / `# queries: security-extended,security-and-quality`) / a blank line / `- name: Perform CodeQL Analysis`, and `yq .` prints the second block after that `- name:` line (`# c2` then a blank line, then `uses:`); succinctly printed it at the end of the document at column 0. Fixed for a sequence at the column of its key (`s:` / `- a: 1`) and an indented one, with one to three blocks, a block that ends right at the next item and an item that is a scalar, a nested sequence or a mapping. A block at or left of the next item's column keeps its placement. Against yq v4.53.3 on 560 generated documents (seven sequence and mapping shapes, one to three comments, every blank-line gap, LF and CRLF): 60 more match, none that matched before differs; on a differential sweep of 1,820 real YAML files the CodeQL workflow now matches and no file changed for the worse. Not fixed: a block that is the first of a run and sits at a sequence owner (`- b: 1` / `  # A` / blank / `  # B` / `- c: 2`), and the other open shapes listed on #5053.
