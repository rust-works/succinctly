- **jq: the second of two parse errors `input` raises is placed where jq places it** (#5923).
  After a fault, jq drops the rest of the read it was in and never counts the dropped bytes, so its
  parser's line and column carry on from where the first fault stopped it: `printf '}\n}\n[4]\n' | jq -n
  '[limit(3; repeat(try input catch .))]'` reports `Unmatched '}' at line 1, column 1` and then
  `... at line 1, column 2`, where `succinctly jq` said `line 2, column 1`. A run of faults on
  adjacent lines (`]\n}\n]`), after blank lines, and one that follows a document now report
  jq's positions; the text and the first fault's position were already right.
