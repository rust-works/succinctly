- **yq: the other spellings of yq's operators, and `root` and `parents`** (#6078). yq's lexer accepts several spellings of most operators (`line_?comment|lineComment`, `to_?entries|toEntries`, `group_?by`, ...), and `succinctly yq` accepted one, so `.a | headComment`, `. | documentIndex`, `.a | toEntries`, `.m | groupby(.k)`, `sortkeys(.)`, `1 | fromunix`, `splitDoc` and `.a lineComment = "x"` were `... is not defined` or a parse error. The spellings of `line_comment`, `head_comment`, `foot_comment`, `document_index`, `file_index`, `filename`, `split_doc`, `map_values`, `group_by`, `sort_by`, `unique_by`, `sort_keys`, `to_entries`, `from_entries`, `with_entries`, `from_unix`, `to_unix` and `array_to_map` are read from yq's rule table and now parse (yq mode only; jq keeps them undefined). `root` (the document's root node) and `parents` (the node's ancestors as a sequence, nearest first, `[]` at the root) are new. 60 rows captured from yq v4.53.3 pin each spelling beside its underscore form (`tests/yq_cli/i6078.rs`). `is_key` and `eval` are still not defined (#6081).
