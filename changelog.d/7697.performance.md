  `[path(.data[] | .name // empty)] | length` freed every member of the document but `.data` as it read
  `.data`, millions of small blocks, and on glibc a later free swept them all again
  (`malloc_consolidate`), about 10% of the run's instructions at 100 MB. The last value's document is
  now kept to the end of the run and forgotten, as its index already was (#7332), for documents of
  64 KiB or more. Instructions (cachegrind, AMD Ryzen 9 7950X), comprehensive input at 10 / 100 MB:
  that row -21.8% / -29.6%, `[path(.users[] | .name // empty)] | length` on users -18.5% / -18.4%,
  `path(.data[] | .name // empty)` -21.5% / -29.1%, `(.data[] | .name // empty) |= 1 | .data | length`
  -21.2% / -28.9%; output identical, and programs the shipped evaluator runs are unchanged.
