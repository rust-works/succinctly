- **jq: `E[a:b] | first`, `| last` and a `?` on the slice or the read no longer build the slice** (#4288). #4251 fused `E[a:b][k]` and `E[a:b] | length` so a loop over them stopped being quadratic; `first`, `last`, `E[a:b][0]?` and `E[a:b]?[0]` still built the whole tail per read. `[range(length-1;-1;-1) as $i | .[$i:] | first | .a.b] | length` over 3,000 elements takes 7.4 ms (was 1,470 ms) and over 6,000 elements 10.6 ms (was 5,804 ms): the old time quadrupled per doubling, the new one is linear. `last` and the two `?` forms match (`[0]?` 6,762 ms before at 6,000), and the already-fused `.[$i:][0]` control row is unchanged (10.4 vs 10.5 ms). Release builds, Apple M5 Max, interleaved min of 3; x86_64 not measured. `first` and `last` are jq's `.[0]` and `.[-1]`, so the fused read applies in jq mode only; yq keeps today's route. A computed index (`.[$i:][$k]`), a literal-bound slice and the `| key`/`| path` reads are unchanged. Like #4251's reads, the fused ones convert only the element they name, recorded in `limitations.md`.
