- **jq: a number literal with an extreme exponent prints the exponent jq's decNumber settles on** (#5508). `0e9999999999` printed `0E+9999999999` where jq prints `0E+999999999`, and `1e-9999999999` printed `1E-9999999999` where jq prints `0E-1147483646`. A zero's exponent is now clamped to `[-1147483646, 999999999]`; a nonzero coefficient that underflows past `-1147483646` is rounded there, half-up on the first digit dropped (`5e-1147483647` -> `1E-1147483646`, `12.5e-1147483646` -> `1.3E-1147483645`, `99.5e-1147483647` -> `1.0E-1147483645`), and a literal whose exponent has more digits than `i128` holds takes the same bound by its sign. A 1,500-literal randomised sweep over both signs, 1-25 digit mantissas with and without a fraction, and exponents from 10 below to 10^30 above each bound printed identically to `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 on every row.
