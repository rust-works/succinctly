- **jq: a long chain of field or index reads over a computed value evaluates in linear memory** (#5663). `{} | .a.a.a…` re-entered the owned evaluator once per stage, and each re-entry built the owned pipe of the stages left and held it while the next ran, so a chain of `n` reads held `n` pipes of up to `n` stages: 2,000 steps took 198 MB, 5,000 took 1.4 GB and 20,000 were killed at 2 GB (jq answers 20,000 in 48 ms). The stages after the first that are plain `.field` or `.[n]` reads are now stepped in a loop, so 20,000 steps peak at 24 MB in a debug build. Reading a document from a file or stdin was never affected.
