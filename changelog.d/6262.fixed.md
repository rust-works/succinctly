- **jq: a backreference to a group that is still open, under a repeat that can run twice, is refused on the second engine instead of answered wrongly** (#6262). `"ab" | [match("(\\1|\\w)+";"g")|.string]` was `["a","b"]` (the group `[1,""]`) where jq 1.7.1 answers `["ab"]` with the group at `[1,"b"]`, and `(a|\1)+`, `(a|\1){2}` and `((?!\1)){2,}` did the same: `fancy-regex` reads such a reference from the group's new start to its old end, empty for contiguous passes, where Oniguruma reads the group's last completed value. Such a pattern now gives `invalid regex` for any text. A reference to a group that is open under no repeat (`(a|\1)`, `(a|\1)?`) and one to a group that has closed (`(a)\1+`) keep their route and answer as jq does. Over a grid of 468 such patterns, 185 differed from jq before; 283 agreed and are refused with them, since a pattern cannot tell which texts reach the reference.
