- **yq datetime operators** (#5863). `succinctly yq` now adds a duration to a timestamp and subtracts one from it (`.t += "3h10m"`, `.t -= "24h"`), keeping the timestamp's zone and raising Go's error for a duration that does not parse; before, `+=` concatenated the text and exited 0. `format_datetime(layout)`, `with_dtf(layout; expr)` and yq's `tz(zone)` are implemented over a port of Go's `time.Parse`, `time.Format` and `time.ParseDuration`. `now`, `from_unix`, `to_unix` and the ordering comparisons read and write timestamps as yq does, in the local zone and under the `with_dtf` layout. `OwnedValue` has no timestamp tag, so a string spelled like a timestamp stands for one: a duration added to a quoted date is added rather than appended, other text is appended (`"2026-01-01" + "T00:00:00Z"`, as in yq), and `now | type` is `!!str`; `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md` records the rest.
