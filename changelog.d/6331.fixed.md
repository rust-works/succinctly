- **jq: `strftime`'s `%I`, `%l` and `%r` print the hour modulo 12 for an hour outside 0-23, as jq's libc does** (#6331). `[2015,2,5,25,0,0,4,63] | strftime("%I %r")` is `01 01:00:00 PM` in jq 1.7.1 and printed `13 13:00:00 PM`; an hour of 100 printed `88` where jq prints `04`, and a negative hour keeps C's sign (`-13` is `-1`). Hours 0-23 are unchanged; a sweep of 93 hours from -30 to 59 plus 100, -100 and 1000 agrees with jq.
