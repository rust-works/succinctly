- **A `$` that `(?m)` does not reach matches before a final newline again** (#6517). A pattern with an `m` in any inline flag group had the final-newline reading of `$` turned off throughout, so `"ab\n" | [match("$(?m)"; "g") | .offset]` was `[3]` where jq gives `[2,3]`, and `"ab\n" | test("b$(?m)")` was `false`. Such a pattern now runs on the exact `fancy-regex` route, which follows `(?m)` group by group. Three random draws of `$` beside `(?m)`, `(?-m)` and `(?m:...)` (1,240-1,271 patterns each, on eight texts) fixed 92-105 patterns and broke none.
