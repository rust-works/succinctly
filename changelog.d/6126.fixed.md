- **jq: a regex group option other than `i`, `m`, `x`, `s` is an error, as in jq** (#6126). `"a" | test("(?U)a")` printed `true` and `"aa" | [match("(?U)a+") | .string]` swapped greedy and lazy quantifiers, where jq 1.7.1 answers `undefined group option`; so do `(?u)`, `(?R:a)` and every other letter, in `(?X)`, `(?X:...)`, `(?-X)` and `(?iX)` spellings, which the `regex` crate used to reject with its own parse-error wording (`(?)` and `(?Ra)` too). `(?R)` is `unmatched close parenthesis`, as in jq. Checked against jq 1.7.1 over 364 option spellings.
