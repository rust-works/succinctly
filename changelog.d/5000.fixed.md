- **yq: `..` over a mapping with a `<<` merge walks the mapping's own entries, not the members the merge brings in** (#5000). `[.item | .. | select(tag == "!!int")]` over `item: {<<: [*a, *b], k: 0}` printed every merged integer (`[1, 8, 2, 0]`); it prints `[0]` as yq does, and `.item | .. | path` names the `<<` entry and its aliases (`["item","<<"]`, `["item","<<",0]`) instead of the merged keys. `path` of a member reached through `.[]` still names the mapping, not the anchor, and a write through `..` behind a path step still reaches the merged members (#6718).
