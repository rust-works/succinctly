- **jq: `[group_by(f)[] | length]` runs the rest of the pipe on each group's nodes** (#7282).
  A `.[]` over `group_by`'s groups with more of the pipe after it (`group_by(f)[] | g` or `group_by(f) | .[] | g`)
  no longer decodes every group before `g` reads it, as the streaming route already did. Over 10 MB of 500 groups,
  `[group_by(.a)[] | length]` takes 165.7 ms instead of 395.2 ms on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X and 125.0 ms instead of
  254.1 ms on an Apple M4 Pro, `[group_by(.a)[] | .[0].id]` 166.2 ms instead of 406.1 ms and 124.4 ms instead of
  282.8 ms, and `[group_by(.a)[] | map(.score) | add]` 241.1 ms instead of 417.7 ms and 193.7 ms instead of
  279.5 ms; `[group_by(.a)[]]`, which keeps every group, is unchanged; output unchanged.
