- **jq: `[group_by(f)[] | length]` runs the rest of the pipe on each group's nodes** (#7282).
  A `.[]` over `group_by`'s groups with more of the pipe after it (`group_by(f)[] | g` or `group_by(f) | .[] | g`)
  no longer decodes every group before `g` reads it, as the streaming route already did. Over 10 MB of 500 groups,
  `[group_by(.a)[] | length]` takes 165.2 ms instead of 386.9 ms on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (289.5 M instructions
  instead of 552.1 M at 1 MB) and 125.0 ms instead of 254.1 ms on an Apple M4 Pro, `[group_by(.a)[] | .[0].id]`
  165.6 ms instead of 401.5 ms and 124.4 ms instead of 282.8 ms, and `[group_by(.a)[] | map(.score) | add]`
  241.5 ms instead of 411.7 ms and 193.7 ms instead of 279.5 ms. `[group_by(.a)[]]`, which keeps every group, runs
  the same instructions as before; a `map(f)` read by `first` or `.[0]` keeps raising on a later element's error,
  as jq does; output unchanged.
