- **`succinctly yq` accepts yq's `eval` (`e`) and `eval-all` (`ea`) subcommands** (#7040). `yq e '.a' f.yaml`, the form most yq scripts use, was `Error: e/0 is not defined` (and `yq -n e 1` read `e` as a file). The first positional is now the subcommand when it names one, with the flags on either side of it; `ea`/`eval-all` also evaluates once over every document, as `--eval-all` does. The rows from yq v4.53.3 are pinned, including stdin, `-n`, value-taking flags before the subcommand, and `.yq` expression files after it. `help`, `completion`, `man` and the `--version` text are recorded in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
