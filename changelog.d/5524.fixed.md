- **jq: a possessive quantifier is refused instead of answered wrongly** (#5524). Oniguruma reads `*+`, `++`, `?+`, `{n,}+` and `{n,m}+` as possessive (the repetition never gives characters back), and the `regex` crate as a quantifier on a quantifier, so `"aaa" | test("a*+a")` was `true` here and `false` in jq, `[match("a++a").offset]` was `[0]` against `[]`. It now fails with `invalid regex: regex parse error: possessive quantifier is not supported` (exit 5) unless the quantifier is the last character of the pattern, where nothing can fail after it and it is rewritten to the plain quantifier (`sub("a*+";"X")` is unchanged). `{n}+` and `a*?+` are a repeat of a repeat in jq as well and are untouched; yq mode already refused the form.
