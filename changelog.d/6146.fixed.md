- **jq: `input` past the end of a `--seq` stream names the position jq names** (#6146). `printf '\x1e1 \x1e' | jq -c --seq '[.,input]'` says `(at <unknown>): break` in jq and said `(at <stdin>:0)` here; a stream with no value at all (`\n\n\n\x1e` under `-n`) names its newline count (`<stdin>:3`) where it said `<stdin>:0`. The rule is the plain-JSON one (#4303), applied to the values the `--seq` reader yields: the position is lost when the read that reached the end of input completed the last value. A sweep of 4,662 runs (1,554 streams, each through `[.,input]`, `-n [.,input]` and `-n [.,input,input]`) against jq 1.7.1 went from 533 differences to the 30 that end in a fault after the last value (#6137's family).
