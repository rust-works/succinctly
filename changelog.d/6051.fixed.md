- **yq: a `-0` expression literal prints `0` in JSON output** (#6051). In yq a `-0` literal is the integer zero: its text stays `-0` for `tostring` and YAML output, and its JSON encoder prints `0`. `succinctly yq -o json` echoed the text, so `-0`, `[-0]`, `{"a": -0}`, `.a |= -0`, `to_json` and `@json` of one printed `-0`, and `-0 | . as $i | .[$i] | path` (a `-0` index bound to a variable) printed `[-0]`. A document's `-0` is a float in both tools and still prints `-0.0`.
