- **yq: a comment block that a blank line splits from the next one, after a nested block sequence, is the foot of the key that owns the sequence, as in yq** (#5053). `printf 'j:\n  b:\n    steps:\n      - run: x\n      # t1\n\n      # t2\n' | yq .` prints `# t1` at `steps`' column (`    # t1`) and `# t2` at column 0; succinctly printed `# t1` at the item's column (`        # t1`, the foot of `run`). It is the walk outward from the block's own column to the nearest enclosing mapping, as `positional_foot_target` does for a block that is not split. A Windows-authored GitHub Actions file hits it at the end of the file, where every CRLF comment line is a block of its own (`rust-1.12.yml` of the `vcpkg` crate matches yq now). Against yq v4.53.3 on 1,008 generated documents (nineteen sequence and mapping shapes, one to three comments, every blank-line gap, LF and CRLF): 60 more match, none that matched before differs; on 1,500 random documents (LF and CRLF): 7 more match, none that matched before differs. Not fixed: the other open shapes listed on #5053.
