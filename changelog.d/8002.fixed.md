- **yq: a literal or `error(...)` after a stage that matched nothing runs on the right side of `|=` and in `map_values`, `with_entries`, `map` and `[...]` over a computed value, as in a pipe** (#8002).
  yq answers a constant over an empty context (`select(false) | 3` is `3`, #6943), and `succinctly yq` already did in a plain pipe, but an update skipped its target instead: `.[0] |= (select(false) | 3)` over `[1, 2]` left `[1,2]` where yq writes `[3,2]`, and `.[] |= (select(. == 5) | error("x"))`, `with_entries(select(false) | error("x"))` and `map_values(select(false) | error("x"))` never raised. The right side of `|=` (and `+=`, `with(...)`, `map_values`) is now asked per target, `with_entries(f)` per entry, and `map(f)` or a collect once over its members, so `to_entries | map(select(false) | 3)` is `[3]`. `succinctly jq` keeps jq's rule (`.[0] |= (select(false) | 3)` is `[2]`). Rows captured from yq v4.53.3 across the document, JSON, `-n`, `--eval-all` and `-i` routes.
