- **`succinctly jq --seq -s` follows jq's reads of a directory given last** (#6676). Under `-n`,
  a failure is now the reads' to reach: a program stopped by a fault in the stream exits 5 with no
  failure said (it exited 2 and printed `Is a directory`), the slurp's array read counts a last
  directory (exit 2 where it was 0), and the read after the one that reaches it says `Is a
  directory`, also after a stream with a fault at its end. Without `-n`, a stream warned of at its
  end (`\x1e[1,`) no longer runs the program after a directory that jq's main loop has stopped at
  (it printed `Is a directory`, `break` and an empty array that jq does not). `--seq -s --stream`
  without `-n` is not covered yet.
