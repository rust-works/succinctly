- **jq: `--seq -s --stream` and `-n -s` put the fault a record left open ends in at the last file that did not open** (#6788). A program that reads `input` raises that fault, and jq has it standing at the missing file, line 0: `jq --seq -s --stream input trunc pa missing` says `jq: error (at missing:0): Unfinished JSON term at EOF ...` where succinctly said `(at <unknown>)`, and `input_filename` after the fault is `"missing"`, not `null`. `'[., input]'`, `'[inputs]'` and `-n -s --stream '[inputs]'` are the same. A directory last, a plain `.` and `--seq -s --stream-errors` are unchanged.
