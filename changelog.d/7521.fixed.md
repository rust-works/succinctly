- **jq: `tonumber` refuses a string with a NUL byte in it** (#7521). `"1\u00002" | tonumber` was `1` and `"12\u0000abc" | tonumber` was `12`, because the text was cut at the NUL as in jq 1.7.1; now they raise `string ("1\u00002") cannot be parsed as a number`, and `tonumber?` is empty, as in jq 1.8.2 (jqlang/jq#3496), which upstream fixed. The difference from the pinned 1.7.1 is recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
