- **jq: only the first `^` after a class's `[` negates it** (#6193). The scan kept the class at its start through every `^`, so in `[^^]z[]` the `]` after the second `^` was read as a literal and the class ran on, and an unterminated class after a `[^^]` was accepted where jq refuses it (`"aab" | test("[^^]z[")` was `false` and is an error). Generated classes with leading carets against jq 1.7.1 went from about 80 differences per 8,000 to none.
