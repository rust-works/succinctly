- **jq: `--slurpfile` and `inputs` read plain JSON in one pass** (#6110). A stream of plain RFC 8259 values
  separated by white space (`--slurpfile d FILE`, `reduce inputs as $u (...)` over ndjson) is read straight into values by the
  reader `fromjson` already used (#5675), not validated by `serde_json`, semi-indexed and walked again; anything
  else (a leniency such as `007` or `nan`, a repeated key, a lone surrogate, nesting past 64 levels, a fault)
  takes the route it took before, so every error and its position are unchanged. On 10 MB inputs on an AMD
  Ryzen 9 7950X `--slurpfile` over ndjson goes from 0.71x to 1.24x of jq 1.7.1, `--slurpfile` over a users
  document from 0.77x to 1.25x, and `reduce inputs` over ndjson from 0.72x to 1.20x.
