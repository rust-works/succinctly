- **jq: `--stream` and `--stream-errors` over a terminal named by its path (`/dev/pts/N`, `/dev/ttysNNN`, `/dev/stdin` on a terminal) keep its first bytes** (#7740).
  The 3-byte probe's handle was kept only for a FIFO or socket, and a terminal reopened and seeked back went on after the probed bytes, so `{"a":1}` and `[2]` on two lines gave `jq: parse error: Unfinished string at EOF at line 3, column 0`, where jq 1.7.1 prints the four events.
