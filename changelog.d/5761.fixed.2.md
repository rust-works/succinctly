- **jq: a fold that adds or defaults its loop element onto a `null` start keeps the element** (#5761). `[nan] as $a | reduce $a as $v (null; . += $v) | . == $a`, `. //= $v` over a `null` or `false` start, and the `foreach` spellings of both are `true` in jq (`null + x` is `x` itself, and jq's own `add` is `reduce .[] as $x (null; . + $x)`) and were `false` here, because the owned fold step only combined two values of one kind and handed everything else to the evaluator, which rebuilt the element. The step now stores the operand as the state; a truthy state is left alone by `//=`, a second element still adds into a new container, and `succinctly yq` is unchanged. `(.x, .y) += $v`, `(.x += $v)?`, `.x = $r.items` and a trailing identity stage after the store (`. += $v | .`) still rebuild it.
