- **yq: an assignment's right side, and an `and`/`or`/arithmetic operand, now re-emit from a
  context-free stage the way yq does** (#2588). Under yq's read-only context an absent key yields
  no node, but the first literal, `[...]`, `{...}`, operator or comma that follows it re-emits a node
  of its own and every later stage runs on it: `.x = (.a.zz | true)` is `x: true` (was unchanged),
  `.x = (.a.zz | true | not)` is `false`, `.x = (.a.zz | (true, false))` is `false`,
  `(.a.zz | 1) + 1` is `2` (was `1`) and `(.a.zz | (true, false)) and true` is `true false` (was
  `false`). #2540 modelled only the pipe's last stage and only `and`/`or`; the classifier is now
  `yq_empty_context_reemit`, a rewrite shared by `=`/`+=`/…, `and`/`or` and the arithmetic and
  comparison fanout in both evaluators. jq mode is untouched. Still open: a re-emitting stage inside
  `[...]`, under `|=`, and as the left operand of a parenthesised `//` (see
  `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`).
