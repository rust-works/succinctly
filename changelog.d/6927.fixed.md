- **yq: an explicit `!!int 08` keeps its integer across the reindex bridge** (#6927). A zero-padded decimal with an 8 or a 9 is the float `8.0` of a plain `08` and the integer `8` of an explicit `!!int 08`, and the bridge wrote both under the same spelling token, whose reader (rightly, since #6866) reads the text `08` as the float. So `k: !!int 08` printed `{"k":8.0}` under a write (`.zz = 1`), `.k = .k`, `with_entries(.)` and in `tojson`, and its `tag` still said `!!int`. The integer is now written under its own token (`5` where the spelling token has `6`), decoded back to the integer literal it was, so all four print `8` as yq does. Checked on 8 rows against yq v4.53.3 (every one of which agrees), pinned in `tests/yq_cli/i6899.rs`, with the entry in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md` and the temporary pin test removed.
