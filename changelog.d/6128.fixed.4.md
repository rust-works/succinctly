- **jq: a lazy `{n,}?` repeat over a body with a consuming way after its empty one is read as Oniguruma reads it, by the body's size** (#6128). Oniguruma writes `X{n,}?` out as `n` plain copies when the body's compiled length times `n` is at most ten, and as a counted loop that leaves at the body's empty way otherwise. The rewrite applied the counted-loop form to both, and gave up past a count of three when the body held a Unicode class, which `\w` is: `"ab" | [match("(?:b?|\\w){2,}?"; "gn") | .string]` printed `["ab"]` where jq 1.7.1 prints `["a","b"]`, and `"ab" | [match("(?:b?|\\w){4,}?"; "gn") | .string]` printed `["a","b"]` where jq prints `["ab"]`. Over 7,098 generated `{2,}?`, `{3,}?` and `{4,}?` patterns, the 259 that differed now agree, and 20,328 patterns across the repeat kinds, with 1,500 random bodies beside them, agree with jq; a count of seven or more over a body of a few operations is still given up (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`).
