- **jq: a slice right after `[.. | parent?]` no longer re-indexes the whole array** (#3824). `[.. | parent?] | .[0:2] | length` is an array holding `n` shared copies of the document, and the slice stage was not one the owned navigation peel (`projection_peel`) took, so the reindex bridge wrote the array out in full (quadratic in its size) to read two elements: 3,611 M, 13,953 M and 54,518 M instructions and 121 MB, 594 MB and 1,620 MB peak over 1,200, 2,400 and 4,800 inner arrays on an Apple M5 Max. A leading slice is now taken natively, with the same `slice_owned_value_read` every other owned slice uses (arrays, strings, `null`, and yq's object rule; a target it cannot slice still goes to the bridge for its error): 242 M, 453 M and 871 M instructions and 21 MB, 32 MB and 55 MB, the cost of `[.. | parent?] | length` itself. On a 7950X (wall-clock, min of 5, interleaved) 210 ms, 763 ms and 3,004 ms become 18 ms, 35 ms and 68 ms. Output is identical to the previous build across 38,896 slice/tail/value/wrapper combinations in both modes. `first` and `last` after the same array stay quadratic (#4068).
