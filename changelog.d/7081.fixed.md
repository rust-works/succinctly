- **yq: `to_entries`, `from_entries`, `with_entries` and `to_entries | from_entries` keep what yq prints before the document** (#7081). yq gives the result of these operators the document's leading content (the comments above it and an explicit `---`) and its root's head comment, so `printf '# c\n---\na: 1\n' | yq to_entries` prints `# c`, `---` and then the entries; `succinctly yq` dropped all three. They are carried under `-P`, `-N`, `-I`, `-0`, `-C` and `--no-doc`, for a mapping, a sequence and an empty result, and not for a step into the document first, a `map` between the two operators, or a source that starts with a byte-order mark. 763 rows from yq v4.53.3 are pinned in `tests/yq_cli/i7081.rs`. A `%YAML` directive, `--eval-all`, `-o=kyaml` and the same result through a construction (`{"x": .} | .x`) still differ.
