- **jq: a loop with capture groups beside a lookaround, an atomic group or a comment answers as Oniguruma's does** (#6426). `"ba" | [match("(?=b)((a|b)|){2,}";"g") | .captures | map(.string)]` printed `[["a","a"]]` and jq 1.7.1 answers `[["","a"]]`, and `"ba" | [match("(?=b|a)((?:(b|)*|a)+)*";"g") | .string]` printed `["ba"]` where jq answers `["b",""]`. The loop and capture rewrites now read such a pattern through the plain copy of it, keep the construct as written, and run the result on `fancy-regex`; a text with a lazy repeat between two greedy repeats of one expression (`b+a*?b*`) is kept off it, because `fancy-regex` 0.20 makes the lazy repeat greedy. Over three draws of 4,000 generated patterns of `scripts/jq-regex-capture-loop-sweep.py --fancy`, 353 patterns that differed from jq now agree (363 differed on seed 3, 10 still do) and none changed the other way; the plain, `--nest`, `--ways`, `--assert`, `--bounded`, `--newlines` and `--flags gx` draws broke none.
