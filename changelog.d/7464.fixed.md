- **`succinctly jq` reads an inline `(?x)` in a pattern with a possessive quantifier** (#7464). `"aab" | test("(?x) a*+ b")` is `true` and `[match("(?x) a*+a"; "g")]` is `[]`, as in jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2, where both were refused with `possessive quantifier is not supported`: the rewrite of a possessive quantifier into an atomic group (#6172) now follows the `x` flag as inline groups set it (`(?x)`, `(?-x)`, `(?x:...)`) instead of refusing every pattern that names one.
