- **jq: a repeated group of a zero-width item and a bare option group is read as jq reads it** (#6221). `"cab" | match("(?:\\G(?m))*";"g")` is four empty matches in jq and was `invalid regex` here, as were `(?:\\K(?m))*`, `(?:\\Z(?m))*b`, `(?:(?=a)(?i))*` and `(?:\\G(?:(?m)))*`: Oniguruma reads the body as a sequence of two nodes, a valid target, where `fancy-regex` refuses a repeat of a body that is only zero-width. Such a group is now rewritten as the atomic group of its body, as #6164 and #6187 did for an option group; `(?:\\K)*` and `(?:(?m)\\Z)*` (an option group first encloses the rest) stay refused, as jq refuses them. A pattern with an inline `(?x)` or an alternation in the body keeps its old answer.
