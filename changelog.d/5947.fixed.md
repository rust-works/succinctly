- **`strptime` reads an empty field and a signed year as jq does** (#5947). `%Y`, `%y`, `%M` and `%S`, and those fields inside `%R`, `%T` and `%D`, read nothing at the end of the input or before white space and succeed, so `"" | strptime("%Y")` is the zeroed time and `"10:30:" | strptime("%T")` reads, where both were no match. `%s` with no digits reads 0 and consumes nothing. `%Y`, `%y` and `%C` take a sign that uses one character of the field's width (`"-5" | strptime("%y")` is 1995, `"+" | strptime("%Y")` is year 0), and a week number in a negative year is placed with jq's libc's own arithmetic. A month outside 1-12 or a day past 31 is refused as it is read, so a later `%s` no longer hides it (`"13" | strptime("%m%s")`).
