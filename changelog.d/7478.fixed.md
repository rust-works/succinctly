- **jq: an input named as a pipe (`<(cmd)`, a FIFO path, `-`, `/dev/stdin`) is read as standard input is** (#7478).
  A lone such source was read to its end before the program ran, so `-n 'first(inputs)'`, `-n input`, `halt` and `-R -n input` over a producer that never ends its stream grew until killed, where jq 1.7.1 answers at once.
  The lazy `input`/`inputs` route now starts on its front, and the plain filter and `-R` read it a window at a time, as for an unnamed pipe on standard input.
