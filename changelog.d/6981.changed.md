- **yq: `/=`, `%=` and `//=` are refused unless `--jq-extensions` is passed** (#6981). yq v4.53.3 has no such operators: it reads `/`, `%` or `//` with a missing operand and stops (`'/' expects 2 args but there is 1`), where `succinctly yq` took them for jq's compound assignments and wrote `{"a":2.0}`, `{"a":0}` or `{"a":4}` for `.a /= 2`, `.a %= 2` and `.a //= 3` on `a: 4`. They now fail with a parse error naming the flag, as a jq-only builtin does (#1512); with the flag, and in `succinctly jq`, they behave as before. `+=`, `-=`, `*=` and `|=` are yq's own and are unchanged. Found by a grammar differential fuzz of yq-mode programs against yq.
