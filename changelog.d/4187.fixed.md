- **jq: a `foreach` source that destructures the register behind a plain bind (`. as $x | . as {a:$a} | .`) or through a `?//` chain of destructuring patterns (`. as {a:$a} ?// [$a] | .`) no longer answers the root where jq raises** (#4187, a #4128/#4150 residual). `del(foreach (. as $x | . as {a:$a} | .) as $y (.; .; .))` on `{"a":false,"b":null}` raises `Invalid path expression with result` in jq and deleted the document here (`null`), `(...) = 9` replaced it (`9`) and `path(...)` answered `[[]]`; `del(foreach (. as {a:$a} ?// [$a] | .) as $y (.; .; .))` on `{"a":[1]}` raises `Cannot index object with number` and also deleted it. The destructure's step moves jq's register onto the matched member and the emitted `.` is no longer there; the source was driven by value because the routing predicate stopped at the plain bind and never read its body (a plain bind's source is a subexpression, so the body meets the register where the bind did), and read a `?//` chain as left to the by-value drive. Both are routed through the resolver now (a chain with a bare `$var` alternative stays by value, where it matches jq). The 24 plain-bind register-sweep operands (159,867 rows) go from 1,579 wrong accepts and 211 wrong answers to 203 and 34, the 12 chain operands (79,947 rows) from 1,114 and 156 to none; 142 and 188 rows that matched jq now refuse. A `(.|.) as {...}` source and a destructure of a bound `$x` are still accepted where jq raises, identical on `main`.
