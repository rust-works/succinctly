- **yq: a block scalar whose `|`/`>` header is on a line of its own reads its text** (#4259). With the header below the `-` or `key:` that owns it (`-` / `  >` / `  text`, or `- # note` / `  |` / `  text`), content indented no more than the header itself was cut off: the item read as `""` and, under a mapping key, the text split into a stray `text: b` entry, losing the text on every read and write. The content is now measured against the parent block, as YAML and yq do; the same-line form (`- >`) is unchanged.
