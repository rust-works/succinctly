- **jq: a backreference compares under the case mode in force where it stands** (#6196). `"aA" | [match("((?i)a)\\1";"g")|.string]` printed `["aA"]` (the `(?i)` ended with its group, so jq 1.7.1 answers `[]`), and `[match("(?i)((?-i)a)\\1";"g")|.string]` printed `[]` where jq answers `["aA"]`. A pattern with a backreference and an inline `i` option now writes each backreference as `(?i:\1)` or `(?-i:\1)` for the mode at its position. Over 2,598 generated patterns and strings, jq and `succinctly jq` agree on all (94 differed).
