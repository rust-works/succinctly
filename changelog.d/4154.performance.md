- **jq: a scalar crossing the owned-evaluator bridge no longer builds a full JSON index** (#4154).
  `OwnedValue::reindexed` serialized a one-token document and ran the general index build
  over it, whose balanced-parentheses directories alone are eight `Vec`s, so `del(.)`,
  `paths(.)` and `del(.[]?)` over a scalar paid 16-18 allocator calls per member. A scalar
  root's index follows from its length, so it is now written directly: 8-10 per member
  (`[.[] | del(.)]` over 2,000 integers: 32,024 -> 16,024 calls). Interleaved A/B, min of 15,
  output identical: `[.[] | del(.)]` over 100k-1M integers -24% to -26% on a Ryzen 9 7950X and
  an M4 Pro (flat across sizes), 300k strings -25% / -21%; a container-rooted control
  (`[.[] | del(.a)]` over 100k objects) -1.3% / +1.1%, within noise.
