- **yq: a line below an own-line block scalar under a dash with properties is refused when it falls between the dash and the content, as yq refuses it** (#6550). `- &x` / `  |` / `    t` / `` / `  u` is `did not find expected '-' indicator` in yq v4.53.3; here `["t\n"]` came out and the line `u` was dropped without an error. The same held for `- !!str`, `- &x # c`, `- - &x` and a header deeper than the dash. The header of such an item was read as a plain scalar of the item, whose text the value layer then re-read as a block scalar that ends at the first line at less than the content's indent; a header on a line of its own below `- k:` or `k: &x` was already read as a block scalar, and `- |` refused the same line. A line that is a dash with nothing after it but node properties (`-`, `- -`, `- &x`, with or without a comment) now counts as leaving its value for the header below it, as a key with properties does, so the header takes the own-line block scalar path and its check of the lines below. A differential of 8,664 documents (a dash with properties, a bare dash, a chain of dashes and a key as the line above; `|`, `>-`, `|2` and `>+` headers at columns 0 to 5; a line below at columns 0 to 5 with and without a blank line before it) against yq v4.53.3 left 136 differences, every one of them the explicit indentation indicator of `|2` under a dash chain (#6490, recorded in the limitations page), none in whether a document is accepted. The grids of #6540, #6543 and #6553 stay at 0, 0 and 10 (all `|2`, #6553). The same line below a dash with properties that follows an explicit key's `:` or `?` (`: - &x`) is still dropped (#6565).
