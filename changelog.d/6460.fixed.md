- **jq: a lazy repeat between two greedy repeats of one expression stays lazy on the `fancy-regex` route** (#6460). `"bab" | [match("(?=b)b+a*?b*";"g") | .string]` printed `["bab"]` and jq 1.7.1 answers `["b","b"]`: `fancy-regex` 0.20 merges `X+ M*? X*` into `X+(?:M+?X*)?`, which makes the lazy repeat greedy. A lazy repeat that can match nothing is written as `(?:M*?|(?!))` there, which the optimizer does not merge and which means the same. Only the lazy repeat in the shape is written so, and the extended mode is read when the leading switches turn it on. Over 3,584 generated patterns of that shape (`scripts/jq-regex-capture-loop-sweep.py --lazyrun`: `+`/`*` on both sides, 4 lazy forms, 7 atoms, under a lookaround, a repeat or a group), 1,675 that differed from jq agree, under `g` and `gx`, and none changed the other way.
