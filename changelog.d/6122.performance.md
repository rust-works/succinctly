- **jq: a path-free `|=` update no longer materializes the document twice** (#6122). `.users |= .` and `.users[0].name |= "x"` took the wildcard bridge, which wrote an owned copy of the whole document out as text and indexed it again before running the update. They now go through the same owned door as `=`, `+=` and `//=`, on the one copy, and answer as before (67 update shapes diffed against jq 1.7.1 on stdout, stderr and exit code). On a 100 MB `users` document, min of 11 interleaved runs against the commit before: 1.73 s to 1.50 s on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (0.93x to 1.07x of jq 1.7.1) and 1.09 s to 0.95 s on an Apple M4 Pro (1.90x to 2.18x of jq 1.7.1-apple), -13% on both; `.users[].age |= .+1`, `.users[0].name = "x"` and `.users |= map(.age)` are within ±1% at 100 MB. `.users |= map(.age)`, the shape the issue opened with, is not fixed by this: it stays at 0.65x (7950X) and 0.71x (M4 Pro) of jq, because its time is the filter's per-element field lookup and the owned copy it runs on, not the second materialization. yq mode and an update whose body needs a path context keep the bridge.
