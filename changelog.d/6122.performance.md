- **jq: a variable-free `|=` update no longer materializes the document twice** (#6122). `.users |= .` and `.users[0].name |= "x"` took the wildcard bridge, which wrote an owned copy of the whole document out as text and indexed it again before running the update. They now run on the owned identity pipe, on the one copy, and answer as before (67 update shapes diffed against jq 1.7.1). On a 100 MB `users` document, min of 11 interleaved runs against the commit before: 1.73 s to 1.50 s on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (0.93x to 1.07x of jq 1.7.1), 1.08 s to 0.95 s on an Apple M4 Pro (1.90x to 2.18x of jq 1.7.1-apple), both -13%; `.users[].age |= .+1`, `.users[0].name = "x"` and the 10 MB documents are within ±1% or scale the same. `.users |= map(.age)`, the shape the issue opened with, is unchanged at 0.65x (7950X) and 0.71x (M4 Pro) of jq: its time is the filter's per-element field lookup, not the copy. yq mode and an update that binds a variable or calls a definition keep the bridge.
