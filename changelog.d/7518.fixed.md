- **jq: `last(f)` of an `f` that yields nothing yields nothing** (#7518). `[last(empty)]`, `[last(range(0))]` and `[[] | last(.[])]` were `[null]`, `[last(empty)] == [first(empty)]` was `false`, and `path(last(.[]))` over an empty array raised `Invalid path expression with result null`; now they are `[]`, `true` and no path, where jq 1.7.1 answered the `reduce`'s `null` seed. `succinctly jq` follows jq 1.8.2, which fixed this (jqlang/jq#3179). The difference from the pinned 1.7.1 is recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md` and as the `syntax:man-last-empty` ledger entry.
