- **jq: a `try` around a nested `reduce`/`foreach` over a frozen `$v` no longer swallows the fold's refusal** (#3984, #4008). `del(. as $x | length | try (reduce 1 as $i ($x; .a)))` raises in jq (`reduce 1 as $i ($v; .b)` in a `foreach` EXTRACT too), and `del(foreach .a as $v ([3,1,2]; (($v | .b?), 1); try (foreach 1 as $i ($v; .; .b))))` deletes `.a.b`. A fold mentioning a frozen `$v` runs against the register the enclosing stage left in place (`length` and a literal do not move it), which `$v` may be by pointer; the refusal of its first navigation was read as exact, the `try` caught it, and the write or path was silently dropped at exit 0. It is now the loud guess every other site makes, so the program refuses (exit 5) where jq writes, and agrees with jq's exit 5 where jq raises. On the nested-fold grid (2,673 rows) wrong answers fell from 441 silent and 252 different to 0 and 0, 399 more rows match jq, and 42 rows that matched by accident (a `del` of an absent key) now refuse.
