- **jq: a broken-down time of fewer than 8 fields is normalised before `strftime`, `strflocaltime`, `todate` and `todateiso8601` format it, as in jq 1.8.2** (#7606). Such an array, which jq 1.7.1 refuses and #7532 reads as jq 1.8.2 does, had its fields formatted as given, so `[2024,5] | todate` was `"2024-05-31T00:00:00Z"` in jq 1.8.2 and `"2024-06-00T00:00:00Z"` here, and `[2024,2,15] | strftime("%a")` was `"Sun"` for a Friday. A full 8-field array is still formatted as given, as jq 1.7.1 does (jq 1.8.2 normalises it too, as a by-product of jqlang/jq#3203's zone fix); the difference is recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`. Also: `strflocaltime("%s")` of a reading that is `-1` seconds from the epoch as UTC printed `-1` instead of that reading's instant in the local zone (`TZ=Europe/Paris`, `[1969,11,31,23,59,59,3,364] | strflocaltime("%s")` is `"-3601"` in both jq releases).
