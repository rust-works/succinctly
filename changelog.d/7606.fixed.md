- **jq: `strftime`, `strflocaltime`, `todate` and `todateiso8601` normalise a broken-down time before formatting it, as jq 1.8.2 does** (#7606). The fields were formatted as given, as in jq 1.7.1: `[2024,0,1,0,0,0,0,0] | strftime("%a")` was `"Sun"` for a Monday, `[2024,1,30,0,0,0,0,0] | todate` was `"2024-02-30T00:00:00Z"` (now `"2024-03-01T00:00:00Z"`), `[2024,13,35,25,61,61,0,0] | todate` was `"2024-14-35T25:61:61Z"`, which jq 1.7.1's own `fromdate` refuses, a short array (#7532) printed a date neither release prints (`[2024,5] | todate` was `"2024-06-00T00:00:00Z"`, now `"2024-05-31T00:00:00Z"`), and `-1.0000000000000002 | todate` was `"1969-12-31T23:59:60Z"` (now `"1970-01-01T00:00:00Z"`). `strflocaltime` reads the fields as local wall-clock time, as `mktime` does. A consistent broken-down time and every other number print as before; the difference from the pinned jq 1.7.1 is recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`. Also: `strflocaltime("%s")` of a reading that is `-1` seconds from the epoch as UTC printed `-1` instead of that reading's instant in the local zone (`TZ=Europe/Paris`, `[1969,11,31,23,59,59,3,364] | strflocaltime("%s")` is `"-3601"` in both jq releases).
