- **yq: a field write through `(PATH[] | select(...)).x` over a document without `PATH` adds `PATH: []` as yq does** (#6902). `(.spec.template.spec.containers[] | select(.name == "web")).image |= "x"` over a stream of Kubernetes manifests raised `Cannot iterate over null (null)` and exited 1 on every document that was not a Deployment, where yq leaves the document with `containers: []` added. `=`, `|=` and the arithmetic assignments are covered.
  The raise stays for a comma among the targets and for an iteration after the select (`(PATH[] | select(...)).x[] = 5`), both recorded in the yq limitations.
