- **jq: a stage that never reads `.` no longer re-indexes the value it is handed** (#4053). `. as $x | .users[] | [$x] | 1` serialized and indexed the whole array `[$x]` at every element only to evaluate the literal `1` (the `{r: $x}` twin never did), so it was quadratic in the document: 58 M, 2,173 M and 19,434 M instructions over 6 KB, 64 KB and 196 KB `users` documents on an Apple M5 Max. A stage built from literals, arrays and objects of them, and the operators over them (`1`, `"s"`, `{a: 1}`, `[1, 2]`, `1 + 2`) is now answered from the expression alone, as an owned-assign right side already was (#3138): 37 M, 55 M and 96 M instructions on the same three documents, and 3 ms -> 2 ms, 100 ms -> 3 ms and 919 ms -> 7 ms wall-clock on a 7950X (min of 5, interleaved), with identical output in both modes (880 stage/wrapper pairs against the previous build). A stage that reads `.` (`type`, `keys`, `not`) still takes the bridge.
