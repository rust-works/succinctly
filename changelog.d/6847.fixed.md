- **yq and the YAML parser: a compact mapping after `? ` or `: ` takes a zero-indented block sequence as its key's value** (#6847). `? a` / `: k:` / `  - 1` is `{"a":{"k":[1]}}` in yq and was refused here with `a sequence cannot follow a completed mapping entry`, as were `? k:` / `  - 1`, `- ? k:` / `    - 1` and `a:` / `  ? k:` / `    - 1`: valid YAML, which the same shapes under a plain key (`a:` / `  b:` / `  - 1`) or a dash (`- k:` / `  - 1`) already read. The check that refuses a sequence after a completed `key: value` entry (#4530) read the previous line's key without walking past the `?` or `:`, so it took the marker's column for the key's and judged the entry complete. It now reads the key both ways, past the dashes only (a bare `:` line is the value indicator that leaves its value below it, YAML test suite 6PBE) and past the `?` and `:` as well. Checked against yq v4.53.3 on 31 pinned documents (the last eight are refusals that stand) and on 6,500 random explicit-key documents (five shapes, nested keys, dashes, properties, flow and block values): 2,006 newly agree, none that agreed before differs, none of the 6,500 differs from yq now.
