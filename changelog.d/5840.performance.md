- **jq: `empty`, `error(...)?`, `select` spelled as an `if`, and `.k // empty` no longer take the reindex bridge under `--stream` and `-R`** (#5840).
  Each input was written out as JSON, indexed and read back for a program holding one of them, about 340 ns per event that `select(c)` did not pay.
  They are now answered from the input itself. Over a 40 MB `users` file with `--stream`, `empty` takes 0.58 s instead of 1.21 s on an Apple M4 Pro and 0.78 s instead of 1.53 s on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X, `if length==2 then . else empty end` 0.97 s instead of 1.85 s (M4 Pro) and 1.24 s instead of 2.55 s (7950X), and `.[1] // empty` 0.73 s instead of 1.53 s (M4 Pro) and 0.95 s instead of 2.10 s (7950X).
  `-R` over 50 MB of lines is 45-65% faster on `empty`, `error("x")?` and the `if` spelling on both chips.
  Output is identical to before and to jq 1.7.1, and `select(c)` itself is unchanged within noise.
