- **jq: `mktime`, `strftime`, `strflocaltime` and `todate` read a broken-down time as jq 1.8.2 does** (#7532). A NaN field was read as 0 (`[2024,0,1,0,0,nan,0,0] | mktime` was `1704067200`, `nan | todate` was `"1970-01-01T00:00:00Z"`), an array shorter than 8 was refused (`[2024,0,1] | mktime`), and a year past the C `int` range wrapped (`[-1e20,0,1,0,0,0,0,0] | strftime("%Y")` was `"2147483648"`), as in jq 1.7.1, whose `jv2tm` casts each field unchecked. `succinctly jq` now follows upstream's fixes (jqlang/jq#3070, #3162): NaN is `<name> requires parsed datetime inputs`, missing fields are 0 (`[2024,0,1] | mktime` is `1704067200`), and each field saturates to `int` after the year has had 1900 taken off (`[1e20,0,1,0,0,0,0,0] | mktime` is `67768036160140800`). The difference from the pinned 1.7.1 is recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
