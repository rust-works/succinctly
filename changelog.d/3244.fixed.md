- **yq: `del()` of a variable that holds the root keeps the document** (#3244). `. as $y | del($y)`, `del(. as $y | $y)`, `del(($y))`, `del($y | .)` and `del(. as $y | ($y, $y))` printed nothing and exited 0, silently dropping the document, where yq v4.53.3 prints it unchanged (`{"a":1}` on `a: 1`, and `true`/`null` on those scalars); so did `.a as $y | .a | del($y)` on a null or boolean `a`. yq's bare `del(.)` empties the document, but a variable holds a node and the root node has no parent to be removed from. The shapes yq does empty (`del(.)`, `del(select(true))`, `. as $y | $y | del(.)`) are unchanged, as is jq mode (`del(. as $y | $y)` is `null`). A variable bound below the root, and the order-dependent mixes such as `del(., $y)`, are the node-identity semantics of #2643 and are untouched.
