- **jq: a `?//` in a builtin's argument inside `map(f)`, `with_entries(f)` or `walk(f)` retries the next alternative, as jq does** (#7602). `["x"] | map(startswith([1,"x"] as [$a] ?// [$b,$a] | $a))` raised `startswith() requires string inputs` where jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 answer `[true]`, and `to_entries | map(has(...))`, `with_entries(...)` and `walk(...)` over such an `f` raised likewise. Such an `f` now runs under jq's own definitions of the three builtins, where the builtin's refusal of the first alternative retries the bind.
