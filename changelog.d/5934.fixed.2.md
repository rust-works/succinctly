- **`succinctly jq` `strptime` counts a `%j` beside a month from the month's start** (#5934). `"2015 03 050" | strptime("%Y %m %j")` is now `[2015,2,-9,0,0,0,4,49]` (it was `[2015,2,0,0,0,0,6,58]`, the zeroed day and the yearday of the month read), the day passing 31 or going below 1 and the year not rolling, as in jq 1.7.1. A day read beside `%j` with no month takes the month from the day of year (`"2015 19 050" | strptime("%Y %d %j")` is February 19). Where the day read disagrees with the day of year jq aborts on its own assertion, so there is no answer to match.
