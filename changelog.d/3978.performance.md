- **jq: a `reduce` assignment under an `as` bind no longer copies its accumulator every step** (#3978). `$r.name as $n | .[$n] = $r.score` and `$r as $s | .[$s.name] = $s.score` were quadratic because `Expr::As` was not an assignment, so `owned_assign_step` declined and the whole object was re-copied per record. A closed bind source (one value, no read of `.`) is now substituted into the body and the write happens in place; nested binds, a shadowing rebind and an outer `//` recurse. Measured on an Apple M5 Max (release, interleaved A/B, min of 3, 5,000 / 15,000 records): `$r.name as $n | .[$n] = $r.score` 0.89 s / 7.91 s → 0.03 s / 0.07 s, `$r as $s | .[$s.name] = $s.score` 1.35 s / 11.92 s → 0.03 s / 0.08 s, the control `.[$r.name] = $r.score` unchanged at 0.02 s / 0.06 s. Output is identical to jq 1.7.1 on every row. Pinned by `fold_assign_step_copies_nothing_3138` (zero forced copies for both shapes; 1,000 `make_mut` without the change).
