- **jq: `log`, `log2`, `log10`, `exp`, `exp2`, `exp10`, `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `asin`, `acos`, `atan`, `sinh`, `cosh`, `tanh`, `asinh`, `acosh` and `atanh` of a number no longer write it out and index it again** (#5669, #5593 follow-up). `floor`, `ceil`, `round`, `trunc`, `sqrt` and `fabs` were answered from the number since #5593 and #5601; the other eighteen libm builtins still met each number through the reindex bridge. They are now the same libm call the builtin makes (`src/jq/math.rs`), for a document number and for one the filter computed, so no digit moves on any platform. A value that is not a number, a non-finite one and yq mode keep the evaluator's route. Over a generated `numbers` document (Apple M4 Pro, interleaved min of 7, 10 MB, jq 1.7.1): `[.numbers[]|log]|add` 0.65x -> 0.99x of jq, `sin` 0.64x -> 0.95x, `atan` 0.66x -> 1.00x, `log2` 0.65x -> 0.98x, `exp` 0.58x -> 0.92x, `tanh` 0.85x -> 1.57x and `log|floor` 0.95x -> 1.80x; on an Apple M5 Max `log` 0.53x -> 0.97x. `pow` and `atan2` take two operands and are not covered.
