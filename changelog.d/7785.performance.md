- **jq: `contains` of a bound container searches it in place** (#7785).
  `[range(n)] as $a | reduce range(k) as $i (0; if $a|contains([$i]) then . + 1 else . end)`, its `foreach`,
  `until`, `limit` and `[range(k) as $i | $a|contains([$i])]` forms, `$o|contains({k: [$i]})`, a bound string's
  `contains("\($i)")`, and the same calls over a document, an `input` or a `--argjson` value wrote the bound
  container out and indexed it again for every call, because neither the in-place fold step nor the owned
  evaluator's doors had a `contains` stage for a needle other than a string literal: the time grew with the
  container's size on every call, where jq searches the value in place and stops at the first match. A
  `contains` whose needle never reads `.` now asks the evaluator's own search of the bound value; a needle of
  another kind keeps the evaluator's route and its containment error, and the answers are unchanged.
