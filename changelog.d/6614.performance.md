- **jq: a document nested 41 to 64 levels deeper than a record is no longer read twice as slowly as a shallower one** (#6614).
  The conversion of a document into a value tries a one-pass text reader on every child of a container, and
  that reader gave up at 64 levels after descending all the way to the limit, so the walk paid for the failed
  attempt again one level further down at every level above the 64th. It now reads to the validator's 128
  levels. Over 1 million nodes in chains 40, 50 and 60 nodes deep (80 to 120 levels),
  `[.[] | recurse(.children[]?)] | length` went from 1.83, 1.82 and 1.76 s to 1.17, 1.15 and 1.14 s
  (jq 1.7.1: 1.18-1.23 s, so from 0.65x to 1.03-1.05x of it) and `[.[] | tojson | length] | add` from 1.01 s to
  0.46 s (jq: 1.58 s; AMD Ryzen 9 7950X, interleaved, median of 5). Documents up to 30 nodes deep (60 levels)
  and `map(.id)` are unchanged. A document nested more than 128 levels (jq 1.7.1 itself refuses one past about
  180) reads 6-8% slower than before: the reader's failed attempts now go deeper before they stop.
