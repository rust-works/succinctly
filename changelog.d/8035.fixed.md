- **yq: `ireduce` keeps folding once its accumulator list is empty** (#8035). yq's `reduceOperator` runs UPDATE over the whole accumulator list for every source node, even an empty one, and a collect, a literal, the loop variable or an `error` answers over an empty list: `.[] as $x ireduce (select(false); [.])` over `[1, 2]` is `[[]]` in yq, `.[] as $x ireduce (select(false); $x)` is `2` and `.[] as $x ireduce (select(false); error("u"))` is `Error: u`, where `succinctly yq` stopped folding and printed nothing. It now answers as yq does, with `-n`, over each document and under `--eval-all`; an UPDATE that reads no loop variable and answers nothing over the empty list still only reads the rest of the source. `reduce` under `--jq-extensions` shares the fold (`reduce .[] as $x (empty; [.])` is `[[]]` there); jq's `reduce` is unchanged. A `//` over an empty context now answers only when its left side does, as in yq: `.a[] | ((1 | select(false)) // 5)` over `a: []` printed `5` and prints nothing, while `.a[] | (null // 5)` is still `5`.
