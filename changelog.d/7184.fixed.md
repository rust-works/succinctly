- **yq: `and` and `or` under `--eval-all` read both operands over the whole document list** (#7184). `yq ea '(.k and .k)'` over four documents of which three have `k` printed `true true true false`; yq prints `true` nine times, because its `and` and `or` are cross-function operators like `+` and `==`: with every context node flagged `EvaluateTogether` they read both sides over the whole list, read-only (an absent key is no node), and pair every left result with every right result. A left result that decides (falsy under `and`, truthy under `or`) is the answer and the right side is not read for it, a left side with no output stands as one `null` (`.zz and 1` is one `false`, `.zz or 1` four `true`), and a right side with no output pairs `false` once. An error in the right side is raised only when an undecided left result reads it. 26 rows are pinned against yq v4.53.3 in `tests/yq_cli/i7184.rs`; without `--eval-all` nothing changes.
