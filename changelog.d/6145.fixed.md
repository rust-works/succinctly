- **jq: `input_line_number` is 0 after a read past a slurped value, as in jq** (#6145). Under `-s`, `printf '1\n2\n3\n' | jq -c -s '[input_line_number, (try input catch .), input_line_number]'` is `[3,"break",0]` in jq and was `[3,"break",3]` here; so is `-n -s '[inputs] | [input_line_number]'` (`[0]`, was `[3]`), and the same holds over `--seq` and `-R`. The slurp had read the stream to its end to build its one value, so the first read past it is already one past the end. Checked against jq 1.7.1 over 182 rows of `-s`, `-n -s`, `-R`, `--seq` and `--stream` with one to two files and empty files: 54 differed before, none after.
