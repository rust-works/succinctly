- **jq: a loop with capture groups answers as Oniguruma's does beside a flag switch written in the pattern, and in a `{n,}` of a non-capturing group of several ways** (#6358, part of it). `"bab" | [match("(?:(a)?|(?i)b)*";"g") | .string]` printed `["","ab",""]` and jq 1.7.1 answers `["","a","",""]`; `"aab" | [match("(?:(a)|()){2,}") | .captures | map(.string)]` printed `["a",null]` where jq answers `["a",""]`, because the empty third iteration ends the loop and sets the second group. The loop rewrite scopes a switch over the items and ways it reaches and takes its empty way from them (a way that is only a switch too), and the capture rewrite reads a counted repeat of a group that holds several groups as the `*` loop it is. Over four draws of 5,000 generated loops with `(?i)`, `(?s)`, `(?m)` and `(?-i)` among the atoms, 297 patterns that differed from jq now agree and none changed the other way; five draws of the existing sweep fixed 52 more. A loop inside another repeat, a leading assertion over a counted repeat and a lazy bounded repeat that leaves a group unset still differ (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`).
