- **jq: `sort`, `unique`, `group_by`, `min` and `max` leave NaN ties in jq's order** (#6008).
  jq orders a NaN before every number and before another NaN, and settles the tie by input position; `succinctly jq` reversed such ties, so `[{"a":nan,"b":1},{"a":nan,"b":2}] | sort` came out `b:2` first, `[3,1,2] | sort_by(nan)` was `[2,1,3]`, and `[3,1,2] | min_by(nan), max_by(nan)` was `3` and `2` where jq answers `2` and `3`.
  `sort`, `sort_by`, `unique`, `unique_by` and `group_by` now keep NaN-keyed items in input order, `min`/`min_by` take the last of a run of NaNs and `max`/`max_by` the first, in both evaluators and in path mode.
  A grid of 13 arrays by 16 builtins differed from jq in 65 of 208 probes and matches in all 208; the scan for a NaN that gates the tie rule is one pass over the keys of a sort.
