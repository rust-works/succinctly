- **yq: `with(PATH; del(...))` deletes** (#6840). `with(.a; del(.x))` left the document unchanged, and an assignment piped after the delete (`with(.a; del(.x) | .z = 1)`) was dropped with it, because the delete was taken for a value-producing stage whose result yq discards. A `del(...)` or `delpaths(...)` in the update is now an in-place write like an assignment, alone, in a comma or a pipe, behind a `select(...)` or a binding, as yq v4.53.3 does for the 22 captured rows pinned in `tests/yq_cli/i6840.rs`. `with(.b[]; del(.))` (which empties the array in yq) is still a no-op here.
