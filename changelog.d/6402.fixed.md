- **yq: a `%` directive line inside a document, and a `%`, `@` or backtick that starts a plain scalar, are refused as yq refuses them** (#6402). `printf 'a: 1\n%TAG ! x:\n' | succinctly yq .` printed `{"a":1,"%TAG ! x":null}` and yq stops with `did not find expected <document start>`; `!!str` / `%YAML 1.2` read as the string `%YAML 1.2` where yq refuses it, and `a: %x`, `- @x`, `[a, %x]` and a key `%k:` were content too (go-yaml starts no plain scalar on `%`, `@` or a backtick, and reads a `%` at column 0 as a directive wherever it stands). A `%` that continues a plain scalar or stands in a quoted or block scalar is still content, as in yq. Over four draws of 1,500 to 2,000 generated streams, 760 that differed from yq in whether they are refused now agree and none changed the other way. The indented `%YAML` of a stream's head and a directive after a `...` marker still differ (`docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`).
