- **jq: `-S` and `-a` apply to the line `debug` and `debug(msg)` write to stderr** (#5558). jq 1.7.1 writes `["DEBUG:",v]` with its dump options minus the indent flags, so `jq -S 'debug'` sorts the keys of `v` and `jq -a 'debug'` escapes non-ASCII as `é`. `succinctly jq` ignored both unless `-C` was also given (#5553) and always wrote plain compact JSON. The CLI now installs its output formatter for the line under any of `-C`, `-S` and `-a`, so the bytes match jq's, number spellings (`1.50`, a literal past a double) and a NaN (`null`) included. With none of the three, under `-M` alone, and in `succinctly yq`, nothing changes. Pinned against jq 1.7.1 in `tests/jq_cli/i5558.rs`.
