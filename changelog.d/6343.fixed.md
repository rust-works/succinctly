- **yq: a scalar, alias or flow line level with a mapping's keys or a sequence's `-` markers, with no value waiting for it, is refused as yq refuses it** (#6343).
  `a: |⏎  x⏎stray` kept `{"a":"x\n"}` and dropped `stray`; `k: &a⏎q` dropped `q`; `? k⏎q` and `a:⏎"q"` read the line as a value; `a: |⏎"q"⏎b: 1` read a key and a value that are in no document; yq stops with `while scanning a simple key` for all of them. A block scalar header at that indent (`- &x⏎|`) stays a node of the entry above it, as in yq. Of 2,500 random small documents compared with yq, 55 that were read here and refused by yq are now refused too, and none that yq reads is newly refused.
