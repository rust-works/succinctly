- **jq: a container where an object key belongs is met at the token after it by the plain reader, as jq does** (#5655). `printf '{[1]\n2\n3' | jq -nc '[limit(6; repeat(try input catch "E"))]'` is `["E",3,"E","E","E","E"]` in jq, because its lexer takes `[1]` as a value and meets the fault at the `2` on the next line; this build met it at the `[` and read `2` and `3` as documents. A fault inside the container is still met first, and `--stream`, whose parser refuses the bracket itself, resumes where it did.
