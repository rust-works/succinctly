- **`succinctly jq` `strptime` resolves `%C`, `%y` and `%Y` in the libc's order, and `%n`/`%t` need white space** (#5934). `"2015 20" | strptime("%Y %C")` is year 2000 (it was 2015), `"20 85" | strptime("%C %y")` is 2085 (it was 1985), and `"2015" | strptime("%Y%n")` is a mismatch (it read), as in jq 1.7.1. `%j` next to a month and the `%z` offset stay different.
