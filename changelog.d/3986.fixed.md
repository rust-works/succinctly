- **`succinctly yq` prints no `---` between the results of `head_comment`, `line_comment`, `foot_comment` and `alias` of a document root** (#3986). Over `a: 1` / `---` / `a: 2`, yq prints these values (a string made from the node's own comment, or the alias's target) with no separator, since a computed result belongs to no document; they printed one here, as every result did. The getters of a navigated node (`.a | head_comment`) keep it in both tools, as do `tag`, `anchor`, `style`, `kind` and `length`. A probe of 990 rows (6 streams, 33 filters, 5 output flags) against yq v4.53.3 fixed 114 and changed none that already matched. `.a, head_comment`, `map(head_comment)` and `.. | alias` are not part of this fix.
