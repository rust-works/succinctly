- **`succinctly yq` writes a key given as a literal block scalar back as an explicit key** (#7032). `? |-` / `  a` / `  b` / `: "1"` printed as `"a\nb": "1"`; it is now written as yq writes it, `? |-` with the value on the `:` line (`: c: "1"`, `: - x`, `: [1, 2]`), at every nesting and under every `-I`, with the anchor and tag it carries and the indentation indicator and chomping its text needs. A key of one short line is double-quoted, as in yq, and one over 128 bytes keeps its block. `-p props` input with a `\n` key prints the same form. 31 rows from yq v4.53.3 are pinned. Folded keys, `keys`/`to_entries` over such a key and `-P` over an anchored one are recorded in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
