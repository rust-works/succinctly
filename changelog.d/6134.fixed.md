- **jq/yq: an alternation whose branches start with the same repeat takes its first branch's match** (#6134). `regex-syntax` factors a shared prefix out of an alternation (`a*a|a*b` became `a*[ab]`, rust-lang/regex#1400), which changes the leftmost-first match when the prefix can match more than one way: `"aab" | match("a*a|a*b")` was `"aab"` and is now jq's and yq's `"aa"`, and `"abbc" | sub("ab*b|ab*c"; "X")` was `"X"` and is now `"Xc"`. A lazy repeat of such an alternation (`(?:a*a|a*b)+?`) now leaves after the first branch's match. Patterns with lookaround, backreferences, `\K` or an atomic group are covered too; only a conditional (`(?(1)...)`) keeps the factored match. On a generated-pattern differential against jq 1.7.1, 882 of 80,000 cases were fixed; the 11 that changed from matching jq to differing are loops whose body can match nothing and holds a capture group (one also a lazy `{2}?`), the empty-iteration shapes #6106 leaves alone, which matched before only because the factoring happened to cancel that difference out (#6069, #6128).
