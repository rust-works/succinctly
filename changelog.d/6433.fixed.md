- **jq: a repeat count past Oniguruma's 100000 is refused wherever jq reads it** (#6433, part of it). `"a" | test("(?=a{100001})")` and `test("a{100001 }")` answered, and `test("{100001}")` said `target of repeat operator is not specified`; jq 1.7.1 says `too big number for repeat range` for all three, because it refuses a count the moment it reads one after a `{`, whatever follows it or surrounds it. The count is now also looked for in the pattern's text, which covers a look-around or an atomic group (the `regex` crate's parser does not read them), a blank before the `}` and a repeat with nothing to repeat. The other rows of the issue (`\g<n>` calls, `(?'x'` names, an unmatched `(` after a `\k`) are still worded by the crate.
