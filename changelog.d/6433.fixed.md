- **Regex refusals #6423 left in the crate's words are now jq's** (#6433). A repeat count past 100000 is `too big number for repeat range` wherever its brace stands, as Oniguruma lexes it: inside a look-around or an atomic group (`(?=a{100001})` was answered `false`), in a brace that never closes (`x{100001`) and with nothing before it (`{100001}`), while an error before the brace still comes first. A call `\g<n>` to a missing group is `undefined group <n> reference`, `\g<0>` and `\g<-1>` before any group `never ending recursion`, and an error after `(?'name'…)` or a reference to a group the pattern has is found (`(?<x>a)\k<x>(` is `end pattern with unmatched parenthesis`). Over 7,500 generated patterns, about a third moved to jq's answer and none moved away from it.
