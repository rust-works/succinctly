- **yq: an error that ends a collected walk no longer converts the outputs delivered before it** (#5505). A yq document answers all or nothing, so nothing reads them, but a walk such as `recurse(.[]?; cond)` whose `cond` raises late (`--jq-extensions`) turned each node it had delivered into an owned copy of its whole subtree to carry as a partial prefix. Over 60,000 integers nested 120 levels deep, `[recurse(.[]?; if . == 59999 then error("x") else true end) | 1] | length` takes 63 ms in 15 MB instead of 578 ms in 136 MB on an Apple M4 Pro (9.2x; 40 levels 4.4x, 80 levels 7.1x) and 80 ms in 19 MB instead of 842 ms in 140 MB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (10.5x; 4.9x and 8.0x), and 240,000 integers 9.2x and 10.7x, the cost no longer growing with the depth. The same walk that does not raise is unchanged (30.6 ms and 38.7 ms), and so is every answer, including those of `first`, `limit`, `isempty`, `any` and `all` over a walk that raises after their last output and of a `try` or `?` that reads the outputs before the raise. A `break` or `halt` keeps the prefix, and so does jq mode (`jq` prints what came before an error), which the walk reaches by another route.
