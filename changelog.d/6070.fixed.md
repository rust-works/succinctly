- **`succinctly jq` reads Oniguruma's escapes the way jq does, and refuses what it refuses** (#6070). A backslash and a letter that means nothing there is the letter (`"h" | test("\\h")` was an error, and `"A" | test("\\u0041")` was `true` where jq, which reads `\u0041` as the text `u0041`, says `false`), `\N`, `\O` and `\Q…\E` work, `\101` and `\0` are octal, `\cA` is control-A, `\xc3\xa9` is `é`, and `(*FAIL)` never matches. An interval with nothing to repeat (`{1}`), a group name that starts with a digit or is empty, a short multibyte escape and a backreference or conditional on a group the pattern lacks are errors, as in jq: `"a" | test("(?(2)a|b)")` panicked inside `fancy-regex` and now fails cleanly. `\X`, `\y`, `\Y`, `\g<n>` and octal escapes of 0x80 or more stay refused. Every row was captured from jq 1.7.1.
