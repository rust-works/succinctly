- **`succinctly yq` keeps a sequence's style when the other operand of `+` is null, and appends to each sequence of a comma of paths in its own first element's style** (#7070). `.a + null`, `.a + .absent` and `.a * null` over `a: ['dog']` are `['dog']` as in yq v4.53.3 (a block sequence's comments stay too), where they printed a block sequence: yq answers the left node itself. `(.a, .b) += "z"` and `(.a, .b) |= . + "z"` over `a: ['dog']` and `b: ["p"]` are `['dog', 'z']` and `["p", "z"]`, where each printed a plain `z`. A fuzz of 2,000 random documents and expressions against yq fixed 692 cases and changed none that already matched. An appended number (`+= 5`, yq's `'5'`), `[] + ["z"]` and `.c = .a + null` are not part of this fix.
