- **perf(jq): `INDEX` no longer rebuilds a temporary document from its array** (#5306). `.users | INDEX(.id)` ran `.[]` over the array it already held as a value, which built a temporary document from the whole array and decoded every element out of it again; an array's elements are now taken directly, and a plain `.name` key is read in place from an object row. Interleaved A/B (base vs head, output identical, control run -0.7%/-0.2%): wall time on the `users` file at 100 MB falls by 34% on an M4 Pro (2.04 s to 1.33 s) and 43% on a 7950X (3.88 s to 2.22 s), 30-38% at 1 MB and 10 MB, and the `| length` form by 42% / 49%; peak RSS at 100 MB falls from 1.58 GB to 0.85 GB (M4 Pro, jq 0.85 GB) and from 1.72 GB to 0.97 GB (7950X, jq 0.76 GB). On the 7950X `INDEX` was 0.62x of jq and is now 1.09x. yq mode and a computed key expression take the previous path.
