- **jq: a look-behind is sized with jq's classes and braces, under the `x` flag and without it** (#7887, #7876). `"KkK" | test("(?x)(?<=[[ab]]|ab)")`, `"x" | capture("(?<=(?i)a{0}|(a))"; "x")` and `"a b" | test("(?<=[[ ]]|a b)"; "x")` answer in jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 (`false`, `{}`, `false`) and were `Regex failure: invalid pattern in look-behind` since #7865 made the look-behind checks run under `x`; without `x` they had been refused all along. The checks read the pattern with a parser that takes three things differently from jq's Oniguruma, and now read them its way: a `[` inside a class is a member, since jq's classes do not nest (`[[ab]]` is the class `[[ab]` and a `]`, two characters, so `(?<=[[ab]]|ab)` is accepted and `(?<=[[ab]]|a)`, which was answered, is refused); a brace that opens no interval is the characters it is (`"abc" | test("(?<=a{0 })")` is `false`, where the body was taken for one that matches nothing and the answer was `true`; `(?<=[^a]\w{1, 2})b` is answered and `(?<=a{x}|bcd)` refused, the two rows of #7876); and an option run in a branch that is sure to match nothing (`(?i)a{0}`) makes the body no look-behind, as a branch written empty does. Three refusals of jq's are made with them: a `\z` or `\Z` in a look-behind the shape rules read (`(?<=a\z)`), a look-behind that can match nothing and is repeated (`(?<=a|)+` is `target of repeat operator is invalid`), and, where the whole pattern is matched without regard to case, a class that holds a character whose folding is longer than itself (`"a" | test("(?<=[[:alpha:]])"; "i")`). Over 420,000 generated patterns against jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2, 2,624 rows that differed from jq now match and no row that matched differs. Not fixed here: under `x`, a group in a look-behind that can match nothing keeps its text (`"ab" | capture("(?<=(?<n>a)|a{0})b"; "x")` is `{"n":"a"}`, `{"n":null}` in jq), the first item of #7887, which predates #7865. Everything runs when a pattern is compiled.
