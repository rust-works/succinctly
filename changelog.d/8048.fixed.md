- **yq: a `-` with no left operand is refused, as in yq** (#8048). yq has no unary minus, only `Subtract`, so `-(1)`, `-.a` and `1 | -.` are a subtraction missing an argument, which yq v4.53.3 refuses with `'<op>' expects 2 args but there is 1` (exit 1), naming the outermost operator whose right side holds the `-` (`'|'` for `1 | -.`, `','` for `.a, -.b`) or the `-` itself when nothing stands before it; `succinctly yq` negated. It now refuses them with the same words, under `--jq-extensions` too. A negative number (`-1`, `.a | -1`, `1 - -1`) and a subtraction (`1 -.a`) are unchanged, and `succinctly jq` keeps jq's unary minus (`-(1)` is `-1`).
