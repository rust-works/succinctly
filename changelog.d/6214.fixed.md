- **yq: a tab after U+2028, U+2029 or U+0085 is refused below the block indent, in a flow collection and in block context, as after a newline** (#6214).
  `a: [x⏎⇥y]`, `a: x⏎⇥y` and `- x⏎⇥y` with a Unicode line break were read as the text `x y`; go-yaml ends a line at those characters as at `\n`, so the tab at the start of the next line violates indentation (`found a tab character that violates indentation`), as #6059 made it after `\n`.
  The break stays scalar content that folds as before.
  The column rule is the one #6059 applies: a tab after enough spaces, a scalar or collection at the document root and a quoted scalar are still read, as in yq.
  The empty-key host (`: [x⏎⇥y]`) is not changed: yq refuses every `: value` with no `?` before it, tab or not (#6288).
