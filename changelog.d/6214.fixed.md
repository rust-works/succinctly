- **yq: a tab after U+2028, U+2029 or U+0085 inside a flow collection is refused below the block indent, as after a newline** (#6214).
  `a: [x⏎⇥y]` with a Unicode line break was read as `["x y"]`; go-yaml ends a line at those characters as at `\n`, so the tab at the start of the next line violates indentation (`found a tab character that violates indentation`), as #6059 made it after `\n`.
  The break stays scalar content that folds as before.
  The block-context rule after such a break (`a: x⏎y` is an error in yq) and the empty-key host (`: [x⏎⇥y]`) are not changed.
