- **jq: whitespace after a `.` no longer joins it to the next token** (#5447). jq reads `.` and `.name` as separate
  tokens, so `. a`, `. true` and `.a. b` are syntax errors there while `. .a`, `. ."a"` and `. .["a"]` are the identity
  with a suffix. `succinctly jq` skipped the gap and read the name across it, so it accepted the first three and
  rejected the rest (`. .["a"]` was read as `..["a"]`, answering `Cannot index array` where jq answers the value).
  Checked against jq 1.7.1. `succinctly yq` is unchanged: whitespace after a dot has its own meaning there (#798, #4237).
