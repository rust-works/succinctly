- **jq: an `[E]` collect as a branch of a compound stage keeps the path register, as in jq** (#4152). `succinctly jq 'path(. as $x | 5 | (select(.), [numbers]) | $x)'` raised `Invalid path expression with result {...}` where jq 1.7.1 prints `[]` once per output, and the same held for a collect under `//`, `if` and a parenthesised pipe, and through `del`, `=` and `|=`.
