- **yq: an entry of `{...}` that yields nothing is skipped, not the whole object** (#4193). `{"a": (1|select(false)), "b": 2, "c": 3}` is `{"b": 2, "c": 3}` in yq; `succinctly yq` built the cross product of the entries, so one empty key or value printed nothing at all. A construction whose keys and values are not all provably a single output now runs as yq's `COLLECT_OBJECT` (the route a bare entry already took), which also reproduces its neighbour-dropping quirks (`{"a": 1, "b": (1|select(false)), "c": (3,4)}` is `{"c": 3}` and `{"c": 4}`). A construction of plain paths, literals, arithmetic and `[...]` keeps the fan-out, and jq is unchanged. An error inside such a construction now aborts it with no prefix, as yq does.
