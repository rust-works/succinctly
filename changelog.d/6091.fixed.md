- **yq: `upcase` and `downcase` map the Unicode simple case mapping, as yq does** (#6091). They were jq's `ascii_upcase`/`ascii_downcase` under yq's names, so `ágUA | upcase` was `áGUA` where yq prints `ÁGUA`, and `ΑΣ | downcase` stayed as it was where yq prints `ασ`. They now map each character as Go's `strings.ToUpper`/`ToLower` do: `ß` has no single-character uppercase and stays, `İ` lowercases to `i`, the Greek letters with an iota subscript (`ᾀ`) uppercase to their titlecase letter (`ᾈ`), and a case pair Unicode 16 added (`ɤ`/`Ɤ`, `ƛ`/`Ƛ`, the Garay letters) maps neither way, as Go's tables are Unicode 15. Compared with yq v4.53.3 over every printable code point of the basic and supplementary planes, in both directions: identical. jq's `ascii_upcase`/`ascii_downcase`, and `succinctly yq --jq-extensions` `ascii_upcase`, stay ASCII.
