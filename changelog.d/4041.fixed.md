- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that is a pipe stage after a comma with a navigating sibling, a bare `abs`, or a neutral builtin inside an `if`, an array or a `catch` handler no longer skips the write silently** (#4041, a #4028 residual). `del(foreach .a as $v (0; (($v|.b?), now) | floor; try ($v | .b?)))` on `{"a":{"b":1,"c":[1,2]},"z":0}` is `{"a":{"c":[1,2]},"z":0}` in jq and was the unchanged document (exit 0) here: the builtins navigate nothing, so jq's register is still on `.a` when EXTRACT runs, but the pipe stage after the comma was resolved without the comma's per-sibling register statement, and `abs`, `if`, `[...]` and `try ... catch` were not read at all, so the `try` swallowed the refusal into a skipped write. A neutral tail is now pushed into the siblings of the comma it follows (`(A, B) | F` is `(A | F), (B | F)` in jq's own evaluation order), `abs` joins the register-neutral builtins, and an `if` (its branches), an array (its contents) and a `try ... catch` (its body and handler) are neutral when what runs in them is; `=`, `|=`, `del` and `path` follow jq. Not changed: a navigating element inside an array (`[$v|.b?]`) and `min`/`max`/`ltrimstr`/`rtrimstr` still refuse or skip as before.
