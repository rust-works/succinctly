- **jq: a `(?m)` `^` inside a look-around no longer holds at the end of a text after a final newline** (#6502).
  #6456 wrote each multi-line `^` as Oniguruma reads it, but only where `regex-syntax` could parse
  the pattern, which a look-around is not: `"a\n" | [match("(?m)(?<!^)";"g") | .offset]` was `[1]`
  where jq 1.7.1's is `[1,2]`. The pattern is now read through its plain copy, which holds the `^`
  at the same offset; that reading is shared with the group-copies rewrite (#6426). A differential
  of 1,500 `(?m)` look-around patterns on 8 subjects per seed, against `main`, fixes 83-94 and breaks none.
