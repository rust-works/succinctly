- **jq: `--seq -s --stream` no longer says a parse warning to a program that only asks `input_line_number` where its read stands** (#6774). With a `--seq` record left open (`\x1e[1,`) running on into a file, then a directory last, `jq --seq -s --stream input_line_number trunc pa dir` printed `jq: ignoring parse error: Unfinished JSON term at EOF` where jq prints nothing: `input_line_number` reads no input, so the unfinished term is never handed to it, as it is not to `.` or `input_filename`. `[., input_line_number]` and `[., input_line_number, input_filename]` are the same. A program that calls `input` or `inputs` is handed the term and keeps its answer. 6 of 39,312 rows of a sweep over eight programs, six flag sets and every list of up to three files moved to jq's answer and none moved away.
