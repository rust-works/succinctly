- **`succinctly jq` reads its command line as jq does: `--name=value` and an unknown bundle letter are refused, and a bundle is read a letter at a time** (#7438). clap's grammar ran `--indent=3`, `--args=`, `--jsonargs=1` and `--arg=x`, which jq refuses with `Unknown option` and exit 2, and refused `-V1`, which jq answers with the version. argv is now walked as jq's own option loop walks it, and every refusal is jq's `jq: Unknown option <word>` with its usage hint, exit 2. Short-option bundles follow jq 1.8.2's in-order parser (jqlang/jq#3194, a fix of 1.7.1's): `-nn`, `-ee` and `-nL lib` work, `-fprog.jq` names its first bad letter (`Unknown option -p`), and of `-hV` and `-Vh` the first letter wins. `--binary` is accepted outside Windows, as jq 1.8 fixed it to be (jqlang/jq#3131). A succinctly-only option keeps its `=` form (`--on-error=stop`). Over 3,211 generated long-option and bundle shapes, the rows where jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 agree on stdout and exit status but `succinctly jq` did not fell from 17 to 8 (all 8 are `--validate`, a succinctly extension), and the `Unknown option` refusals whose first line differs from jq 1.8.2's fell from 759 to 15 (extension options and a missing option value, which keep clap's words).
