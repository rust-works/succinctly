- **yq: a block scalar header to the left of the key it would be the value of is refused, as yq refuses it** (#6540). `- - k:` / `  |` / `    t` (the header at the second dash's column, left of `k` at column 4) is `did not find expected '-' indicator` in yq v4.53.3 and was read here as `[[{"k":null},""]]`, a null-valued key beside an empty item; the same held for `>`, `|-` and a property after the colon, with one to three dashes in front of the key, and for a header at column 0 under `- k:`. A header on a line of its own is now a value slot only when it stands at the key's column or to its right (`- k:` / `  |` is still the key's value), the key's column being the line's indent plus the `- ` markers it opens with. A differential of 2,556 documents (nested compact keys behind one to three dashes, under a parent mapping, with and without a property, comments, `|`/`>`/`|2` headers at every column) against yq v4.53.3 found 100 accept-versus-refuse differences on the first grid before the change and none after, and no document yq reads is refused. Bare-dash chains and a `: - k:` entry after an explicit `?` still accept headers yq refuses (#6543).
