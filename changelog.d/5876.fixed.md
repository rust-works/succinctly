- **`succinctly yq` answers a value the program builds past 384 levels, as yq does** (#5876). `.[] as $i ireduce (0; [.]) | length` over 385 or 2,000 items is `1` (it was `Error: nesting depth exceeds limit of 384`), and the value prints as YAML, `-o json` and `-P` byte for byte as yq prints it. A walk over such a value now checks the native-stack floor once per level, as `succinctly jq` has since #5554, and refuses past it with `nesting depth exceeds limit of the native stack`. A value built to 257-384 levels and read back out of a fold (`{"a": .}` folded 300 times, then `.a | length`) no longer hits the 256-level document ceiling.
