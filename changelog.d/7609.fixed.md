- **jq: an undefined name inside the arguments of an undefined call no longer shifts the cited position of a later error** (#7609). `def d0: ua(ub(1));` then `ub(2)` reported `ub/1 is not defined` at the `ub(1)` of line 1 (and echoed that line), where jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 cite the `ub(2)` of line 2; the same held for a variable or a `break` inside the arguments, and for `ua(ub(1)),` then `ub(2)` in the main program. jq never compiles such arguments, so they are still not diagnosed, but their sites are now counted.
