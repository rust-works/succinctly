- **yq: an empty `{}` after a fan-out is one mapping, as in yq** (#6801). `.x[] | {}` over `x: [1, 2, 3]` printed three `{}`; yq builds the empty mapping once however many nodes reach it (a `[]` and every other literal are still one per node), so it now prints one, and so do `(.x[], .x[]) | {}`, `.x[] | ({}, {})` and `.x[] | select(. > 5) | {}` (an empty context). `.x | map({})` still answers one per element, where yq's is `[{}]` (limitations).
