- **yq: a property in front of a block sequence `-` on its line, and a tab after the `-`, are refused as yq refuses them** (#6414, #6379 follow-up). `&t - x`, `- &t - x`, `a: &t - x`, `- !!seq - x` and `? &t - x` are `block sequence entries are not allowed in this context` in yq and were the sequence `["x"]` here (`&t - a: b` was `{"- a": "b"}`, the key `- a`); `-⇥x`, `- ⇥x` and `-⇥# c` are `found character that cannot start any token`, where go-yaml skips no tab in the place of a simple key, and were read as the item. A property on a line of its own above the `-` is read as before, and so is a tab after the item's content. Both checks are made in yq mode only (the library loader reads YAML 1.2's way, and `key: - a` with no property still reads as the sequence, #6355), through one helper that the explicit-key site shares with `parse_sequence_item`. Over 518 generated documents the 409 that differed from yq in whether they are refused now agree, and a sweep of 2,308 valid documents near the rules changed none the other way.
