- **jq: a `foreach` source that destructures the register behind a stage that hands it on (`select(true)`, `first(.)`, `limit(1;.)`, `try`, `label`) now raises as jq does in path position** (#3956, a #3940 residual).
  `path(foreach ((.|select(true)) | foreach . as {a:$a} (0;.;.)) as $x (.;.;.))` on `{"a":1,"b":2}` answered `[]` where jq raises "Invalid path expression with result {"a":1,"b":2}", and a write
  through it replaced the document; on `null` jq answers `["a"]` and this answered `[]`. #3940 taught the source predicates to skip a stage that is syntactically the register (`.`, `(.|.)`, `(., .)`); a stage that merely
  forwards it hid the destructure from the resolver, so the by-value drive answered. `select` with a literal condition (it cannot raise, and emits the register or nothing) and the wrappers
  `try`/`?`/`first`/`limit`/`nth` and `label` are now read the same way, in `foreach_source_destructures_register` and `yields_only_the_register`. A `select(f)` whose condition navigates is left to the by-value drive (its condition can raise). jq mode only.
