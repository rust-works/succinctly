- **`path()`/`del()`/`=` catch a navigation off the register a routed `foreach` moved, as an `and`/`or` operand** (#3938). A `foreach` emission now states the register its source's pattern walk left even when it holds a container, so `del(. as $x | ((foreach (. as {$a} | .) as $k (.; .; .)) and (.a | .b)?) | $x)` answers like jq instead of refusing with an uncatchable path error.
