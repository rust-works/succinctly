- **cli: `succinctly jq` and `succinctly yq` start faster, because they parse their own arguments, not every subcommand's** (#7149).
  clap's derive builds the argument definitions of every subcommand (`dsv`, `json`, `bench`, `dev`, ...) before it matches the one that was typed, and drops that tree at exit; with the `sjq`/`syq` aliases already skipping it, the spelled-out form now does too.
  `jq -n 1` takes 0.979 ms against 1.127 ms on a Ryzen 9 7950X (-13.1%) and 2.136 ms against 2.253 ms on an Apple M4 Pro (-5.2%), and runs 1,083,465 instructions against 1,590,091 (-31.9%, callgrind); `jq .` over a small file, `--help` and `yq -n 1` move the same way, and `succinctly dsv --help` does not (interleaved, 21 samples of 50 spawns, an A/A control that moved 0.5% at most).
  Help, version, bad flags, missing values, usage errors and runs give the same stdout, stderr and exit status as before (79 argument vectors compared against the previous binary).
