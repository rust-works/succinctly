- **jq: `.numbers | sort` retires 6% fewer instructions** (#7316).
  The `SpelledScalar` arm that #7251 added to `jq_kind` called `jq_kind` again on `kind.canonical()`, and a function that calls itself is not inlined, so the kind of each value became an out-of-line call inside every comparison of the sort (399 M of the 6,421 M instructions of `.numbers | sort` on the 10 MB `numbers` document).
  The arm now maps the kind directly and output is unchanged. `sort_by`, `min`, `max`, `unique` and `group_by` compare through the same `sort_rank`; only `.numbers | sort` was measured.
  Instructions, #7251's merge against the same merge with this change: 6,421 M to 6,037 M on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (cachegrind; 6,024 M before #7251), 5,957 M to 5,429 M on an Apple M4 Pro (`time -l`; 5,385 M before #7251).
  Wall-clock, min of 15 interleaved runs: 372.3 ms to 358.0 ms on the 7950X (356.7 ms before #7251) and 248.9 ms to 242.2 ms on the M4 Pro (239.0 ms before #7251).
