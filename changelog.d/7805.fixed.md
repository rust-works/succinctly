- **A regex group name is accepted or refused as jq does** (#7805). The first character of a `(?<name>…)` or `(?'name'…)` must be a word character other than an ASCII digit, as Oniguruma's `fetch_name` reads it: `(?<(>x)` and `(?<€>x)` are `invalid char in group name <…>` and `(?<-a>x)` and `(?<+a>x)` `invalid group name <…>` (exit 5), where they were accepted, while `(?<²a>x)` and `(?<‿a>x)` are still accepted. Every later character up to the closing delimiter belongs to the name and is no pattern text, so `"a" | test("(?<a[>a)")` is `true` instead of `premature end of char-class`, and `(?<a$>x)` and `(?<a{>x)` report the groups `a$` and `a{`. A `)` or the end of the pattern before the delimiter is `invalid group name` with Oniguruma's cut of the name (`(?<ßx` is `<ß>`), and `(?<` alone is `end pattern with unmatched parenthesis`, all as in jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2.
