- **jq: a `?//` destructuring bind or a `foreach` whose INIT moved the register no longer answers where jq raises a path error, so `|=` and `del` can no longer write through it** (#4150). `(try (((foreach ((., .) as {a:$a} | .) as $x (.; .; .)))) catch .) |= 9` on `{"a":[true]}` printed `9` (the root overwritten) and `del(try ((. as [$q] ?// $z | (.a?, null)) or .[0]))` on `[{"a":1}]` echoed the document, where jq 1.7.1 raises `Invalid path expression` (exit 5). Three causes. A non-last `?//` alternative whose body refused was not retried, and the refusal came back as an ordinary error that an enclosing `try`/`?` swallowed, though jq retries the next alternative and checks what it yields at the end of the path, outside the `try`; it is now the uncatchable guess when a later alternative would yield a value (so `try (. as [$q] ?// $z | .a)`, which jq answers `[]`, still does). A pattern that fails by value (`0 as [$q]`) is a certain retry in jq, and now counts as one. A `foreach` whose INIT navigated and then computed (`(.a|tostring)`, `(. as {a:$a} | .)`) ran its SOURCE against a register the equal root paths said it was still at, and a `(., .) as {..}` source was not routed to the resolver. Over 735 sweep operands (the 40,000-row targeted sample, seed 21) `ACCEPT_WRONG` went from 75 to 8 and `DIFF` from 27 to 16, and over the whole grid (20,000 rows, seed 11) from 14 to 0 and 18 to 15, with no row moving toward a wrong answer; 20 rows jq answers (contrived `?//` binds under `try`/`?` whose later alternative yields) are now refused, in the safe direction.
