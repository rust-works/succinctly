- **jq: a `?//` destructuring bind or a `foreach` whose INIT moved the register no longer answers where jq raises a path error, so `|=` and `del` can no longer write through it** (#4150). `(try (((foreach ((., .) as {a:$a} | .) as $x (.; .; .)))) catch .) |= 9` on `{"a":[true]}` printed `9` (the root overwritten) and `del(try ((. as [$q] ?// $z | (.a?, null)) or .[0]))` on `[{"a":1}]` echoed the document, where jq 1.7.1 raises `Invalid path expression` (exit 5). Four causes. A non-last `?//` alternative whose body refused was not retried, and the refusal came back as an ordinary error that an enclosing `try`/`?` swallowed, though jq retries the next alternative and checks what it yields outside the `try`; it is now the uncatchable guess when a later alternative would yield a value, so `try (. as [$q] ?// $z | .a)`, which jq answers `[]`, still does. A pattern that fails by value (`0 as [$q]`) is a certain retry in jq, and now counts as one. A `foreach` whose INIT navigated and then computed ran its SOURCE as if the register were still at the entry. A `(., .) as {..}` source was not routed to the resolver. On the path-register sweep the targeted sample went from 75 wrong accepts to 8 and the whole-grid sample from 14 to 0, none moving toward a wrong answer; 20 contrived `?//` binds under `try`/`?` that jq answers are now refused, in the safe direction. A plain-bind prefix, a bare-`.` `?//` chain and a `(.|.) as {..}` source are still accepted (#4187).
