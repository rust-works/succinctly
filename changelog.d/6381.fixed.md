- **yq: a block scalar's leading blank line that is wider than its first content line ends the scalar, as it does in yq** (#6381). go-yaml takes an auto-detected block scalar's indent from the widest of the leading blank lines and the first content line, so `a: |` over a line of four spaces and then `  x` has an indent of four: the scalar is empty and `  x` is a stray line, an error in a mapping or sequence. `succinctly yq` took the indent from `  x` alone and read `{"a":"  \nx\n"}`. A blank line no wider than the content (`a: |` over a line of two spaces and `  x`), a deeper content line, and an explicit indent indicator are read as before. Checked against yq v4.53.3 on a grid of 13,824 documents (six hosts, eight headers, blank-line widths 0-7, content indents 1-6), 7,384 of them refused by yq, with no difference in which are refused; before the change 2,520 of them were read here.
