- **yq: a zero-padded `08` is a float after a write, and `!!int 1_000` is a number** (#6938). `tag` and `type` over `p: 08` after a write (`.zz = 1 | .p | tag`) said `!!int`, where yq says `!!float`; an explicit `!!int 08` stays an integer. A tag-forced number now drops its underscores as yq does: `!!int 1_000` is `1000`, `!!int 0_8` is `8`, `!!float 1_0.5` is `10.5` (a `_` that is not between two digits is still refused for a float); they were the string. A plain `1_000` is still the string.
