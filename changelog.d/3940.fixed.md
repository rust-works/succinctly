- **jq: a `foreach` source that destructures the register after a leading no-op stage now raises as jq does in path position** (#3940, a #3853 residual). `path(foreach ((.|.) | foreach . as {a:$a} (0;.;.)) as $x (.;.;.))` answered `[]` where jq raises "Invalid path expression with result", and `(foreach ((.|.) | foreach . as {a:$a} (0;.;.)) as $x (.;.;.)) = 9` replaced the whole document with `9`. `foreach_source_destructures_register` read only a pipe's first stage, so any stage that merely hands the register on (`.`, `(.|.)`, `(., .)`) hid the destructure behind it from the resolver. It now skips those stages and judges the first one that does something, which also covers `(.|.) | . as {a:$a} | .` and `.|(. as {a:$a} | .)`. A bare loop variable, a nested `reduce` and a navigated leading stage are unchanged.
