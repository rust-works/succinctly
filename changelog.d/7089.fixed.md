- **yq: after a byte-order mark, the comment block under a first `---` that a blank line detaches from the body is the first node's foot** (#7089).
  `# c1` / `---` / `# c2` / blank / `a: 1` put both blocks in the head of `.a`'s key (`c1\nc2\n`) and printed them above it; yq reads `c1` as its head and `c2` as its foot, and prints `# c1` / `a: 1` / `# c2`.
  A blank line, `---`, `# c1`, a blank line and the body is the same: `c1` is the foot.
  A scalar document gets it as its foot, and before a flow collection it is dropped, as in yq.
