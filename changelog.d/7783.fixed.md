- **jq: a POSIX `TZ` takes any number of digits in its offsets and rule times, as macOS libc and glibc read it** (#7783). `TZ=XYZ5:30:5`, `XYZ1:5`, `XYZ005`, `XYZ+005:00`, `EST5EDT4:5,M3.2.0,M11.1.0` and `EST5EDT,M3.2.0/2:3,M11.1.0` were read as UTC by `localtime`, `strflocaltime` and `strptime`'s `%Z`, because the zone parser took exactly two digits for minutes and seconds and at most two for an offset's hours; jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 apply them on both platforms. The forms the two C libraries read differently (a name shorter than three characters, a minute of 60, a trailing `:`) keep their reading, recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
