- **yq: a mapping key that is a collection is written as the key node** (#6496). `printf -- '? - p\n: v\n' | succinctly yq .` printed `"": v` and yq v4.53.3 prints `? - p` / `: v`: the writer wrote the text of the key (empty) though the index held the node. A sequence, a block mapping and a flow collection key are written after `? ` with their lines one indent step in (`-I=4` too), the value after it on the `:` line, and an empty `[]` or `{}` key is the simple key `[]: v`. A compact collection inside such a key lost every item after the first of the one it opened (`? - - p` / `    - q` printed `? - - p`): the parser closed the key's first item behind the container its value had opened, which is now a frame of its own as an ordinary sequence item is. Over 24 documents (the keys above, nested, among other entries, in a sequence, with values of every kind) at `-I=2` and `-I=4`, 48 outputs, every one is yq's. `-P`, `keys`, `to_entries`, `sort_keys`, `$x` and `[.]` still write `""` (#6547).
