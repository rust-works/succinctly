- **jq: the values after an invalid byte outside a string are read** (#5994). A lead byte outside any string took the white space after it into its U+FFFD (the short-tail rule jq applies inside a string), so in `\xe1\n"a" 1` the newline was lost and `[limit(4; repeat(try input catch .))]` read the fault and then nothing. jq substitutes nothing outside a string; the bytes there are now substituted one at a time, and the read goes on to `"a"` and `1`, as in jq.
