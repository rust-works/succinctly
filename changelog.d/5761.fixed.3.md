- **jq: the right-hand side of `op=` and `//=` keeps the operand's storage** (#5761).
  `[nan] as $a | null | . += $a | . == $a`, `{x:null} | .x += $a | .x == $a`, `(.x, .y) += $v` and `(.x += $v)?` over a fold, `. += $v | .` and `. //= $v | .` are `true` in jq, where the operand is stored as itself, and were `false` here: the per-path filter rebuilt a container operand from literals.
  A container operand now rides a by-value marker, as a fold's loop variable already does; a scalar stays a literal and `succinctly yq` is unchanged.
  The marker is by-value on purpose: a plain untracked one took `. + <operand>` off the owned fast path (`.[] += [0]` measured +64% in a prototype).
  Measured interleaved A/B on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X, min of 9, output identical: `.[] += [0]` over 150,000 pairs -1.0%, `map(. += [0])` -6.0%, `[.[] | .meta += {c:1}]` -4.3%, `.tags += [1]` over 60,000 records -0.3%, `//=` -2.4%.
  Not measured on ARM.
