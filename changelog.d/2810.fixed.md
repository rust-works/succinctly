- **yq: the first error a program raises ends the run** (#2810). `printf 'a: x\n---\na: 1\n' | yq '.a | tonumber'` prints one error and nothing else in yq, because the documents after the failing one are never evaluated; succinctly kept evaluating them and printed their results, with one diagnostic per failing document. It now stops at the first error across documents and files on every stdout route and keeps what the earlier documents printed, as yq does. A failure to decode a value and an evaluator resource cap still let the run reach the next document. The issue's premise, that yq also erases the earlier documents' output, was not true: its example fails in document 1 too, because yq's `error` fires when `select` yields nothing.
