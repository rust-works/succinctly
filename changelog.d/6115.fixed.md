- **jq: `--seq` with `--stream` or `--stream-errors` feeds the filter the events of each record** (#6115). The flags were ignored under `--seq`, so `printf '\x1e[1]\n\x1e{"a":2}\n' | succinctly jq -c --seq --stream .` printed the two records where jq prints `[[0],1]`, `[[0]]`, `[["a"],2]` and `[["a"]]`, and `--seq --stream-errors` never turned a fault into the `[message, path]` event jq prints.
  Both now read with the port of jq's streaming parser (`jq_stream_errors.rs`) in its record-sequence mode, so a record cut short still yields the events before the cut, and the fault is jq's own warning (`jq: ignoring parse error: Truncated value at line 2, column 1`), or under `--stream-errors` an event.
  `input`/`inputs` raise a fault as an error, `-s` closes its array where jq does, and `input_line_number`/`input_filename` follow jq's reader; 15,500 random record sequences over stdin and up to three files, with long lines across jq's 4,095-byte read, matched `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 on stdout, status and the set of stderr lines.
