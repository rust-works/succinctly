- **jq: `walk(f)` on an object reads one output of `f` per member, as in jq** (#5794). `{"a":1} | walk((., error("x")))` printed nothing; jq prints `{"a":1}` before raising, because `walk` rewrites an object's members with `map_values` and never runs the rest of `f` on a member. A control that `f` raises on a member after its first output (an error, a `break`) no longer replaces the parent's output; arrays, which collect every output, are unchanged. A side effect of a later output (`halt_error`'s stderr text) still fires; that residual is recorded in the limitations page. `succinctly yq --jq-extensions` follows jq, as real yq has no `walk`.
