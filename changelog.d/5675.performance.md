- **jq: `-R 'split("lit") | length'` counts a line's pieces from the line** (#5675). A `-R` line is already the
  string the filter reads, but `split(sep) | length` still wrote it out as JSON, indexed it and split the decoded
  copy, then counted the array. Over 10 MB of NDJSON on an Apple M-series (interleaved, min of 11, output
  identical) `split(",")|length` takes 29.9 ms instead of 108.7 ms (jq 1.7.1: 89.8 ms) and `split("")|length`
  23.8 ms instead of 229.8 ms; x86_64 not measured. The pieces are counted without building them, by the same
  rules as `split` (an empty line has none, an empty separator counts characters).
