- **jq: `--slurp --stream` keeps the last closing event in the array** (#7523). `printf '[1][2]' | jq -c -s --stream .` printed `[[[0],1],[[0]],[[0],2]]` and then `[[0]]` (the same for `[1,2]` and `{"a":1}` with no newline after them), as jq 1.7.1 does; now it prints one array of every event, `[[[0],1],[[0]],[[0],2],[[0]]]`, as jq 1.8.2 does (jqlang/jq#3279), which fixed this. `-n`, `input`/`inputs` and `--stream-errors -s` follow. This replaces the held-back event of #5548. The difference from the pinned 1.7.1 is recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
