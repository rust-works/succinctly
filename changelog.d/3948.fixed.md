- **jq: a nested `foreach` over a `null` document with a `?//` pattern chain now answers the member the matched alternative moved to** (#3948, a #3939 residual). `path(foreach (foreach . as [$a] ?// {a:$a} (0; .; .)) as $x (.; .; .))` on `null` answered `[]` where jq answers `[0]`, and `(foreach (foreach . as {a:$a} ?// [$a] (0; .; .)) as $x (.; .; .)) = 9` replaced the document with `9` where jq gives `{"a":9}`. A chain with at least one array or object alternative over a bare `.` source is now routed through the resolver like the single-pattern case.
