- **yq: a write made inside an update body reaches a merged member's source** (#6827). `.list[] |= (.x = 7)`, `with(.item; .x = 4)` and `.item |= del(.x)` over mappings that merge `<<: *base` now change `base` and every mapping that merges it, as yq does; they changed only the mapping's own copy before. A body that reads the member it writes (`.x += 1`), one that deletes a key and then writes more, and the shapes of #6785 that stay (a source replaced with other keys, a chained merge) still differ and are recorded in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
