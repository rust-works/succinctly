- **jq: `capture`, `scan` and `sub`'s `.` report a group of an empty match as `""`, not `null`** (#5502). jq builds them from `match`'s captures, where every group of a zero-length match is an empty match at its position, so `"b" | capture("(?<x>a)?")` is `{"x":""}` and `"b" | [scan("(a)?")]` is `[[""],[""]]`; here they were `{"x":null}` and `[[null],[null]]`. A group that took no part in a non-empty match is still `null` (#5363), and yq mode keeps Go's `null`.
