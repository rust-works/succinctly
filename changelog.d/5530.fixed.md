- **jq: the regex flags `l` and `n` now do what Oniguruma's do** (#5530, #920, #922). `l` (`ONIG_OPTION_FIND_LONGEST`) was accepted and ignored, so `"abaa" | match("a|aa|aaa"; "l")` answered `"a"` at offset 0 where jq answers `"aa"` at offset 2, and `sub("a|aa|aaa"; "X"; "l")` on `"aaa"` was `"Xaa"` against `"X"`. `n` (`ONIG_OPTION_FIND_NOT_EMPTY`) only skipped forward, so a lazy quantifier or an empty-first alternation found no match: `"xaab" | gsub("a*?"; "X"; "gn")` was a no-op and is now `"xXXb"`. A pattern with `l`, or with `n` and a way to match empty, is compiled by `fancy-regex`, whose longest-match and not-empty modes `l` and `n` use; every other pattern keeps the `regex` crate. `l` picks the longest match anywhere in the text, not the leftmost one, and stays a no-op on a pattern with a lazy quantifier (`fancy-regex` returns the lazy match there). yq mode is unchanged.
