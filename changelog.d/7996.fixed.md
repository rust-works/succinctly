- **yq: `env(NAME)` and `strenv(NAME)` read the variable once for their whole context, with no document, as yq does** (#7996). yq's `envOperator` returns one new node for the nodes it is given, however many there are and even when there are none, and that node has no document index. Over `a: 1` / `---` / `b: 2`, `env(X)` and `strenv(X)` printed `hi`, `---`, `hi` where yq v4.53.3 prints `hi` twice, and under `--eval-all` they printed it once per document where yq prints it once. `.[] | env(X)` over a three-member sequence printed `hi` three times and `select(false) | env(X)` printed nothing, where yq prints one `hi` for each; `map(env(X))` is `[hi]`, a write's right side reads it once too (`.x = [.x[] | env(X)]` writes `[hi]`), and an unset variable in `env()` or `--security-disable-env-ops` is now an error after a stage that matched nothing, as in yq. A write still runs per document. `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
