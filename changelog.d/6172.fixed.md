- **jq: a possessive quantifier that something follows is answered as jq answers it, not refused** (#6172). `"aaa" | test("a*+a")` is `false` in jq, and #5524 refused it because the `regex` crate has no atomic group. The quantifier is now written as the atomic group of its atom and quantifier (`(?>a*)a`), which the second engine reads, so `a*+a`, `(ab)*+ab`, `[a]{1,2}+b`, `a?+*`, `a*+*` and `a?+{2}` (which `fancy-regex` cannot read written out) give jq's answer. An atom the scan cannot place (a quoted run, an unsized escape, a group of flags), an inline `(?x)`, and a pattern with a lazy quantifier followed by `+` keep the refusal. 30,000 generated patterns against jq 1.7.1 (three flag sets) went from about 2,900 differences per 8,000 patterns, nearly all refusals, to 640, none new.
