- **compare(jq): `--build-time` times cold builds from source** (#5552).
  `succinctly dev compare jq --build-time` adds a table to the operational section: a cold build of jq 1.7.1 and
  jq 1.8.1 (`configure` + `make` + `make install`, the download not on the clock), a cold
  `cargo build --release --locked` of succinctly after an untimed `cargo fetch`, and succinctly's rebuild after
  touching `src/lib.rs`. One run each, in a scratch directory that is removed afterwards. `scripts/compare-jq-build.sh`
  takes `COMPARE_JQ_DEST` and `COMPARE_JQ_TIMING_FILE` for it; without them it behaves as before.
  CI's `compare-jq` workflow passes the flag on both Linux runners.
