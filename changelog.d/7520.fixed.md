- **jq: an optional capture group that takes no part in an empty match is unmatched** (#7520). `"a" | match("(b)?") | .captures` reported `{"offset":0,"string":"","length":0}` for the group, and `capture("(?<x>b)?")`, `scan("(b)?")` and `sub("(?<x>b)?"; "[\(.x)]")` showed `""` for it, as jq 1.7.1 does; now the group is `{"offset":-1,"string":null,"length":0}` and the others show `null`, as in jq 1.8.2 (jqlang/jq#3238), which fixed this. A group that took part in the empty match is unchanged. The difference from the pinned 1.7.1 is recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
