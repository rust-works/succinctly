- **`succinctly jq` reads a field access written with an interpolated string** (#6053). `{"a1":5} | ."a\(1)"` is a compile error here and `5` in jq, where `.` followed by a string indexes by that string and the string may hold `\(...)`; it is `.["a\(1)"]`. The step works after another field (`.a."b\(0)"`), takes `?`, and works under `=`, `|=`, `del` and `path`. `succinctly yq` is unchanged. Found by a syntax-edge sweep against jq 1.7.1.
