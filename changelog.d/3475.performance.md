- **yq: an array of the document's own nodes streams instead of copying the document first** (#3475).
  `[.users[] | select(.age > 30)]`, `[.[]]`, `[.users]` and `.users | [.[]]` print from the nodes' cursors, as
  `map(select(...))` already did, where the DOM route copied the whole sequence into an owned value and a comment
  tree before printing a byte. Over comment-free records on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (interleaved A/B, same output):
  at 100 MB, `[.users[] | select(.age > 30)]` peaks at 262 MB instead of 2,350 MB and takes 2.7 s instead of
  7.2 s (-62%), and `[.users]` peaks at 261 MB instead of 4,813 MB and takes 2.3 s instead of 12.6 s (-81%);
  at 1 MB and 10 MB they take 56% and 61% (`select`) and 79% and 82% (`[.users]`) less, and `map(select(...))`
  is unchanged. With `-o json` the same shapes peak at 262 MB instead of 1,055 MB and 1,182 MB and take 19-33%
  less time. On an Apple M4 Pro the same two shapes take 48% and 68% less at 100 MB (46-47% and 66-68% at 1 and
  10 MB) and peak at 442 MB instead of 2,347 MB and 274 MB instead of 4,857 MB. Only a body that is navigation to one node, or navigation that fans out with `.[]` and goes on
  through `.`, `.[]` and `select(...)`, streams; a stage that computes, a `.a` after a fan-out, a slice or a `,`
  keeps the old route. A YAML document with an alias keeps it, because the streamers would write an `*alias` whose anchor the array does not
  hold; a comment does not (#6736, and the document's own foot comment for `[.]`): over a 10 MB file with a comment
  before every tenth record, `[.users[] | select(.age > 30)]` takes 331 ms instead of 818 ms (-60%) and `[.users]`
  285 ms instead of 1,545 ms (-82%) on the 7950X, with the comments kept. An element that fails to decode, or a complex key that collides with another, is
  still found before the first byte is written.
