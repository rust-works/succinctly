- **yq: an array built from the document's own nodes streams instead of copying the document first** (#3475).
  `[.users[] | select(.age > 30)]`, `[.[]]`, `.users | [.[]]` and `[.users]` print from the nodes' cursors, as
  `map(select(...))` already did, where the DOM route copied the whole sequence into an owned value and a comment
  tree before printing a byte. Over comment-free records on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (interleaved A/B, same output):
  at 100 MB, `[.users[] | select(.age > 30)]` peaks at 262 MB instead of 2,350 MB and takes 2.7 s instead of
  7.2 s (-62%), and `[.users]` peaks at 261 MB instead of 4,812 MB and takes 2.4 s instead of 12.6 s (-81%);
  the same 62% and 81% hold at 1 MB and 10 MB, and `map(select(...))` is unchanged. With `-o json` the same
  shapes peak at 262 MB instead of 1,054 MB and 1,182 MB and take 19-33% less time. Only a body that is
  navigation or `select` streams, never a stage that computes, a slice or a `,`. A YAML document with a comment,
  or a stream of several documents, keeps the DOM route: the streamers write a sequence without the comments
  between its items. An element that fails to decode, or a complex key that collides with another, is still
  found before the first byte is written.
