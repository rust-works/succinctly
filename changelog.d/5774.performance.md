- **jq: a `reduce`/`foreach` body that reads through a container named argument no longer rebuilds the whole value on every step** (#5774). `-n --slurpfile d users.json 'reduce range(50) as $i (0; . + $d[0].users[$i].age)'` is 97% faster on an Apple M5 Max at 1 MB (372 ms to 11 ms; jq 1.7.1 is 9 ms), and 98% faster at 10 MB (3.63 s to 72 ms) and 100 MB (37.3 s to 656 ms), so the cost no longer grows with steps times the argument's size; `[foreach range(200) as $i (0; . + $d[0].users[$i].score; .)]` goes from 1.63 s to 13 ms. A `$d` that is read through (`$d[0]`, `$d.k`, `$d[$i]`, `$d[1:]`, `$d[]`, `$d | length`, `keys`, `has(k)`) is one shared value in a fold; a bare `$d` (an operand of `+`, an element of `[...]`, a fold's initial value) and any fold with an assignment, path expression or definition keep the literal, as before (within noise). `closed_expr_to_owned` now answers a computed index `$r.items[$k]` and a container's `length` from the owned value, for every fold, not only a named argument.
