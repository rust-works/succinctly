- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that is a bare register-neutral builtin, or a nested comma, pipe, `//` or `?` of them, no longer skips the write silently** (#4028, a #3960 residual). `del(foreach .a as $v (0; now; try ($v | .b?)))` on `{"a":{"b":1,"c":[1,2]},"z":0}` is `{"a":{"c":[1,2]},"z":0}` in jq and was the unchanged document (exit 0) here: `now`, `floor` and the other builtins #3960 lists navigate nothing, so jq's register is still on `.a` when EXTRACT runs, but only a flat comma sibling was read as leaving it, and the `try` swallowed the refusal into a skipped write. UPDATE is now read through the shapes that only fork or sequence such builtins (`now`, `((now,now),(now,now))`, `(now, now|tostring)`, `(now, first(floor))`, `(now, floor // 1)`, `(now, floor?)`, `(now)?`, `(($v|.b?), (now|floor))`), and `=`, `|=`, `del` and `path` follow jq. An 80,000-row seeded grid of UPDATE shapes against jq 1.7.1 fixes 1,783 rows with no regression and no new accepted-where-jq-refuses row. A pipe stage after a comma with a navigating sibling (`(($v|.b?), now) | floor`) and `abs`/`min`-class builtins still skip the write silently, as before.
