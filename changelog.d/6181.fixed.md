- **jq: a repeat operator after a bare option group is refused, as jq refuses it** (#6181). `"caab" | test("(?i)*a")`, `test("a(?i)+")` and `test("(?i){1}a")` printed `true`; jq 1.7.1 has nothing to repeat there and answers `Regex failure: target of repeat operator is not specified`, at the start of a pattern, after an item and past comments alike. `(?i:a)*`, `((?i))*` and `(?i){,2}a` (a literal run) still answer. Over a 1,350-pattern sweep of option groups, operators and positions, 1,048 refusals now match jq; the rest are patterns with an inline `(?x)` and possessive operators, which keep their earlier answers.
