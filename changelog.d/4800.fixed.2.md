- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` destructure `foreach` in a `foreach` UPDATE over a moving source answers on the step where `$x` is the register** (#4800, #4797).
  With `P = foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)`, `path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; P; .))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]` in jq and raised `Invalid path expression with result null` here (likewise with `try (P)` as the UPDATE, and `path(. as $x | null | foreach ($x as {a:$a} | .) as $y (.; .; .))`): the empty first step leaves `null`, the second step's element is the root `$x` is bound to, so its pattern step passes and moves the register onto a `null` the state equals by kind. Only where the pattern lands on a `null`/boolean of the state's kind; a pattern landing on a non-null node and the same shape behind a literal stage, a fallback or a gate are unchanged.
