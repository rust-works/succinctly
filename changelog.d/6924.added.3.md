- **yq: `-o toml`** (#6924, slice 2 of #6850).
  The results are written as TOML by a port of yq's TOML encoder: attributes first, then `[a.b]` tables and `[[a]]` arrays of tables, inline tables inside arrays, bare or Go-quoted keys and `%q` strings (the set of characters Go escapes is generated from the pinned yq, `scripts/gen_go_print.py`).
  Checked against yq v4.53.3 over 3,800 random JSON documents (no difference); comments and a value's source spelling (`True`, `yes`, `1_000`) are not read yet and are listed in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
