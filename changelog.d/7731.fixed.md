- **`succinctly jq` steps an all-integer `range` past `2^53` in doubles, as jq does** (#7731). It walked exact `i64` integers at every magnitude, so `[range(-9007199254740994; -9007199254740990)]` had four values where jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 have three (`-9007199254740994 + 1` is the double `-9007199254740992`), `[range(9223372036854775806; 9223372036854775807)]` was `[9223372036854775806]` where jq's bounds are both the double 2^63 and the range is empty, and `[limit(3; range(-9223372036854775808; 0))]` counted up where jq repeats `-9223372036854776000`. Within ±2^53, where the two walks agree value for value, the `i64` walk is unchanged; past it the first value is still `$from` itself, `range/3` between two integer literals compares them as decimals first as jq's `<` does, and a walk whose sum rounds back to itself (jq loops forever) raises `range: maximum iterations exceeded` at the existing cap. `succinctly yq --jq-extensions` keeps yq's exact `int64` walk. The divergence `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md` recorded for #2131 and #2219 is closed.
