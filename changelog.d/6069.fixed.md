- **jq: a group under a repeat whose body can match empty reports jq's last, empty iteration** (#6069). `"ab" | match("(a*)*b")` had the group at offset 0 holding `"a"`, the last non-empty iteration; jq's Oniguruma ends the loop with an empty iteration and reports `""` at offset 1, and so does succinctly now. `capture`, `scan` and `sub` read the same groups (`"aab" | capture("(?<x>a*)*b")` is `{"x":""}`). A group nested in the body keeps what an earlier iteration captured, as in jq. Patterns that go to `fancy-regex` (lookaround, backreferences, the `l` flag) keep the old captures; yq mode is unchanged, as Go's `regexp` reports the last non-empty iteration too.
