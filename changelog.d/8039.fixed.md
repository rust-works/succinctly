- **yq: `key` and `parent` inside `with(PATH; . |= f)`, and inside any `. |= f` nested in an update, read the target's position** (#8039).
  `with(.b; . |= key)` is `.b |= (. |= key)`, and yq writes `"b"` there as it does for `.b |= key`, but the nested update answered no `key` at its own input, so `{"a":1,"b":5}` came back unchanged; `.b | . |= key` answered `5` for yq's `"b"`, and `with(.a.b; . |= (parent | keys))` left the node where yq writes the keys of `.a`. `key` is now the last component of the position the enclosing update installed, and `parent` climbs that update's input, so nested `with`, array members (`with(.a[]; . |= key)` is `[0,1,2]`), a missing target and `--eval-all`/`-i` agree. A node the body builds still has no position (`.a |= (.b |= (5 | . |= key))` leaves `5`, as in yq), and `with(.b; . |= (select(false) | key))` still leaves the input unchanged. `succinctly jq` is unchanged (`with` is not a jq builtin).
