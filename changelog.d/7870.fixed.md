- **`succinctly jq` reads a closure argument that grows a generator at each level of a recursion one output at a time** (#7870). `def f(g; $n): if $n <= 0 then g else f(g + (1, 3); $n - 1) end; first(f((1, 3); 500))` is `501` in jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2, at once; `succinctly jq` took time and memory that doubled with each level and aborted on a failed allocation at 500. An argument that is pure was read eagerly, every output at once, and a `,` over constants counted as pure: this one yields `2^n` outputs at level `n`, all of them built before `first` saw one. An argument is now read eagerly only when its output count cannot grow from one level to the next: it yields once, or its count (the product of every `,` in it and of every argument it reads) is at most 4, or is that of its own `,`s alone, or is no more than an argument it reads yields, so a chain that only carries a `,` along (`f(n - 1)` from `f(1, 2)`) keeps its eager read. Past that, a link of a chain (it reads one argument, once) is read one output at a time, as jq reads it, and any other pure argument (one that reads an argument twice, or calls a definition that calls itself) is read ahead by at most 32 outputs, so that a small one is still done before its consumer runs. The pipe spelling (`f(g | . + (1, 3); ...)`), a wider `,`, a helper `def` that adds the `,` and `limit`, `isempty` and `label` as the consumer were the same and are fixed with it. Under cachegrind on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (x86_64 Linux, release build with the `cli` feature) the program retired 5.7 million instructions at 8 levels, 89.6 million at 12 and 1.77 billion at 16; it now retires 2.0 million at 16, 21.5 million at 500 and 162 million at 4,000, about 40 thousand a level (jq 1.7.1: 13.2 million at 500, 52.4 million at 4,000), and a deeper recursion is refused at the native-stack floor like any other. The answers are unchanged wherever there was one. In `succinctly yq --jq-extensions` the same argument chain under `limit(1; ...)` is linear too, and there an argument past the bound that raises an error after its first outputs now raises it when the consumer reaches it, as `succinctly jq` does, where it was raised before any output.
