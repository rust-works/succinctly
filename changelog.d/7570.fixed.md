- **A `--stream` run that stops on a close at a chunk's last byte leaves a redirected stdin where jq does** (#7570). When the closer right after a scalar (`[2]`, `{"a":2}`, `[[[2]]]`) was the last byte of one of jq's 4095-byte reads, a run that stopped on the close (`--stream -n 'limit(k; inputs)'`, a main-loop `halt`) left standard input at the end of that read; jq hands that event out only after reading the next chunk, and the offset is now left at the end of that one, as jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 leave it.
