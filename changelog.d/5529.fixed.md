- **jq: `\<`, `\>` and `(?P<name>...)` no longer compile to a meaning jq does not give them** (#5529). `"<a>" | [match("\\<a\\>").string]` was `["a"]` (the `regex` crate reads `\<` and `\>` as word-boundary assertions) and is now `["<a>"]` as in jq, where Oniguruma's Perl syntax makes them the literal characters. A Python-style group (`"a" | test("(?P<n>a)")`) answered `true` where jq errors with `undefined group option`; it now errors (`invalid regex: undefined group option`, this crate's usual prefix). `succinctly yq` is unchanged.
