- **jq: `strptime` derives the month and day from `%j`** (#5383). `"2015 060" | strptime("%Y %j")` was `[2015,0,0,0,0,0,3,-1]` (the day of year was parsed and discarded); with a year read and nothing that sets the month or day it is now `[2015,2,1,0,0,0,0,59]`, as in jq 1.7.1 on macOS, a day past the end of the year (`"2015 366"`) rolls into the next year, and day 0 and 367 are refused as `does not match format`. `%j` next to a month or day, or with no year, is the libc's own and aborts jq on several rows; it is unchanged and recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
