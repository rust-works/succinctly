- **`succinctly yq` keeps each item `with_entries(select(...))` and `map(select(...))` let through as it was written** (#6674). These filters took the plain route, so every surviving item lost its spelling, quoting and comment: `printf 'a:\nb: 1\n' | succinctly yq 'with_entries(select(.key != "zz"))'` printed `a: null`, and now prints `a:` as yq does, likewise `~`, `True`, `'y'` and a trailing comment. A document's head comment now survives `with_entries` and `to_entries | from_entries` (yq keeps it on the first key, so the written-out `to_entries | map(select(...)) | from_entries` drops it with a filtered-out first entry, and so does this). Of 165 rows compared with yq v4.53.3, 45 that differed now agree and none regressed; a `with_entries` over a sequence and an anchor a `select` drops while an alias to it stays are recorded in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
