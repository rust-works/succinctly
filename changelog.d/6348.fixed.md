- **yq: a block scalar header with anything after its indicators is refused, as yq refuses it** (#6348). The header holds one chomping character, one digit 1-9, blanks, a comment and the line break. `a: |  x` / `  y`, `a: |x`, `a: |-x`, `a: |"q"` and `- |  x` read `{"a":"y\n"}` (the text dropped, the lines below taken as the content) and are now `did not find expected comment or line break`, as is a second chomping character or digit (`a: |--`, `a: |-+`, `a: |22`) and a `0` (`a: |0`). A header with a comment, blanks or a tab before its break reads as before. U+0085, U+2028 and U+2029 end the header in yq and still do not here.
