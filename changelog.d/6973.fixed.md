- **yq: an `--eval-all` arithmetic result keeps its left operand's `EvaluateTogether` flag** (#6973). yq's `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` and `%` return a copy of their left node, so when that node is one of the list's own (`.`) the result is flagged too: `yq ea '. * {"z":1} | [.]' md.yaml` over two documents is one array of the four results (it was four arrays of one), `. + 1 | [.]` over `5` and `6` is `[6,6,7,7]`, and a following binary stage crosses the whole list (`(. * {"z":1}) | . + {"w":2}` is 16 results, it was 4). A left operand that navigated or built its node still has no flag (`.x + 1 | [.]` is one array per result), and a comparison's boolean is a node of its own. 21 rows are pinned against yq v4.53.3 in `tests/yq_cli/i6973.rs`; `line` of a computed result, an `as` binding over the list and the `document_index` of a comparison stay open in #6973.
