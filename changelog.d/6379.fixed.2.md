- **yq: a property before a `?` and a tab after a `?` or an explicit `:` are refused, as in yq** (#6379). A `?` after a node property opens no key in go-yaml, so `&t ? x`, `- &t ? x`, `k:` / `  - &t ? x` and `? - &t ? x` are `mapping keys are not allowed in this context` (a property on a line of its own, and `&t ?x`, are not that); `succinctly yq` read `- &t ? x` as a key sequence and `? - &t ? x` as an empty key. go-yaml skips no tab right after those indicators, so `?⇥x`, `? ⇥x`, and `:⇥b` after an explicit key are `found character that cannot start any token`, which the top level read (the library loader keeps YAML 1.2's reading, a tab as separation); a tab whose blanks end at a `#` is its comment scanner's and is read, which #6340's nested check refused (`? ?⇥# c`). Over every stream of up to three lines from 26 explicit-key lines (18,278 streams) the baseline differs from yq on 4,441 and this build on 967, all of them streams both tools refuse (yq prints the part before its error, `succinctly yq` prints nothing); 3,474 are fixed and none changed the other way.
