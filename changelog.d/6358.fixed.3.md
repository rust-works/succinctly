- **jq: a lazy bounded repeat over a body that opens with a flag switch answers as Oniguruma's does** (#6358). `"bbc" | [match("((?i)|b*){2,5}?c";"g") | .captures | map(.string)]` printed `[["bb"]]` and jq 1.7.1 answers `[[""]]`: the switch hid the body from the rewrite, which is needed to make the `regex` crate leave the repeat at the empty iteration as Oniguruma does. Oniguruma never unrolls a lazy repeat with an upper bound, so the operation count that made a switch unknown does not apply to it. Over ten draws of 2,500 `--bounded` loops 7 to 16 patterns per draw that differed from jq agree and none changed the other way; the plain, `--nest`, `--ways` and `--assert` draws are unchanged. A lazy repeat with no upper bound, and a body with a way that is only an anchor, keep their text beside a switch.
