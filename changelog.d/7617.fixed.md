- **yq: an unparenthesised comma in `ireduce`'s initial value is the union yq refuses** (#7617). yq's `,` and `;` share one precedence and associate to the right, so `.[] as $x ireduce (0, 1; . + $x)` is `0, (1; . + $x)` there, and yq fails with `reduce must be given a block, got UNION instead` when the fold is evaluated, where `succinctly yq` folded two accumulators (`3` and `4`). It now raises yq's error, with no output and exit code 1, where yq's fold runs in a pipe: over an empty source or a `select(false)` context too, but not behind a short-circuited `false and`. Under `|=` and `with_entries`, a right side whose context empties before the fold does not run it, where yq still raises (#8002). A parenthesised initial value (`ireduce ((0, 1); . + $x)`) still folds two accumulators, as in yq, and jq's `reduce` keeps reading a comma there as two folds.
