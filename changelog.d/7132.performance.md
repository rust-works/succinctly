- **jq: `first`, `last`, `nth(n)`, `has(k)`, `keys` and a comparison or boolean over a count, as a stage over a bound array, are answered from the value** (#7132, part). In a loop over a bound array (`. as $a | [range(n) | $a | first]`) each of these wrote `$a` out as text and indexed it again, 6 to 25 ms per call over 100,000 elements where jq takes 0.08; `eval_owned_input`, the entry a stage after an owned value takes, lacked the single-value doors its lazy twin has, and `first`/`last`/`nth` had none anywhere. The stage is now answered right after the fast path, before the embed-table scans (the witness walk was O(input)): 0.07 to 0.10 ms per call, jq's own speed. `first`, `last` and `nth` are the `.[0]`, `.[-1]` and `.[n]` they are defined as (a negative or computed `nth`, an element an owned `Int` cannot spell as the bridge does, and a container while the embed table is live stay the bridge's), and a read that raises declines, so the error text is jq's. Output, errors and their text are jq 1.7.1's (328 programs pinned in `tests/jq_cli/i7132.rs`, captured from `/usr/bin/jq`). Stages that are a comma, a pipe of stages, an `as` bind or a construction over reads (`(length | tostring)`, `[.[0], .[1]]`, `.[0] + .[1]`, `if length > 5 ...`) still take the bridge.
