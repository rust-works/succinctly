- **yq: `now | format_datetime(..)` over several documents prints no `---` between its results, as yq does** (#8077). yq's datetime operators build a replacement of the timestamp they read (`format_datetime`, `tz`, `to_unix` and `from_unix`, `operator_datetime.go`), so a result built from `now`, a literal or a document's root belongs to no document, while one built from a child (`.t | format_datetime("2006")`) keeps the child's. `now | format_datetime("2006")` over two documents printed `2026`, `---`, `2026` where yq v4.53.3 prints `2026`, `2026`; `.t | tz("UTC")` still prints its separator.
