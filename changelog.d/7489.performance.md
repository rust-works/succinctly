- **jq: a path-mode run that cannot hand an object key out keeps the document's keys as read** (#7489). #7401 gave every object key its own identity in the path-mode interpreter (the route for path expressions that do more than navigate), one or two allocations per key, but a key's identity is only asked of a key handed out as a value, by `.[]` on an object, `..`, `keys`, `keys_unsorted` or a jq-defined builtin built on them (`to_entries`, `paths`, `map`, ...). The interpreter now decides once per run whether the program can reach one, and leaves the keys alone when it cannot; answers are unchanged. Peak RSS of `. as $d | path($d)` (interleaved with an A/A copy of the base): over the generator's `users` pattern -31 to -33% at 10-100 MB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (968 to about 649 MiB at 100 MB) and -28 to -30% on an Apple M4 Pro (858 to about 601 MiB), and -7% over `comprehensive`, about the 8% #7401 added. Programs that can hand a key out keep the identities and are unchanged.
