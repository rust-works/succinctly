- **yq: `-o props|shell|csv|tsv|base64|uri`** (#6850, slice 1).
  `succinctly yq` took `yaml`, `json` and `auto` at `-o` and refused every other format yq lists.
  The results of the filter are now written through yq's properties, shell-variable, CSV, TSV, base64 and URI encoders (short names `p`, `s`, `c`, `t`; `--shell-key-separator` is read), with no `---` between documents and the line ends yq writes.
  Checked against yq v4.53.3 over 2,400 random documents (no difference in what is printed) and a hand-written matrix; the six rows that still differ are in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
  `kyaml`, `xml`, `toml`, `hcl`, `lua`, `ini` and the input formats are still refused.
