- **jq: junk after a container that is its parent's last child is refused, off the lazy routes** (#5654, #1676/#2243 residual). `[[1]@]`, `[1,[2]\f]`, `[{}@]`, `{"a":[1]@}` and `[[1]},2]` printed the document at exit 0, and so did a trailing comma after such a child (`[[1],]`, `{"a":1,"b":[1,2],}`, `length` over `{"a":[1,2],}`); jq 1.7.1 refuses each as a parse error. Only a scalar last child had the gap before the closing bracket checked, since a container's end could not be told from its start. The end now comes from the balanced parentheses, asked of the last child only. The lazy routes (`map(.)`, `first(.[])`) still skip the check. Interleaved A/B on an Apple M-series chip: `.` and `length` are within noise; `[.[]|.id]|length` over records whose last field is a container reads +7% to +13% (each lookup locates the end of that field).
