- **jq: a literal regex pattern, or an alternation of literals, is searched without Oniguruma** (#7965). A pattern with no metacharacter but `|`, no empty alternative and no flag but `g` is searched with `aho-corasick` (Teddy for an alternation), whose leftmost-first match is the one Oniguruma's backtracking finds; every answer is unchanged (the 459,678-row regex corpus, and a 40,000-row corpus of literal patterns identical to jq 1.7.1). Against the build before, interleaved over seeded layouts at 10 and 100 MB on a Ryzen 9 7950X and an Apple M4 Pro: a literal `test` is 3-5% faster, a literal `scan` or `gsub` 7-10%, `test("lorem|dolor|amet")` 7-9% and `scan` with it 27-30%, which more than recovers what the move to Oniguruma cost these shapes (ADR-0031 step 5).
