- **`succinctly jq` `strptime` reads the `0` and `+` flags and a width between the `%` and the specifier** (#5989). `"03" | strptime("%0d")`, `"-2015" | strptime("%+5Y")` and `"201599" | strptime("%6Y")` now match as in jq 1.7.1, where they used to fail with an unknown specifier. A width is the most digits a numeric field takes (`"012" | strptime("%3m")` is month 12, `"31" | strptime("%1d")` is no match), `%s`, `%w`, `%u`, the names and the composite formats ignore it, `%c %x %X %r` accept the prefix, and `%Z` with a width reads exactly that many capitals. A second `E`/`O`, a `+` after a digit and `_`/`-` flags stay no match, as in jq.
