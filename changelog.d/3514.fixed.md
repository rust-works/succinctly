- **jq: `limit`/`first` over a pipe stop the stages after the bound on the `key`/`path` walk** (#3514). `.a | limit(1; .[] | if ("A"|stderr) then . else . end) | key` on `{"a":[1,2]}` wrote `AA` where jq's `path(...) | last` writes `A`: the cursor route's walk stepped the whole pipe before truncating it, so every stage after `.[]` ran at the positions past the bound too, and a `?//` in it retried once more per dropped position (`AAAA` against jq's `AA`). The pipe is now streamed with a sink that stops at the bound, and each branch of a comma body gets only the budget the earlier branches left, so a pipe inside a comma branch stops as well. Output and exit status are unchanged on well-formed input; only the side effects (`stderr`, `debug`, `input`) and retries of positions jq never reaches go away.
