- **yq: an integer written in hex or octal against a float raises `strconv.ParseFloat`, as in yq** (#5651). `0x10 < 1.5` answered `false` and yq stops with `strconv.ParseFloat: parsing "0x10": invalid syntax`, because it compares an int with a float through `ParseFloat` of each node's text and Go's `ParseFloat` refuses a radix prefix (`0x`, `0o`). Two integers still compare as integers, a decimal spelling (`007`, `+5`, `1e3`) still parses, and `==` and `succinctly jq` are unchanged. The timestamp-string rows of the issue stay open.
