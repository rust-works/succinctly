- **jq: a lazy repeat that must run twice or more ends at its body's empty way, as Oniguruma's does** (#6128). `"bab" | [match("(?:(?:b|c)||[ab]){2,3}?"; "gn") | .string]` is `["b","ab"]` in jq and was `["b","a","b"]`. A differential of 2,435 generated lazy patterns over fifteen strings under `g` and `gn` differed on 386; it differs on 4 of 3,866 now, all unbounded `{n,}?` with a consuming way before the empty one (listed in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`).
