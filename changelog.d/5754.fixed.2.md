- **`strptime` reads `%U`, `%W` and `%V`** (#5754). With a year read, the week number places the date as jq's libc does: `%U` counts from the year's first Sunday, `%W` from its first Monday and `%V` from the Monday of ISO week 1, and a weekday read with `%w`, `%u` or `%a` picks the day within the week (`"2015 09" | strptime("%Y %U")` is `[2015,2,1,0,0,0,0,59]`). Week 0 before the year is no match and week 53 rolls into the next year. `%w` now refuses 7 and up and `%u` refuses 0 and 8 up, as jq does.
