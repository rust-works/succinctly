- **jq: a pipe's tail copies inherit the "no" answers the pipe already holds** (#5488, first step).
  A body rebuilt for each value (`. as $x | (. + $x | . + $x | ...)`) copies the rest of its pipe once per stage, and each copy started with empty memo cells, so it re-walked its `O(rest)` stages for `needs_path_context`, `effect_before_last` and the `holds_*` questions: about 55% of a 2-second sample at 80 stages, beside the clones themselves.
  `tail_copy` and a kept copy now take every `false` the pipe has settled; a `true` is not inherited, since the stage that holds it may not be among the copy's.
  On an Apple M5 Max (release, instructions retired, `[range(0;20000) | . as $x | (<n stages>)] | length`) at n = 10 / 20 / 40 / 80: every stage reading `$x` -13% / -20% / -25% / -29%, `[.][0]` stages with the read last -17% / -25% / -31% / -34%, and the read-first controls within +1.2% to -0.9%.
  The cost is still quadratic in the number of stages, because the copies are; removing them is the next step of #5488.
