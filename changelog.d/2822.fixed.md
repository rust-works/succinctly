- **`succinctly yq` reads a non-scalar mapping key's entry through `.[""]`** (#2822). Real yq addresses a mapping entry whose key is a sequence or mapping by the `""` it collapses that key to: `printf '? - a\n  - b\n: v\nb: 2\n' | yq '.[""]'` is `v`; it was `null`. A real `""` key and a second non-scalar key are matched the same way, the last one winning. `.[""] | key` (the key node) and the plain-YAML round-trip of such a mapping are still unfixed.
