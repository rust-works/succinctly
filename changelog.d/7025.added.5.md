- **yq: `--yaml-fix-merge-anchor-to-spec`** (#7025). `succinctly yq` rejected the flag. With it a `<<` is read as yq reads it under the flag: an own key beats a merged one wherever the `<<` is written (`.t.k`, `.t[]`, `[.t[] | key]`), of the sources of `<<: [*a, *b]` the earlier-listed wins, a source's own `<<` is expanded, and the JSON rendering and `explode(.)` list the own keys and then the members of each source that no own key or earlier source holds. Without the flag nothing changes. It takes its value after `=` only. Pinned against 300 rows from yq v4.53.3 in `tests/yq_cli/i7025_merge_anchor_to_spec.rs` and about 10,000 runs over random merge documents with no difference; a `<<` that names no mapping (yq fails on it under the flag), the write routes and the builtins that rebuild a mapping still differ (limitations.md).
