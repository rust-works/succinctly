- **yq: `-o xml`** (#6924, slice 2 of #6850).
  The results are written as XML by a port of yq's XML encoder: a key is an element, a sequence repeats it, `+@name` keys are attributes, `+content` is the text, `+p_target` a processing instruction and `+directive` a directive, with `-I` indent and the `--xml-attribute-prefix`, `--xml-content-name`, `--xml-proc-inst-prefix` and `--xml-directive-name` options.
  Checked against yq v4.53.3 over 5,000 random JSON documents (no difference); comments, source spellings (`~`, `True`) and an empty document are not read yet and are listed in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
