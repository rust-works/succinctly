- **yq: `<<` merge keys and complex keys print as yq prints them in an array of nodes** (#3475).
  `[.items[]]` over `- <<: *b` now prints `!!merge <<: *b` as yq does, where the owned route expanded the merge
  into the entries it names, and `[.]` over a mapping with a collection as its key prints `? [a, b]` / `: 1`
  where it printed `'': 1`. Both follow from the array streaming from the nodes instead of copying them
  (a document with a comment keeps the owned route, and so its old output).
