- **yq: a whitespace-only line in the head of a stream prints as a comment, and a head that ends mid-line gets its line break** (#6401). yq hands go-yaml a line of only blanks in the head of the first document, which reads it as a comment and writes `# ` plus the line (`⇥` is `# ⇥`, wherever it sits between comments, a `---` or a `%YAML` line), where `succinctly yq` printed nothing for it; a line of blanks after the first node is dropped by both. A head with no final line break (`%YAML 1.2`, `# c`) is written with one, and a `---` that ends it is followed by an empty line, kept under `-N` where the marker is not. Pinned against yq v4.53.3 in `tests/yq_cli/i6401.rs`; the `-P` route still does not reproduce the header.
