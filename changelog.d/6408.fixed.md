- **yq: an indented `%YAML` directive before any content is yq's header, not content** (#6408). yq's header slurp trims a line before it looks for `%YA`, so ` %YAML 1.2` (a space or tab before the `%`), in the head of a stream or after its leading `---`, is set aside as a column-0 one is: the stream is a null document, `  %YAML 1.2` / `a: 1` is `{"a":1}`, and the line prints back as a comment, `# ` and the line with its indent (`#  %YAML 1.2`). `succinctly yq` read it as the string `%YAML 1.2` (an error since #6402). Every stream of a 40-row matrix, as JSON and as YAML, now prints what yq prints; any other indented directive (`%TAG`, a misspelt `%YAML`) and a `...` after the head are still refused, as in yq.
