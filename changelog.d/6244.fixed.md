- **jq: a dash after a range that ends in `\x42` or `\102` is a member of the class, as in Oniguruma** (#6244). `"aA1-" | match("[\\x41-\\x42-\\d]"; "g")` was refused as `char-class value at end of range`; jq 1.7.1 answers `"A"`, `"1"`, `"-"`. The scan of a class's dashes took the digits of an unbraced `\x` or octal escape for items of their own, so a range just ended could start another. Over 2,160 generated classes (a range ending in a letter, a hex, octal or braced escape, then a dash and a class escape, a letter or an escape), 268 differed from jq before and none now.
