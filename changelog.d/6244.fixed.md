- **jq: a dash after a range that ends in `\x42`, `\102` or `\cB` is a member of the class, as in Oniguruma** (#6244). `"aA1-" | match("[\\x41-\\x42-\\d]"; "g")` was refused as `char-class value at end of range`; jq 1.7.1 answers `"A"`, `"1"`, `"-"`. The scan of a class's dashes took the digits of an unbraced `\x` or octal escape, and the letter of `\c`, for items of their own, so a range just ended could start another. Over 2,835 generated classes (a range ending in a letter, a hex, octal, braced or control escape, then a dash and a class escape, a letter or an escape), 328 differed from jq before and none now.
