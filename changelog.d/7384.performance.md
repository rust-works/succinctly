- **The release binary is 22% smaller: the CLI is compiled as part of the library, not as its own crate** (#7384).
  As a separate crate the CLI instantiated every generic evaluator function it reached a second time, and fat LTO
  does not merge identical functions, so the binary carried two copies of most of the evaluator. Stripped, as the
  release archive ships it, `succinctly` goes from 19.92 MB to 15.61 MB on an Apple M4 Pro and from 24.77 MB to
  19.34 MB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X. Over the 225 rows of `succinctly dev compare jq --full`, against jq 1.7.1 timed
  in the same runs, wall-clock is -1.5% on the 7950X (-2.6% at 1 MB, -9% at startup) and -0.3% on the M4 Pro,
  and peak RSS is -10.5% and -6.7%. `.numbers|add` on 100 MB is -18.7% (559.2 ms -> 454.7 ms) and -11.0%
  (577.7 ms -> 514.3 ms), because a document float is now added to a float sum in place. Three object-building
  rows regress on the M4 Pro only, `wide_map_values` by up to 8.5% (#7392), and `wide_keys` by up to 6.8%
  (507.6 ms -> 542.5 ms at 100 MB) on the 7950X only. yq is 0.5% to 0.7% faster on both chips (median of 45
  rows, JSON and YAML output; an A/A control of identical binaries reads within 0.11% of zero), with no row
  slower by more than 2.1%.
