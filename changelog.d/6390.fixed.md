- **yq: a block scalar header at the indent of an empty `:`, `-` or property-carrying key is that node's value** (#6390, #6386 follow-up). `? a` / `:` / `|` / `  t` read `{"a": null}` and yq reads `{"a": "t\n"}`; `a:` / `  -` / `  |` / `    t` read `{"a": [null, "t\n"]}` (a second, extra item) where yq reads `{"a": ["t\n"]}`; `a:` / `  b: &y` / `  |` / `    t` read `{"a": {"b": null}}`. A `|` or `>` line cannot be a key or an item, so at the indent of the empty node above it is that node's value, as a block sequence is for a key. Four places that tell a null value from one on the next line each had their own test (the explicit value after `:`, a bare dash and a dash with properties, and a key with an anchor or tag); they now share one helper, `Parser::header_at_indent`, with a test that the lookaheads agree. The `-` and `:` owners, with no property, `&y`, `!!str` or both, and `|`, `>`, `|-` or `>+`, 128 documents across 8 owners, agree with yq v4.53.3 on value and verdict, and a sweep of 2,640 documents around them (header indent below, at and above the owner's; with and without a following key or item) fixed 470 and changed none the other way.
