- **jq: a look-behind jq cannot size is refused under the `x` flag or an inline `(?x)` too** (#7865). Oniguruma sizes a look-behind's body after it has parsed the pattern, so the blanks and `#` comments the extended mode ignores are no part of it: `test("(?<=a|bc)"; "x")`, `test("(?x)(?<=a|bc)")`, `test("(?-x)(?<=a|bc)")` and `test("(?<= a | bc )"; "x")` are `Regex failure: invalid pattern in look-behind` in jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2, where succinctly answered `false`: its look-behind checks stood down for any pattern under `x` or with an inline flag group naming `x`. They now read the pattern without what the mode ignores, following `(?x)`, `(?x:...)` and `(?-x)` group by group; a brace that opens no interval for the blank it holds counts as the literal characters it is (`a{ 0}` is `a{0}`), and an item repeated no times counts for nothing, with or without `x` (`(?<=(?i:a)b|c{0})` and `(?<=(?:a|bc){0})` under `i` are accepted as in jq, where succinctly refused them). The check runs when a pattern is compiled, never per input. On 22,000 generated rows (2,200 look-behinds, each written ten ways) against both jq versions, 2,827 rows now match that did not, and none that matched differs.
