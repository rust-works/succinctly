- **jq: `[..|f]` runs each node through `f` as the walk reaches it, and `sort` over document numbers sorts their parsed values** (#7260).
  A pipe that starts with `..` (or `recurse`) collected every node, then every intermediate result, before
  the rest of the pipe ran: two vectors the size of the document. `[..|numbers]|length` on 10 MB of the
  `comprehensive` input peaks at 61 MB instead of 212 MB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (jq 1.7.1: 149 MB) and runs
  in 185 ms instead of 277 ms (jq: 598 ms); on an Apple M4 Pro 62 MB instead of 251 MB (jq: 163 MB), 10% faster.
  `sort`, `sort_by(.)`, `unique`, `unique_by(.)` and `group_by(.)` over an array of document numbers now sort a
  24-byte (value, index) pair instead of a decoded copy of every number with its cursor plus a merge buffer;
  every number keeps its spelling. `.numbers|sort|.[0]` on 10 MB peaks at 59 MB instead of 108 MB on the 7950X
  (jq: 97 MB) and is 46% faster; 57 MB instead of 95 MB on the M4 Pro (jq: 104 MB), 34% faster. `group_by(.)`
  is 2.2-4.5x faster.
