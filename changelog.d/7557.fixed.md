- **jq: a postfix after `break $label` parses** (#7557). `label $f | break $f.a`, `break $f[0]`, `break $f.[]`, `break $f[1:2]` and the rest of the postfix forms were a compile error (`unexpected character '.'`, exit 3); `break $name` is a term in jq's grammar, so they parse as in jq and the postfix never runs (`[label $f | 1, break $f.a, 2]` is `[1]`).
