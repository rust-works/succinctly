- **`succinctly jq` reads the faults of its lazy routes through the model of jq 1.7.1's reader, and comes back to lazy reading after each** (#7987). A program that reads `input`, `inputs` or `input_line_number` over a lone source longer than its first 256 KiB, several regular files of 1 MiB or more between them, or input read whole and scanned a chunk at a time takes its documents from a lazy producer. A fault that producer met used to hand the rest of the stream to the eager reader, which read it whole (a file to its end, and every later file) and placed every later fault by the line breaks in the text. Now the model (ADR-0033, step 7b) is entered at the last sync point before the fault, reads one read at a time over the bytes as read to the first sync point after it, and hands back to the lazy scan there, carrying the reader's state to the next fault, in the same file or the next. So the reads a fault drops, and the line breaks in them that jq's parser never counts, are jq's: with `}` on line 12,001 and `6 } 7` on line 12,012, the second fault is now `at line 12011`, as jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2 say it, where it was `at line 12012`. A source with invalid UTF-8 is now substituted a window at a time on these routes, as a stream already was, and holds a window too. Peak RSS under a program that counts every input past its faults (Apple silicon): a fault at 1 MB of a 100 MB file, 1.41 GB to 13.4 MB (the same on a pipe); a fault in the second of three 35 MB files, 678 MB to 13.6 MB; one invalid byte at 1 MB of a 100 MB file, 440 MB to 15.5 MB (475 MB to 13.8 MB on a pipe); the clean file and list are unchanged at 13.4 and 14.0 MB. A file that is not UTF-8 in its first 256 KiB is still read whole, as before. Over six differentials of 1,200 generated cases of 300 KiB to 2.5 MiB (lone files and pipes past their front, lists of 1 MiB or more, edges past the first chunk, at window boundaries and file seams; two dense with substituted strings beside faults, two with documents over several lines) and 12 programs, the 560 answers that changed were all of programs that read past a fault, and moved to what both releases print (370), to jq 1.7.1 where the releases differ (122), or differ from both only at a NUL or after it on the same parser line (66, ADR-0018's E2) or only in a later fault's message after a NUL in a string (2, E1); none moved away from both, and `-n '[inputs]|length'`, `input_line_number`, `.`, `--slurp`, `--seq`, `--stream` and `-R` changed on none. `SUCCINCTLY_READER_MODEL=0` selects the old reader.
