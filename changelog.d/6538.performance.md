- **jq: a `reduce`/`foreach` step that rebuilds its state with a constructor extending one of its lists is linear** (#6538).
  A step such as `{a: (.a + [$i]), n: (.n + 1)}`, `[.[0] + [$i], .[1] + 1]` or `{a: .b, b: (.a + [$i])}`
  now moves each field or index it reads out of the old state and extends it in place, as `.a += [$i]`
  already did. Before, it re-indexed the whole state every step, so the fold was quadratic: on an Apple M5,
  `reduce range(8000) as $i ({a: [], n: 0}; {a: (.a + [$i]), n: (.n + 1)})` takes 9 ms instead of 888 ms
  (jq 1.7.1: 52 ms), and 32,000 steps take 25 ms (jq: 734 ms). Strings and objects grow the same way.
