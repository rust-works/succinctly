- **`succinctly yq` refuses a fractional or float-spelled slice bound** (#3415). Real yq hands every slice bound's text to `strconv.ParseInt`, so `.[0:1.5]`, `.[(1.5):]`, `.[1.0:]` and `.[1e0:]` are errors on every target (and under `?`); `succinctly yq` floored/ceiled them like jq. A float that is integral by computation (`.[(1.0+1):]`) is still accepted, as in yq. `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
