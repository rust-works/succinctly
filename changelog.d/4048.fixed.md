- **jq: a `reduce` with a destructuring loop pattern leaves jq's path register where it entered** (#4048, a #3999 residual). `path(. as $x | {a:{b:1}} | ((reduce . as {a:$a} ?// $a (0; .)) or true) | $x)` on `{"a":[true]}` is `[]` in jq and was exit 5 here (so were `reduce .[] as [$a] (0; .)` before a frozen `$x`, the same fold under unary minus, and the `del`/`=` rows built on them): the pattern's steps run inside the loop jq backtracks, but the fold handed back no register, so the next stage could not tell `$x` was still the register. A destructuring `reduce` now states the register unmoved, from the stage's frame on an untracked entry. Over the 20 `reduce`/`foreach ... ?//` operands of `scripts/jq-path-register-sweep.py` (6,027 sampled rows) 375 refusals became 155, no row worse and no new wrong answer. `foreach`, a `try`-wrapped fold and a fold entered under a `?//` bind still refuse where jq answers.
