- **yq: a properties-only line with no owner, and a `- ` in a flow collection, are refused as yq refuses them** (#6413). A line of node properties (`!!map`, `&a`) in the place of a sibling, level with the keys or items of a block mapping or sequence, was accepted and attached to nothing: `printf 'a: 1\n!!str\n' | succinctly yq .` printed `{"a":1}` where yq stops, and so did `k: |` / `!!map`, `k: !!str` / `&a` and `- a` / `&a`. A `-` followed by a blank inside a flow collection (`[- x]`, `[a, - x]`, `{a: - x}`) was read as the text `- x`; go-yaml refuses it as a block sequence indicator. The properties of a document, of a value and of a key on its own line are read as before. Over five draws of 2,000 generated streams, 474 that differed from yq in whether they are refused now agree and none changed the other way; three YAML Test Suite cases that are invalid YAML (`G9HC`, `GT5M`, `H7J7`) are refused now and leave the known-failures list.
