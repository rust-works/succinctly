- **jq: `strptime` reads `%+`, and on macOS `%+` is the locale's own layout, as jq prints and reads it there** (#7784). `strptime("%+")` never matched (`0 | strftime("%+") | strptime("%+") | mktime` raised `does not match format "%+"`; jq answers `0`), and `%+` was printed in the C layout under every locale, where macOS's libc expands it to the locale's `date_fmt` both ways (`LC_ALL=de_DE.UTF-8`: `"Di. 14 Nov. 2023 23:13:20 CET"`). `%v` takes the locale's month abbreviation on macOS too (`de_DE`: `" 1-Jan.-1970"`, was `" 1-Jan-1970"`). A `+` before the end of the format, a `%` or white space is the conversion, as Apple's `strptime` reads it, and a `+` before a specifier is still a flag (`%+Y`). glibc has neither (a glibc jq prints them as written); off macOS the C layout is printed and read, as recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
