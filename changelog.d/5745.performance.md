- **jq: `reduce`/`foreach` whose UPDATE or EXTRACT reads no loop variable run it as written instead of as a copy rebuilt for every step** (#5745). Over `--stream` events, `reduce inputs as $e (0; .+1)`, `foreach inputs as $e (0; .+1; .)` and `reduce (inputs|select(length==2)) as $e (0; .+1)` are 5% to 9% faster on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X at 10 MB and 100 MB (interleaved, output identical) and 12% to 14% faster at 29 MB on an Apple M5 Max; `[inputs]|length`, which never rebuilds a body, is unchanged. A body that reads a loop variable, binds the same name, or holds a definition or call keeps the per-step copy, as before.
