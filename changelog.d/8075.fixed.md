- **yq: `now`, `env(NAME)` and `strenv(NAME)` inside an operator or an object, and `with_dtf(L; E)`, answer once after an empty stage, as yq does** (#8075). yq answers each of these once over an empty context, the way it answers a literal there. `[.x[] | select(false) | (now + "1h")] | length` over `x: [1, 2, 3]` was `0` where yq v4.53.3 answers `1`, and so were `{"t": now}`, `strenv(X) == "hi"`, `{"a": (strenv(X), 1)}` (which kept only its `1`), `. as $t | now` and `error(strenv(X))`. `[.x[] | select(false) | with_dtf("2006"; "x")]` was `[]` where yq answers `["x"]`. The same holds on the right side of `|=` and in a `map` body: `.x[] |= (select(false) | (strenv(X) + "a"))` now writes `hia` to every member. A `now` that is itself the stage was already answered once, and over a context with nodes nothing changes. `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
