- **yq: under `--eval-all`, `=` and `+=`/`-=`/`*=` read their right side once over the whole document list, as yq does, where each document read its own** (#7137). `yq ea '.k = fi' a1.yaml a2.yaml a3.yaml` writes the last file's index (`2`) into every document, `.k = .k` writes the last document's own `k` into all of them, `.k = [fi]` is `[0, 1, 2]` in each, and `.k += fi` adds the last file's index to each `k`; `succinctly yq` gave each document its own index and its own `k`. The assignment also hands its nodes back with their `--eval-all` flag, so `.k = fi | .m = fi` and `.k = 1 | [.]` read the whole list in the next stage. `|=`, a literal right side and a run without `--eval-all` are unchanged. 131 rows over two sets of three files are pinned against yq v4.53.3; `.k = [.]` (yq's array holds the live nodes) and the separator after a following filter (#7019) still differ (limitations.md).
