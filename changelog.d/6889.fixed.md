- **yq: `style` of a block mapping whose first key is quoted is `""`, not `"single"` or `"double"`** (#6889). The mapping's text begins where its first key does, so the key's quote read as the mapping's own style: `a:` / `  'k': 1` answered `"single"` for `.a | style`, where yq answers `""`, and `select(style == "double")` over a document with quoted keys counted every such mapping. A quoted scalar value, a flow collection and a mapping inside a sequence item are unchanged. Found by a differential sweep of real YAML files (Playwright accessibility snapshots).
