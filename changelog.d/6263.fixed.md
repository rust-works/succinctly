- **jq: `\w`, `\W` and `\p{Word}` under the `i` flag are one operation in a lazy counted repeat's body, as in Oniguruma** (#6263).
  The translation writes them as a bracketed class, and the count of the body took any bracketed class under case folding for a long one, so with the `n` flag `"ca" | [match("(?i)(?:a?|\\w){2,}?"; "gn") | .string]` was `["ca"]` where jq answers `["c","a"]`. The translated word class is now recognised and counted as one operation, also inside an option group (`(?i-s:\w)`, which #6241 counts as its body).
