- **yq: a zero-padded decimal with an 8 or a 9 (`08`, `0018`, `-09`) is a float, as in yq** (#6866). go-yaml tries a leading `0` as octal, which an `8` or `9` breaks, and falls back to the float: `a: 08` is `!!float` and `8.0` in JSON, where it was `!!int` and `8` here; `007`, `0755` and `010` stay integers. The spelling is kept (`tostring`, copies, `==` against a number), as for `007.5`. `tojson` of such a scalar still prints `8` where yq prints `8.0`. Found by a differential sweep of real YAML files (a fixture holding `00000000000000000000000008`).
