- **jq: a loop whose body has a capture group before its empty way, in it, or with its own empty way first ends at the empty iteration and sets its groups, as Oniguruma's does** (#6332). `"abca" | [match("(?:(a)|b*|c)+";"g") | .string]` printed `["abca",""]`; jq 1.7.1 answers `["ab","","a",""]`, and `"ba" | [match("(?:b*|()|a)+";"g") | .captures[0].string]` left the group unset where jq has `""` at the exit. The loop is now written with the group copied (the ways before the empty one are written twice, a counted repeat writes its body again for each count) and ended with the body's empty way, and each copy is read back into its group as the one the match set last (the greatest start, then end, then number). A capture group counts as two operations in the count that decides whether Oniguruma writes a lazy `{n,}?` out as copies. A loop inside another repeat that can run its body more than once is left as written. Over two draws of 5,000 generated loops (`scripts/jq-regex-capture-loop-sweep.py`), 236 and 236 patterns that differed from jq now agree and none changed the other way, and six draws with the loops nested inside repeats fixed 140 to 187 and broke none; about 50 in each plain draw still differ, mostly in a counted `{n,}` of a capture group with a group inside it, or beside a flag switch or an assertion (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`).
