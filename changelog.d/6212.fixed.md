- **jq: an inline option switch inside a capture group ends at the group's `)`** (#6212). `"xA" | match("(?i)(x(?-i))a(?=)")` is `xA` in jq, because Oniguruma ends `(?-i)` at the `)` of the group it is in and the `a` after the group is still case-insensitive; `succinctly jq` found nothing, since the second regex engine (any pattern with a look-around or a back-reference) let the switch run on past the group. The body of a plain or named capture group (`(?<n>`, `(?'n'`) that holds an option run is now wrapped in a non-capturing group, as #6183 did for a look-around and an atomic group; group numbers do not move.
