- **`succinctly jq --indent 0` prints newlines with no indentation, as jq 1.8 does** (#7605). jq 1.7.1 printed `--indent 0` as `-c`; jq 1.8 calls that a bug (jqlang/jq#3232) and prints one element or key per line with the space after each `:` and no indentation, for `-0`, `00` and `+0` too, and for `-c --indent 0` and `--tab --indent 0` (the last of `-c`, `--tab` and `--indent` still wins). Every print route follows it, and `-C` keeps its colours in the new layout.
