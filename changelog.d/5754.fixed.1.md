- **`succinctly jq` `strftime`/`strflocaltime` take the `_`, `-` and `0` padding flags and fail on an empty or oversized result** (#5754). `1420167845 | strftime("%_d|%-d|%0e")` is `" 2|2|02"`, as in jq 1.7.1, where the format used to come back unchanged. A character that follows `%` and names nothing is printed bare (`%Q` is `Q`), and a result that is empty or `strlen(fmt) + 100` bytes or longer is `strftime/1: unknown system failure` (`0 | strftime("")`, `%p` under `de_DE`), where it was returned as it stood.
