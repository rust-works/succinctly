- **jq: a `?//` bind over a `group_by` group retries its next alternative when a later stage raises** (#7337).
  `[group_by(.a)[] | (. as [$x] ?// $x | $x) | .b + 1]` reported the first alternative's error (`string ("y") and
  number (1) cannot be added`) where jq 1.7.1 retries `$x` bound to the whole group and reports `Cannot index array
  with string "b"`. The same held for `group_by(.a) | .[0:2][] | ...`, `| first | ...`, `| .[-1] | ...` and for a
  `map(f)` sequence in their place. A lazy sequence, or the values a `.[a:b][]` stage leaves, met by a `?//` bind is
  now run through the rest of the pipe as one expression, so the retry sees the later stage's error.
  `[limit(1; group_by(.a) | .[0:3][] | (. as [$x] ?// $x | $x) | .a)]` answered `[1]`; it now raises as jq does, the bind
  retrying on the consumer's stop.
  The bind may sit inside parentheses or a sub-pipe (`(( . as [$x] ?// $x | $x )) | ...`), not only open its stage.
