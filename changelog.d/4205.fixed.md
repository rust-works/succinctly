- **yq: `match` returns yq's object** (#4205). `succinctly yq` built jq's: keys `offset, length, string`, code-point offsets and lengths, and `"name": null` on an unnamed capture. yq v4.53.3 is Go's `regexp`: keys `string, offset, length, captures`, **byte** offsets and lengths (`"héllo" | match("l")` is offset 3, `"日本語" | match("本") | .length` is 3), and a capture has `name` only when the group is named; a group that did not take part is `{"string": null, "offset": -1, "length": 0}` even when the overall match is empty. `capture`, `test`, `sub` and `split` were already right. jq mode is unchanged.
