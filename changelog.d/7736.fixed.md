- **jq: a `?//` destructuring alternation in the loop of a `while` or `repeat` under `limit`, `first`, `isempty`, `any`/`all` or `nth` retries its next alternative when their `break` is raised after it, as jq does** (#7736). `[1] | [limit(3; while(. as [$x] ?// $x | $x; .))]` is six `[1]`s, as in jq, where succinctly gave three. A `?//` in the update of `|=` stays on the shipped route, whose remaining difference (`.[] |= select((. as [$x] ?// $x | $x) != null)`) is recorded in the jq limitations page.
