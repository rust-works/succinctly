- **jq: a nested `foreach` whose source navigates now answers the member it left the register on in path position** (#3883, a #3459 follow-up). `path(foreach (foreach .[]? as $x (0; .+1)) as $k (null; .))` on `{"a":null,"b":null}` refused (exit 5) where jq answers `["a"]` then `["b"]`, and `(foreach (foreach .[]? as $x (0; .+1)) as $k (null; .)) = 9` refused where jq writes `{"a":9,"b":9}`. jq's `foreach` does not backtrack its source, so each visited element is where the register sits, and an outer `null`/`true`/`false` accumulator is `jv_identical` to a `null`/boolean member by kind. The nested emission was handed to the outer fold off the root path with no register and read as lost; when nothing after the source can move the register (UPDATE and EXTRACT that cannot navigate), a computed emission now states the register at the step's own path on a `null`/boolean member. An UPDATE that navigates (`.a?`), a member that is not `null`/a boolean, and a nested `reduce` are unchanged. The fold-source sweep's last tracked nested-`foreach` residual is closed.
