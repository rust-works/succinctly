- **jq: a loop with one way that is only an anchor answers as Oniguruma's does** (#6358). `"bab" | [match("(?:(a)\\b|\\bb?){2,}?";"g") | .string]` printed `["b",""]` and jq 1.7.1 answers `["",""]`; `"ba" | [match("(a|\\b)*") | .string]` kept the group's last consuming iteration where jq's empty one sets it. An anchor way is a conditional exit, on both sides of the ways after it, which one star cannot write: the loop is written from what the anchor is (`\b` by its complement, `\A` at the first head, `\z` where nothing can consume), and a repeat Oniguruma counts leaves at the anchor outright. Over three draws of 3,000 loops with anchors the patterns that differ fall from 113, 115 and 137 to 69, 50 and 62 and none changed the other way. Two anchor-only ways in one body still differ.
