- **jq: a `-0` written as a program literal keeps its sign through path mode** (#6042). `-0 | del(empty)`, `[-0|try path(error) catch .]` and `[-0|try pick(error) catch .]` printed `0` where jq 1.7.1 prints `-0`: the path-mode literal arm rebuilt the `-0` literal as an integer zero. A `-0` read from a document, `-0.0` and a computed negative zero already kept their sign.
