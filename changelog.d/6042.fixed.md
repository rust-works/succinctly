- **`succinctly jq` keeps the sign of a literal `-0` that is raised or picked inside a path expression** (#6042). `[-0|try path(error) catch .]`, `[-0|try pick(error) catch .]` and `-0|del(empty)` answered `0`; jq answers `-0`, as `succinctly jq` already did outside path mode. Found by a builtin x value sweep against jq 1.7.1.
