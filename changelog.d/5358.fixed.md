- **yq: `ea '[.a]'` drops a document that lacks the key instead of collecting `null`** (#5358).
  Under `--eval-all` yq evaluates the body of `[...]` read-only, so an absent map key yields nothing: `[.a]` over a document without `a` is `[]`, and `[.a|type]`, `[.x.y]` and `[{"k":.a}]` behave alike. We collected `null`. The plain (non-`--eval-all`) collect, `[.a // null]`, `[.a, null]` and an explicit `a: ~` are unchanged.
