- **jq: regular expressions compile and match with Oniguruma, as in jq 1.7.1** (#7965). `test`, `match`, `capture`, `scan`, `split`/`splits`, `sub` and `gsub` run on the Oniguruma that jq 1.7.1 bundles, through a port of jq's `f_match`, instead of an imitation of it over the `regex` and `fancy-regex` crates. On a corpus of 459,678 generated pattern, flags and subject rows, the imitation's answer, refusal or error text differed from jq 1.7.1's on 49,641; now none does outside the recorded adoption of jq 1.8.2's empty-match captures (#7520). The `l` and `n` flags, look-around, back-references, subroutine calls, the absence operator, possessive and stacked quantifiers and Oniguruma's error messages now all behave as in jq. The earlier engine stays selectable with `SUCCINCTLY_REGEX_ONIG=0` until it is removed. Interleaved over seeded layouts, the same binary with the new engine against the earlier one: -2% to -24% on rows with capture groups, `i`, `gsub`, a global `match`, `scan` or `split`, within 2% on anchored and short-string `test`, +4% to +10% on a plain `test` over long strings, and +34% to +76% on a class with a counted repeat that never matches (`[0-9]{2,}`), on a Ryzen 9 7950X and an Apple M4 Pro at 10 and 100 MB.
