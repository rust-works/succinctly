- **jq: a `^` under `(?m)` does not hold at the end of a text that ends with a newline, as in Oniguruma** (#6456). `"a\n" | [match("(?m)^";"g") | .offset]` printed `[0,2]` and jq 1.7.1 answers `[0]`; `"a\nb\n" | gsub("(?m)^";"> ")` ended with a stray `"> "`; `"a\n" | test("(?m)\n^")` was `true` and is `false`. The `regex` crate's `^` holds after any newline. A match of the crate that ends at the end of such a text is now asked of `fancy-regex`, with each multi-line `^` written as `(?:\A|^(?!\z))`; a match that ends before the end of the text cannot have used it and is kept. Of 333 subject and pattern pairs 159 differed before and none do.
