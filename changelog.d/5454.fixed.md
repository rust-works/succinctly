- **jq: a slice after a dot and an empty parameter or argument list are syntax errors, as in jq** (#5454). jq 1.7.1
  has `Term '.' '[' Exp ']'` but no slice there, and no `()` in a `def` or a call, so `[1,2,3].[1:]`, `def f(): 1; f`
  and `def f: 1; f()` are compile errors (exit 3). `succinctly jq` accepted all three. `.[1:]`, `.a.b[1:]`,
  `[1,2,3].[1]`, `.[]` after a dot and `def f(a): a; f(1)` are unchanged, and so is `succinctly yq`.
