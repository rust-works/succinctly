- **jq: a greedy regex loop ends at an iteration that matches nothing, as Oniguruma's does** (#6106). A loop body whose empty way came before a consuming one matched longer than jq: `"ba" | sub("(?:b*|a)+"; "X")` was `"X"` and is now jq's `"Xa"`, and `"foo-bar" | gsub("(?:\\w*|-)+"; "X")` was `"XX"` and is now `"XX-XX"`. A bounded repeat ends at its empty way the same way. A body with a capture group or a zero-width assertion, and a pattern that goes to `fancy-regex`, keep the longer match (recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`).
