- **yq: a write that selects by `tag` leaves custom-tagged nodes alone** (#6895). `(.. | select(tag == "!!str")) |= upcase` over `a: !Ref x` upcased the scalar, because a write resolved its left side on the owned document, where `!Ref x` is the string `x` (`!!str`); yq's `tag` is the node's own, `!Ref`, and it selects nothing. The left side is now resolved on the document's cursor first when it reads `tag`, as #5479 does for `style`, `anchor`, `kind` and the comments, so CloudFormation's `!Ref`, `!Sub` and `!GetAtt` scalars, gcloud's `!REF` and any other custom tag are skipped by `select(tag == "!!str")`, `"!!int"` and `"!!seq"` as in yq, and `select(tag == "!Ref")` finds them. Found by a differential sweep of real YAML files (182 of 399 gcloud files).
