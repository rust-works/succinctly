- **jq: `first`/`limit` over a `try`/`?` wrapper or a parenthesised pipe stop at the bound on the `key`/`path` walk** (#4014, a #3514 residual). `.a | first(try (.[] | if ("A"|stderr) then . else . end)) | key` on `{"a":[1,2,3]}` wrote `AAA` where jq's `path(...) | last` writes `A`, and `limit(1; .[] | (.[]? | C)) | key` ran its inner stage at an element past the bound. The walk collected a `try` body and a nested pipe whole before delivering their first position, so every `stderr`, `debug` or `input` in a later stage ran at positions the bound never reaches. Both now deliver position by position, so the stop reaches them; a `try` still catches only its own body's errors, and the computed-bracket rollback is unchanged. Over a 2,520-row base-vs-branch-vs-jq-1.7.1 sweep, 38 rows changed to jq's answer, none regressed, and 432 yq-mode rows are byte-identical.
