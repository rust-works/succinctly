- **yq: `-P` holds the comment of a flow element that is a collection** (#6334, part of it). `printf 'v: [[1], # a⏎  2]⏎' | succinctly yq -P .` printed `- 2` with no comment, losing `# a` from the file; yq writes a flow collection it turns into a block without the line its comment sat on, holds that comment (as it already did one after the whole collection, #4377) and writes it at the end of the next line without a comment of its own (`- 2 # a`; `- # a` above the members of a collection; `b: 2 # a` in a mapping). Between the elements of a flow sequence an element with a comment of its own (an alias too, which takes the held one when it has none) overwrites the held comment instead of letting it wait, and the held comment is one slot that the next held comment overwrites. Over 500 generated documents with same-line comments, the ones that differed from yq under `-P` fell from 45 to 15, none changed the other way; the 15 left are an empty `[]`/`{}` after a held comment, a second comment on a comma's line and the foot of a footed collection that a key follows (all in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`).
