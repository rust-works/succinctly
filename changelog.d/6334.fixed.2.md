- **yq: `-P` holds the comment of a flow element that is a collection** (#6334, part of it). `printf 'v: [[1], # a⏎  2]⏎' | succinctly yq -P .` printed `- 2` with no comment, losing `# a` from the file; yq writes a flow collection it turns into a block without the line its comment sat on, holds that comment (as it already did one after the whole collection, #4377) and writes it at the end of the next line without a comment of its own (`- 2 # a`; `- # a` above the members of a collection; `b: 2 # a` in a mapping). Between the elements of a collection that was written in flow an element with a comment of its own overwrites the held one instead of letting it wait. Over 500 generated documents with same-line comments, the ones that differed from yq under `-P` fell from 45 to 15, none changed the other way; the 15 left are an empty `[]`/`{}` after a held comment (already listed in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`) and a second comment on a comma's line.
