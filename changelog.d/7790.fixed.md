- **jq: `strftime("%OB")` is the locale's alternative month name on macOS, as jq prints it** (#7790). Under `cs_CZ`, `pl_PL`, `ru_RU`, `uk_UA`, `el_GR`, `lt_LT`, `be_BY`, `kk_KZ` and `mn_MN`, macOS's libc gives `%OB` the nominative name a month standing alone takes, where `%B` is the genitive of a date (`0 | strftime("%OB")` is `"leden"` and `strftime("%B")` is `"ledna"` under `cs_CZ`); `succinctly jq` printed `%B` for both. `strflocaltime` follows, and `strptime("%OB")` reads those names (`leden`, not `ledna`). Every other `%E`/`%O` form was already the plain one, as in macOS's libc under every installed locale.
