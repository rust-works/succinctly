- **jq: a loop whose way starts with a captured anchor before an item that can match nothing answers as Oniguruma's does** (#6506). `"cba" | [match("(?:(a)|(\\z)b*)+";"g") | .captures | map(.string)]` printed `[["a",null],["",""]]` for the first match where jq 1.7.1 prints `[["a",""],["",""]]`, and `"abc" | [match("(?:(a)|(\\b)b*){2}";"g") | .string]` printed `["a",""]` where jq prints `["",""]`: the empty way the anchor opens is an exit, and the loop kept its text because a group around the anchor counted as a group already gone past. The way that consumes is now written from the text of the items before it, group included (as #6492 does for the other order), and the empty way is set by the exit.
