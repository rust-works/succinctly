- **jq: a stream with a parse fault inside a container that never closes is reported at the fault** (#7812).
  `[tru,`, `{x`, `[1 2` or random bytes after a `{` on a pipe, a FIFO, a device or a terminal were read on looking for the close, so `-c .`, `-n input` and `-n '[inputs]'` never answered and grew until killed (about 1 run in 128 of `jq -c . /dev/urandom`), where jq 1.7.1 reports the fault at once.
  A value a stream is read on for past the look-ahead is now read by jq's parser model as it goes, and its first fault ends the read.
