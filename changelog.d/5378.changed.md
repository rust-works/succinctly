- **jq: `recurse(f)` is no longer refused at 10,000 nodes** (#5378). `0 | [recurse(if . < 300000 then . + 1 else empty end)] | length` stopped with `recurse: maximum nodes exceeded`, where jq answers `300001`. The cap that applies to an `f` that does not descend the tree is now 2^20 (1,048,576) nodes. A node costs `1 + size / 64` of it, where `size` is its string length, array or object length, or (in `path(recurse(f))`, `del`, `|=` and `pick`) the length of its path, so a walk whose nodes grow is bounded by what it holds and not only by how many it has: `[] | [recurse(. + [1])]` peaks at 3.3 GB (2.5 GB before) and `null | [path(recurse(.a))]` at 2.3 GB (1.7 GB), each raising the cap's error in under 6 s on an Apple M4 Pro debug build, where counting nodes alone ran past 4 GB in two seconds. See `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
