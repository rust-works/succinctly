- **jq: `recurse(f)` is no longer refused at 10,000 nodes** (#5378). `0 | [recurse(if . < 300000 then . + 1 else empty end)] | length` stopped with `recurse: maximum nodes exceeded`, where jq answers `300001`. The cap that applies to an `f` that does not descend the tree is now 2^20 (1,048,576) nodes. A node of a `path(recurse(f))`, `del`, `|=` or `pick` walk costs `1 + depth / 64` of it, so a long path stays bounded as well as a long walk (`null | [path(recurse(.a))]` raises after 11,554 paths, in 3.7 s and under 4 GB, where counting nodes alone ran past 4 GB in two seconds). The cap still counts nodes, not the memory they hold; see `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
