- **jq: a fold step that appends or stores a container built by a builtin or a pipe is linear** (#7451).
  `reduce range(n) as $t ([]; . + [$t|tostring|split("")])`, `.[$t] = [range(3)]`, `. + [[$t]|sort]`,
  `.a += [[$t]|sort]` and their `foreach`, `+=`, `|=`, `setpath` and constructor (`[.[0] + [[$t]|sort], .[1] + 1]`)
  forms copied the whole accumulator on every step, because the in-place fold step had no stage for the builtin
  that built the operand and handed the step to the evaluator, whose `+` or write on a state the fold still held
  copied it: the time grew with the square of the step count where jq 1.7.1 is linear. An operand that reads no
  input and has no effect is now evaluated once on `null` and the step writes in place; one that reads `.`, has
  an effect, raises or yields other than once keeps the evaluator's route, and the answers are unchanged.
