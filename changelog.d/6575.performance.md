- **jq: a fold step that is a pipe of stages is 1.9-4.8x faster** (#6575).
  A `reduce`, `foreach`, `until` or `while` step such as `. + [$x] | .[-5:]` (a sliding window),
  `. + $x | . % 1000` or `[., $x] | max` now applies its stages to the state one after another.
  Before, the whole pipe ran on a re-indexed copy of the state every step unless every stage was an
  assignment. On an Apple M5 over 4,000,000 `reduce` steps the sliding window takes 832 ms
  instead of 3,189 ms (jq 1.7.1: 1,869 ms), and `[., $x] | max` takes 831 ms instead of 1,594 ms
  (jq: 907 ms).
