- **`succinctly jq` `strptime` reads a `%` specifier after a width, and refuses it straight after a `+` flag** (#5754). `"%" | strptime("%E4%")`, `"%" | strptime("%O5%")` and `"%" | strptime("%E+0%")` now read the `%`, and `"%" | strptime("%+%")` is no match, as in jq 1.7.1: the libc refuses a `%` specifier whose prefix ends in a modifier or a `+` flag (`%+%`, `%E%`, `%E+%`, `%4E%`) and reads it when digits end the prefix (`%5%`, `%+0%`). Found by a 2,925-format grid of flag, modifier and width prefixes against `/usr/bin/jq`, which also confirmed that a layout holding a `_` or `-` flag matches jq (both are no match), so that item of #5754 needed no change.
