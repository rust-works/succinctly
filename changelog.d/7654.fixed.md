- **`succinctly jq` reads a `?` or `+` after a blank, a `#` comment or a `(?#..)` comment as a new quantifier, as jq does** (#7654). Under the `x` flag `"" | [match("a{1,2} ?$"; "gx")] | length` is `1` and `"aaa" | [match("(a)? +"; "xn")] | map(.length)` is `[3]`, as in jq 1.7.1 and 1.8.2, where they were `0` and `[1]`: Oniguruma ends a quantifier at the gap, so `a{1,2} ?` is `(?:a{1,2})?` and `(a)? +` is `(?:(a)?)+`, which the pattern the regex engine reads now says, where it had read the lazy `a{1,2}?` and the possessive `(a)?+`. The same holds without `x` across a `(?#..)` comment, inline `(?x)` and `(?x:...)` included; and `{,n}`, which jq reads as literal text, is no longer taken for a quantifier there (`"a{,2}}b" | test("a{,2}+b")` is `true`). Over 17,956 generated patterns 643 rows that differed from jq 1.7.1 now match it; two that matched by accident now show two older, separate faults.
