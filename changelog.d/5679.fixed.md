- **jq: a long pipe of computed stages (`. + 1 | . + 1 | …`, `map(.) | map(.) | …`) evaluates in linear memory** (#5679). A pipe of more than 128 stages handed each call a copy of every stage after it and held that copy while the rest ran, so `n` stages held `n` copies of up to `n` stages. A tail is now the rest of its block of 16 stages plus one link to a kept chain of the blocks after it, so a call copies at most two blocks. Peak RSS of `-n '0 | . + 1 | …'` (Apple M5 Max, release build, output identical): 750 stages 99 MB to 11 MB, 1,500 358 MB to 17 MB, 3,000 1.37 GB to 28 MB, and 24,000 stages 183 MB (the old build's growth extrapolates to about 90 GB there; it was not run); `[1] | map(.) | …` 3,000 stages 874 MB to 29 MB. Interleaved A/B, min of 15: 3,000 stages 416 ms to 14 ms (`. + 1`) and 228 ms to 14 ms (`map(.)`); pipes of up to 128 stages do not reach the new code. jq mode only; a pipe holding a `def` stage, or a marker and a node that can read it, is copied as before. x86_64 and the M4 Pro were not measured.
