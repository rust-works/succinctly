- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE `if` with a `null` `then` beside a fold in the `else` answers when a handler `try` wraps it and the step after it, the `foreach`'s last, takes the fold on the stored `null`** (#5113). A `null` literal `then` stores `null`, so the next step's `.a` is falsy and that step runs the fold on the stored `null`, where a stored `1` or boolean makes `.a` raise and the handler's literal answer. The lookahead gave up there and kept the whole-`if` refusal, which a `?` turned into a truncated answer. A caught-destructure fold (`foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)`) hands its input on, so it stores `null` again whether or not its source emits, and the lookahead now reads that last step as the one an INIT of `null` resolves. `[path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.a) as $i (.; try (if .a then null else FE end) catch 1; $i))?]` on `{"a":{"a":null}}` is jq's `[["a"]]` where it answered `[]`, as are `path`, `|=`/`del` (`{}`) and a bare, `?` or `elif` spelling; so is `{"b":true}` with a `.a` EXTRACT over `(.a,.)` (`[["a","a"],["a","a"]]`). A step with others after it (a source of three steps or more, or an unbounded one), a fold that changes the value, a state that is not `null`, and a `null` state beside a leading comma sibling keep the whole-`if` resolve.
