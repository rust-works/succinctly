- **jq: `walk(f)` and a plain assignment write the materialized document in place** (#7260).
  `walk` no longer writes its input out as JSON and indexes it again, and it rewrites an unshared tree in
  its own storage instead of building a second one. An assignment like `.users[0].name = "x"` writes into
  the document instead of first copying every container on its path. `walk(.)|.users|length` on 10 MB of
  `users` now peaks at 83 MB instead of 156 MB on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X, against jq 1.7.1's 90 MB, and runs
  in 84 ms instead of 165 ms (jq: 782 ms). On an Apple M4 Pro it peaks at 75 MB instead of 129 MB (jq:
  96 MB), and runs in 60 ms instead of 95 ms. The assignment row is 2-5% faster and holds 2-21 MB less at
  10-100 MB.
