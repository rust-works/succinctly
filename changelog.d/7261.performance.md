- **jq: bare `min` and `max` take their single walk again** (#7261). #5683's walk, which keeps only the running winner, had been unreachable since the commit that tidied it: its dispatch sat in the `sort`/`unique` arm, so `min`/`max` still collected every element's cursor and decoded value before comparing. The walk also now decodes a plain integer when the element it is compared with is not one, so two document numbers compare as jq 1.7.1 compares their literals (`[123456789012345679,123456789012345678.9] | min` is the fraction). Over `.numbers|max` on the `numbers` pattern, interleaved, output identical to jq's: AMD Ryzen 9 7950X 14.5 -> 10.2 ms at 1 MB, 123 -> 81 ms at 10 MB, 1191 -> 789 ms at 100 MB (jq 1.7.1: 13.9, 125, 1305 ms), peak RSS at 100 MB 999 -> 126 MB (jq 548 MB); Apple M4 Pro 12.6 -> 11.8, 92 -> 84, 891 -> 795 ms (jq 13.4, 103, 992 ms), 909 -> 125 MB (jq 806 MB). `min` moves the same way; `add`, `[.[]|floor]|add`, `sort` and `max_by(.)` are unchanged.
