- **yq: no `---` between results yq builds with no document** (#7019).
  yq separates two results only when their document index differs, and a node it builds from a document's root or from nothing (a literal, `[...]`, `length`, `keys`, `tag`, `to_entries`, `@json`, `@props`, interpolation, a comparison) has index 0, as the first document's do: `length` over three documents prints `2`, `2`, `2`, and two files print no separator between them either.
  A filter whose results are all of that kind is now recognised from its stages and prints them back to back, under `-0` and `--split-exp` too; a navigation (`.a`, `.a | length`, `{"k": .a}`) keeps the separator, and so does `-i`, where yq's output would rewrite several documents as one.
  Over a 5,850-row three-way differential (yq v4.53.3, `main`, this change) 2,070 rows now match yq and none that matched stopped matching; `map` over a document's node, `//`, and a comma that mixes the two kinds still differ (recorded with #2427).
