- **yq: a UTF-8 byte-order mark in front of a YAML file leaves yq no header, so a leading `---` is read, not echoed** (#5357). yq's `--header-preprocess` looks for its leading blank, comment, `%YAML` and `---` lines at the first byte, and a mark is not one of them; `printf '\xef\xbb\xbf---\na: 1\n---\nb: 2\n' | yq .` prints `a: 1` / `---` / `b: 2`, where this printed the first `---` too (#5356 had dropped the mark but kept the header model). A BOM-prefixed source now has no header on the streaming routes (stdin and files, `.`, `-N`, `-I=0`, `-o=json`, a path): `---` / `---` / `x` is a null document and then `x`, blank lines and a comment's indent in front of the first content are normalised away, and a text with no content is one empty document (a blank line), as captured from yq v4.53.3. The routes that gather a source first (`-P`, writes, `-i`) follow in #6762; `head_comment`, a first document on its marker line and a first-line `%YAML 1.2` after a mark are not read as yq reads them yet and are listed in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
