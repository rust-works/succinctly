- **jq: a group repeated `{0}` keeps its capture entry** (#6108). `"ab" | match("(a){0}b")` had no capture where jq reports one (offset `-1`, string `null`; an empty string at the match offset when the whole match is empty), so `.captures` was one entry short whenever no later group followed the `{0}` group, `capture` dropped the named key and `scan` returned the match instead of the capture array. `{0}`, `{0,0}`, `{00}`, a lazy or stacked repeat and groups nested inside one are all counted, as in jq.
