- **jq: `first`/`last` right after `[.. | parent?]` no longer re-index the whole array** (#4068). The owned navigation peel took `.[0]`/`.[-1]` but not the builtins, so `[.. | parent?] | first | length` (an array of `n` shared copies of the document) wrote the array out in full and indexed it to read one element: 2,579 M, 9,787 M and 38,184 M instructions and 62 MB, 192 MB and 693 MB peak over 1,200, 2,400 and 4,800 inner arrays on an Apple M5 Max. In jq mode a leading `first`/`last` over an array or `null` is now taken natively (jq defines them as `.[0]`/`.[-1]`): 242 M, 453 M and 870 M instructions and 21 MB, 32 MB and 55 MB, the cost of the array itself. yq (its own `first`) and any other target (an object or scalar raises in the bridge's words) still take the bridge. On a 7950X (wall-clock, min of 5, interleaved) 136 ms, 498 ms and 1,960 ms become 19 ms, 35 ms and 69 ms. Output is identical to the previous build across 13,520 first/last/tail/value/wrapper combinations in both modes.
