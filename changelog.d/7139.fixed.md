- **yq: a verbatim tag is printed as yq spells it** (#7139). `- !<tag:yaml.org,2002:str> x` printed the tag as written; yq prints its `!!` form (`- !!str x`, and `-P` drops it as it does `!!str`), and a local verbatim tag `!<!foo>` is `!foo`. The tag is read in that spelling, so every route prints it alike (streaming, a write, `to_entries`, `[.a]`, a block scalar header); `!<!>`, `!<foo>` and a tag of another namespace stay as written. 143 rows of `tests/yq_cli/i7139.rs` are yq's.
