- **jq: a composed `isempty(recurse)` / `any(recurse; .)` as a `foreach` UPDATE keeps the register** (#4706, #4698).
  `[path(foreach .[]? as $k (0; (isempty(recurse) | not); $k))?]` on `[1]` is `[[0]]` in jq and was `[]` here (the refusal was swallowed by the `?` and the stream element dropped; without `?` it raised `Invalid path expression`), as were `(isempty(recurse) and true)`, `(any(recurse; .) | not)`, `isempty(def f: recurse; f)` and `(isempty(..) | not)`, with the `del(...)`/`=` twins. A verdict that stops on a recursion's seed now leaves the register where it entered, as the bare spelling already did. `any(recurse(f); .)` and verdicts decided on a descendant are unchanged.
