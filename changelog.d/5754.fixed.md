- **`succinctly jq` `strptime` reads `%E`/`%O` modifiers, `%k` and `%l`, and refuses what the libc does not know** (#5754). `"03/05/15" | strptime("%Ex")`, `"2015" | strptime("%EY")` and `"9:30 PM" | strptime("%l:%M %p")` parse as in jq 1.7.1, where each was an error. A specifier jq's libc does not know (`%Q`, `%P`) and a `%` that ends the format are now a mismatch (`"x" | strptime("%Q")` is an error, as in jq) instead of matching the literal text.
