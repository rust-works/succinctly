- **yq: a line break after an escaped line break in a double-quoted scalar is kept, as in go-yaml** (#6067). `a: "z\` followed by an empty line and ` x"` read `z x` here and `z\nx` in yq, and two empty lines read `z\nx` where yq has `z\n\nx`: after a backslash-newline go-yaml is at the start of a line, so it keeps every break that follows instead of folding the first into a space. The same holds in the YAML and JSON writers, for CRLF and after an escaped U+0085, U+2028 or U+2029. A scalar with two escapes in a row, or an escaped backslash before a break, is unchanged. Of 28 bodies in each of two output formats, 38 differed from yq v4.53.3 before and none do now.
