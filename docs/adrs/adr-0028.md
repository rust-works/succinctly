# ADR-0028: Carry Null and Bool Spellings in OwnedValue (#2802)

## Status

🟡 Proposed (2026-11-10). Decides the design question #2802's plan amendment of 2026-10-28 calls "the
`Null`/`Bool` audit", which the integer slice (#5475) left open. [ADR-0018](adr-0018.md)'s fidelity
rule is the reason for the work: `succinctly yq` follows yq. Builds on the bridge spelling token
(#5466) and the same carrier #5475 used for integers (`OwnedValue::NumberLiteral`).

## Context

yq keeps a scalar as the text it was written as and decides a number of things from that text:
what `tostring` prints, what `==` compares, whether `select(.)` passes it. `OwnedValue::Null` and
`OwnedValue::Bool(bool)` carry no text, so a document's `~`, `Null`, `True` or `FALSE` is the
canonical `null`, `true` or `false` the moment it is materialized. The same gap for integers
(`0x1f`, `01`, `+5`) closed in #5475 by reusing `NumberLiteral(NumberRepr, SharableLiteral)`,
which every evaluator arm already handled.

### What differs

Captured against yq v4.53.3 (Homebrew build) and `succinctly yq` at `main` after #6822, with
`a: <spelling>` / `b: 1`, `-o=json -I0`, 24 filters per spelling (`tostring`, `tag`, `type`, `==`
against null, true, false and the spelling's own text, `not`, `//`, `unique`, `sort`, `@json`,
`@text`, interpolation, `length`, `select(.)`, `any`, `and`, and a `to_entries | from_entries`
round trip). `tests/yq_cli/i2802.rs` pins the integer half of the same matrix.

| spelling | rows that differ | what differs |
|----------|-----------------:|--------------|
| `~` | 6 of 24 | `tostring`, `[.a] \| .[0] \| tostring`, a `to_entries \| from_entries` round trip, `unique` and interpolation print `~`, here `null`; `select(.)` passes it in yq |
| `Null`, `NULL`, an empty value | 6 of 20 (the first 20 filters) | the same text readers |
| `True`, `TRUE` | 8 and 7 of 24 | the text readers, `== true` (`false` in yq: it compares the text), `== "True"` (`true` in yq) |
| `False`, `FALSE` | 7 of 24 | the text readers, `== false` (`false` in yq), `select(.)` passes it in yq |
| `null`, `true`, `false` | 0 | canonical already |
| `yes`, `on`, `y`, `no` | 0 | strings in both tools, so there is nothing to carry |

Three kinds of row, then:

1. **Text readers.** `tostring`, string interpolation, `unique` (which returns a node) and the
   `to_entries | from_entries` round trip print the spelling. `sort`, `@json`, `@text`, `type` and
   `tag` agree on every spelling probed; a YAML re-emit and `join` were not probed.
2. **Equality.** yq compares the node's text, so `True == true` is `false` and `True == "True"` is
   `true`. #6292 is the explicit-tag form (`!!bool yes == "yes"`).
3. **Truthiness.** yq's `select(.)` passes `~` and `False`: the answer depends on the text, not on
   the resolved value. Seven functions decide truthiness here (`OwnedValue::is_truthy`,
   `eval.rs`'s `is_truthy`, `lazy.rs`, `path_mode.rs`'s `truthy`, `path_mode/reference.rs`,
   `document.rs`'s and `stream.rs`'s `is_falsy`), with 84 call sites of `is_truthy()` among them.

A typed mapping key is the same gap (`null: z` / `~: u`): `to_entries | from_entries` now agrees
(`{"null":"z","~":"u"}`), and `[.[] | key | tostring]` is `["null","~"]` in yq and `["null","null"]`
here.

Issues waiting on this decision: #2802, #6292 (compare by text), #6674 (a spelling lost by
`with_entries(select)`, `$x`, the merge operators and `from_entries`), #6101 (`to_string` of a null
prints the node's text), and the "Spellings `OwnedValue` cannot keep" entries in
`docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md` (two, near lines 5680 and 5759).

### What the change touches

Measured on `main` (the site counts at 2532bf56f, the spike at 7e69f8a6e):

- `OwnedValue::Null` appears at 968 sites and `OwnedValue::Bool` at 588 (`eval.rs` 630 and 399,
  `eval_generic.rs` 136 and 60, `value.rs` 53 and 40, `yq_runner.rs` 42 and 24, `path_mode.rs` 25).
  760 functions hold at least one such pattern, 493 of them in `eval.rs`.
- A spike adding one variant beside `NumberLiteral`
  (`SpelledScalar(u8, SharableLiteral)`, nothing else changed) fails `cargo check` at **25**
  exhaustive matches: 15 in `eval.rs`, 4 in `value.rs`, 2 each in `stream.rs` and `eval_generic.rs`,
  1 each in `parser.rs` and `lazy.rs`. Every other `match` on these variants has a `_` arm and every `if let` and `matches!` is a test
  for one value; the compiler says nothing about any of them, so a spelled value would read as
  "something else" there, silently.
- The integer slice reused `NumberLiteral` and so needed no new arms: #5466 was +292/-6 in 4
  files, #5475 +445/-32 in 16. `into_plain_number`, which demotes a literal that passes through an
  operation, has 26 sites.
- `OwnedValue` is 32 bytes, pinned by
  `owned_value_is_32_bytes_because_its_containers_are_one_pointer_3000`; `NumberLiteral` is its
  widest arm.

## Decision

**We will carry a null's and a boolean's source spelling as a new `OwnedValue` variant that is
built only from a yq document when the spelling is not the canonical one (alternative A), and we
will land it in the five steps of the plan below, each gated on a differential matrix and a
two-architecture A/B.** This is a proposal: it binds when the status becomes Accepted, and the
three questions at the end are what the owner has to answer first. We will not retype the
payloads of `Null` and `Bool` (B), and we will not use the presentation tree for values (C).

## Alternatives Considered

**A. A spelled scalar variant, produced only from a yq document.**
`OwnedValue::SpelledScalar(ScalarKind, SharableLiteral)` with `ScalarKind` one of `Null`, `True`,
`False`; same shape as `NumberLiteral`, so the width should stay 32 (the pin asserts it).
It is built only where the spelling is not the canonical one (`~`, `Null`, `NULL`, empty, `True`,
`TRUE`, `False`, `FALSE`, and a tag-forced `!!bool`/`!!null` text), only under yq semantics, and
only from a document, so jq mode and every canonical document build exactly what they build today.
Readers that want the text (the three kinds above) read it; every other reader goes through one
`canonical()` that returns `Null` or `Bool`.
*Cost:* the 25 flagged matches, plus the audit of the unflagged sites. The risk is the unflagged
ones: a reader that matches `Bool(false)` beside `_` and meets a spelled `False` takes the `_` arm
and calls it truthy. Enforcement has to be tests, not the compiler: a sweep over every builtin and
operator with each of the eight spellings as input, compared with yq's answer, in the shape of the
integer slice's matrix (`tests/yq_cli/i2802.rs`), run per slice and not only at the end.

**B. Retype the payloads so the compiler flags every site.**
`Null` becomes `Null(Spelling)` and `Bool(bool)` becomes `Bool(Truth)`. Every one of the 1,556
sites stops compiling until it is fixed, so nothing is missed. It also rewrites every pattern in
`eval.rs` (630 and 399 sites) for eight spellings that appear in no benchmark corpus, and `eval.rs`
is the file concurrent PRs land in, so the rebase cost is high. Rejected on cost unless the audit
in A turns out to be unmanageable.

**C. Keep the spelling in the presentation tree (`NodeMeta`, ADR-0017).**
It is keyed by a node's position in the document, so it covers `.a |= to_string` and `.a = (.b |
...)` and nothing that builds a value with no position: a collect, a construction, `map`, a `$x`
binding. #6101 and #5429 both hit this limit. It cannot give `==` or `tostring` the text once the
value has moved. Rejected as the mechanism; it stays the home of presentation (style, comments).

**D. Read the text from the cursor where there is one, and record the rest.**
`tostring` and `==` over a value that is still a document cursor can read its text. The owned
routes (`--slurp`, `--eval-all`, `--inplace`, a `$x`, `[...]`, the reindex bridge) cannot. This is
the status quo plus a few arms, and it leaves the matrix's `to_entries | from_entries`,
`unique` and interpolation rows. ADR-0018 permits a divergence only where matching would corrupt
data, discard a write, or take the process down; none of those applies, so a documented gap here
would need that rule amended.

**E. Do nothing.** Same objection as D, without the arms.

## Proposed plan (for A)

Each step is its own PR, gated by the differential matrix, the full suite, both clippy lists, and
an interleaved A/B on the 7950X and the M4 Pro (min of 11, output identity, control first) that
includes `comprehensive` and a corpus with no spelled scalar, so the cost of the new arm on
documents that never use it is a number and not an assumption.

- **P0. One truthiness function.** The seven definitions above become one that the rest call, and
  a test that the call sites agree (the #106 lesson). No behaviour change; it removes seven copies of one
  predicate before any spelled value exists, and it is worth doing without the rest.
- **P1. The variant and `canonical()`**, with the width pin, round-trip tests over every
  spelling, a document-provenance negative test (the same bytes in a user document stay what they
  were), and the 25 flagged matches. Nothing produces it yet. Reuse #5466's bridge token for the
  reindex bridge: `to_entries | from_entries` and every `bridge_value` reader cross it.
- **P2. Null spellings** (`~`, `Null`, `NULL`, empty, `!!null x`): the producer in the cursor
  conversions, the text readers, equality, truthiness, YAML re-emit and typed keys.
- **P3. Bool spellings** (`True`, `TRUE`, `False`, `FALSE`, an explicit `!!bool`): the same, and
  #6292's rows.
- **P4. The follow-ups** that were waiting: #6674's rows, #6101's null row, and the
  "Spellings `OwnedValue` cannot keep" entries in the limitations page.

## Consequences

**Positive**

- yq's `tostring`, `==`, `select(.)` and re-emit rows for the eight spellings agree, and the
  issues in the context section close or shrink to their own residuals.
- jq mode and every document without a non-canonical spelling build what they build today; the
  gate for P1 is that this holds by measurement.
- P0 stands alone.

**Negative**

- A new `OwnedValue` arm that 1,500 sites do not mention. A missed reader gives a wrong answer for
  a document that spells a scalar unusually and no failure anywhere else; the sweep is the only
  guard, so it has to cover every builtin, not a sample (the lesson of #1120).
- The width pin and `JqValue`'s width are asserted, not assumed, but not yet measured for this
  variant: P1 measures them.
- Another arm to maintain in every new builtin.

## Questions for the owner

1. **A, or D/E?** Eight spellings, Low severity in every issue, against 1,556 sites and an
   unguarded wildcard risk. ADR-0018 as written says A; the rule's decision order only lets cost
   break a tie.
2. **Order.** Null before Bool (P2, P3), or both at once.
3. **Tagged forms.** `!!bool yes`, `!!null x` in P2 and P3, or after.
